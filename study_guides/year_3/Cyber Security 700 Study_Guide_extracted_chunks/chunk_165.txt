Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report 

Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR). This authoritative and highly respected report is 
based on data on security incidents systematically collected from a wide variety of organizations. The 
results in the 2018 report are based on data from more than 53,000 security incidents and over 2,200 
data compromises from 65 countries and 67 organizations. The results are broken down by 20 industry 
sectors, such as accommodation, entertainment, finance, healthcare, manufacturing, public, and 
utilities. Further, threats are broken down along three dimensions: 

 Pattern: This includes DoS, privilege misuse, lost and stolen assets, point of sale, miscellaneous 
errors, web app attacks, crimeware, payment care skimmers, and cyber-espionage. 
 Action: This includes hacking, malware, social breach, error, misuse, physical breach, and 
environmental-based damage. 
 Asset: This includes servers, media, user devices, persons, networks, kiosks/terminals, and 
embedded devices (for example, Internet of Things [IoT] devices).
