The European Union Agency for Network and Information Security (ENISA) has identified 
three main processes in the development of an information security awareness program 
[ENIS08], as shown in Figure 5.3. 
 
change management Business processes that seek to ensure that

The three main processes are as follows: 

 Plan, assess, and design: Awareness programs must be designed with the 
organization mission in mind. They should support the business needs of 
the organization and be relevant to the organization’s culture and IT 
architecture. The most successful programs are those that users feel are relevant to the 
subject matter and issues presented. In the design step of the program, the awareness needs 
are identified, an effective awareness plan is developed, organizational buy-in is sought and 
secured, and priorities are established.
