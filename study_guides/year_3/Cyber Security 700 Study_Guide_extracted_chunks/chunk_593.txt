6.9 Emergency Fixes 
 
Security incident emergencies must be handled with a greater sense of urgency than other security 
incidents. An emergency response may entail making an emergency fix to temporarily eliminate 
ongoing damage until a more permanent response is provided. Implementing an emergency fix can 
also require that an information security officer be temporarily given access privileges not normally 
authorized. 
 
To get a feel for what constitutes an emergency, consider a classification scheme for security incidents 
suggested in ISO 27035: 
 
 Emergency: Severe impact. These are incidents that: 
o Act on especially important information systems and 
o Result in especially serious business loss or 
o Lead to especially important social impact 
 Critical: Medium impact. These are incidents that:  
o Act on especially important information systems or important information systems 
and 
o Result in serious business loss or 
o Lead to important social impact 
 Warning: Low impact. These are incidents that: 
o Act on especially important information systems or ordinary information systems and 
o Result in considerable business loss or 
o Lead to considerable social impact 
 Information: No impact. These are incidents that: 
o Act on ordinary information systems and 
o Result in minor business loss or no business loss or 
o Lead to minor social impact or no social impact
