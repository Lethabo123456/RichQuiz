Further, as the IT infrastructure develops and matures, meeting enterprise 
strategic goals is likely to involve new arrangements with outside providers, such 
as cloud service providers, more use of mobile devices by employees and outside actors, and perhaps 
reliance on a variety of new hardware and software to develop Internet of Things (IoT) capability. These 
activities may create unintended barriers to flexibility and introduce new areas of risk. IT 
management must be guided by strategic planning to meet these challenges. 

One of the best-documented examples of IT 
strategic planning is the process used at Intel [HAYD08a, HAYD08b, PETE12].
