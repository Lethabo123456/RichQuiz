Factor Analysis of Information Risk 

An important contribution to risk assessment is Factor Analysis of Information Risk (FAIR), first 
introduced in 2005. FAIR, which has been standardized by The Open Group, has received wide 
acceptance. Its relationship to International Organization for Standardization (ISO) risk standards are 
summarized as follows: 

 ISO 27001 describes a general process for creating an information security management 
system (ISMS). 
 In that context, ISO 27005 defines the approach to managing risk. 
 FAIR provides a methodology for analyzing risk. 

The Open Group 

A global consortium with more than 500 member organizations that enables the achievement of 
business objectives through IT standards. 
Thus, FAIR provides more specific guidance that can be used within the framework defined by ISO 
27005.
