9. Exfiltrate data from victim systems. The APT uses a variety of tools and protocols to

10. Maintain persistent presence. An APT seeks to attain what its controllers have tasked it to do: 
maintain access to the target environment. It is not uncommon for the APT to sit undetected 
in an enterprise network for lengthy periods of time before being activated.
