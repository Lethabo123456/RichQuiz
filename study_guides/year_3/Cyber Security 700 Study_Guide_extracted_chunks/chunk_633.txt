 Protection of audit information: Deals with providing technical or automated protection of 
audit information. Enhancements include: 

o For initial generation and backup of audit trails, use hardware-enforced, write-once 
media. 
o Store audit information in a repository separate from the audited system or system 
component. 
o Implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect the integrity of audit information 
and audit tools. 
o Authorize access to management of audit functionality to only selected privileged 
users. 
o For executing selected actions, require the approval of two authorized individuals. 
o Authorize read-only access to audit information to a selected subset of privileged 
users. 
o Store audit information on a component running a different operating system than 
the system or component being audited. 

 Non-repudiation: Deals with protecting against an individual falsely denying having performed 
selected audit-related activities. Enhancements include: 
o Provide organizational personnel with the means to identify who produced specific 
information in the event of an information transfer. 
o Prevent the modification of information between production and review. 
o Maintain reviewer or releaser identity and credentials within the established chain of 
custody for all information reviewed or released. 
o Prevent the modification of information between review and transfer/release.
