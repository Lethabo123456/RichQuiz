X.1054 provides an example of information security status report structure that includes the following 
detailed contents: 
Introduction 
 Scope (strategy, policies, standards), perimeter (geographic/organizational units), period 
covered (month/quarter/six months/year) 
Overall status 
 Satisfactory/not yet satisfactory/unsatisfactory 
    
Updates (as appropriate and relevant)
 Progress toward achieving the information security strategy 
 Elements completed/in-hand/planned 
 Changes in information security management system 
 ISMS policy revision, organizational structure to implement ISMS (including assignment of 
responsibilities) 
 Progress toward certification 
 ISMS (re)certification, certified information security audits 
 Budgeting/staffing/training 
 Financial situation, headcount adequacy, information security qualifications 
 Other information security activities 
 Business continuity management involvement, awareness campaigns, internal/external audit 
assistance
