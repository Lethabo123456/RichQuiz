6.6 Cyber Attack Protection 
 
The 
National 
Information 
Assurance 
Glossary [CNSS10] defines cyber attack as 
an attack, via cyberspace, targeting an 
enterprise’s use of cyberspace for the 
purpose 
of 
disrupting, 
disabling, 
destroying, or maliciously controlling a 
computing environment/infrastructure; or 
destroying the integrity of the data or 
stealing controlled information.
