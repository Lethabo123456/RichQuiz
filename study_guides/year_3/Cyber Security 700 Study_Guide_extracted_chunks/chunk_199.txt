benefit gained. 
 
Qualitative Risk Assessment 
It is not reasonable to suppose that all impact costs and likelihoods can with confidence be expressed 
quantitatively. Security breaches are rare, and organizations are reluctant to reveal them. 
Consequently, security incidence information is typically anecdotal or based on surveys and cannot be 
used to develop reliable or accurate probability or frequency values. At the same time, the total cost 
or potential loss due to a security breach is hard to quantify. The cost may depend on a variety of 
factors, such as length of downtime, amount and effect of adverse publicity, cost to recover, and other 
factors that are difficult to estimate.
