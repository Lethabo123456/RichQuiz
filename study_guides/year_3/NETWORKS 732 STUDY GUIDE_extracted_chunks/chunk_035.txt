Adaptability and Experience 
 
Although this process is presented in a linear fashion, practical experience may warrant 
deviations. For example, if a technician is already aware of unreliable cabling in a certain area, 
they might immediately test or replace that segment without performing every prior 
diagnostic check. However, following the step-by-step order generally ensures fewer mistakes 
and avoids unnecessary hardware or software replacements.
