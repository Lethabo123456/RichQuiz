1.7 Implement the Solution or Escalate as Necessary 
After carefully analysing a network issue and forming a solid plan of action, the next logical 
step is to implement the solution. This stage can range from a simple conﬁguration change, 
such as updating a routing table, to a lengthy hardware replacement, such as installing a new 
server hard drive. Regardless of complexity, implementing a solution requires methodical 
thinking, attention to detail, and preparation.

Eﬃcient and Safe Implementation Steps 
 
Tamara Dean (2013) outlines a set of practices that help ensure the resolution is applied both 
safely and eﬀectively. These steps reduce the risk of creating new issues while ﬁxing the 
current one. 
1. Notify All Aﬀected Users 
Communication is critical. Users who will be impacted by the ﬁx—whether a single 
user or the entire organization—should receive as much notice as possible. This 
allows them to prepare for potential downtime and helps manage expectations. 
2. Collect and Reference Documentation 
Keep all investigation notes, error reports, conﬁgurations, and logs nearby. These 
materials provide crucial context when applying the solution and troubleshooting 
any further issues.
