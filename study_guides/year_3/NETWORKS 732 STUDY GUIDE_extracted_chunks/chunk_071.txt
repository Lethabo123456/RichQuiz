Sharing and Communicating the Fix 

Once a ﬁx has been veriﬁed, it is essential to communicate it to your team or organization. 
This serves several purposes: 

• It keeps everyone informed of changes to network infrastructure. 
• It builds a knowledge base that others can reference. 
• It helps prevent repetition of troubleshooting steps for recurring or similar problems. 
 
The next step in the troubleshooting process—documenting your solution—ensures 
continuity, accountability, and team-wide preparedness.
