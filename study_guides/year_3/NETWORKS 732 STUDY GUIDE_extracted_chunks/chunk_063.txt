3. Create Backups and Save Previous States
Before making any changes: 
o Backup software or conﬁguration ﬁles. 
o Save copies of old settings. 
o Retain original hardware, such as NICs or cables, in case the new 
components do not work. 
Even minor changes should be documented or printed, so you can revert if needed. 

4. Apply the Fix Carefully 
Execute the planned change, replacement, or conﬁguration update. Record every 
step of your actions—this becomes part of the organization’s troubleshooting 
records. 

5. Test the Solution Immediately 
After implementation, validate the change by testing system functionality. This 
conﬁrms the problem is resolved and helps identify if new issues have been 
introduced.
