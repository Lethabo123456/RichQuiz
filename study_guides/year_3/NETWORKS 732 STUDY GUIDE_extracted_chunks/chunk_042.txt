1.3 Determine If Anything Has Changed 
 
During the early stages of troubleshooting, it's essential to determine whether recent changes 
have been made to the network environment. Many networks’ problems stem from 
modiﬁcations to software, hardware, or conﬁgurations, and identifying these changes can 
signiﬁcantly shorten the diagnostic process.

Questions to Help Identify Change-Related Issues 
 
Ask the following to isolate whether recent activity might have caused the problem: 
 
• Has the operating system or conﬁguration changed on any server, workstation, or 
network device? 
• Were any new components added to devices? 
• Were any components removed?  
• Have new users or network segments been introduced?  
• Was any device relocated within the network? 
• Was a device replaced entirely? 
• Was software installed or modiﬁed? 
• Was software removed?
