Importance of Change Documentation 
 
Maintaining accurate records of all changes is critical to successful troubleshooting. Logs 
should include: 

• A description of the change 
• The reason for the change 
• The date and time it was performed 
• Who performed the change 
 
Documentation should be accessible to all IT personnel, whether through a centralized web- 
based form or even a simple logbook kept in the server room. 

Real-World Example 
 
Suppose a group of users in the Marketing Department loses connectivity. Upon reviewing the 
network change log, you discover that a switch in that department's telecom closet was 
recently moved. This information allows you to: 

• Investigate whether the switch was reconnected improperly 
• Check for physical damage 
• Determine if it lost conﬁguration settings during the move
