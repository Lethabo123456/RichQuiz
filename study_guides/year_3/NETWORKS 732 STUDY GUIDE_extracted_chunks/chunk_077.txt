Tone Generator and Tone Locator 

When managing network infrastructure, especially in complex or poorly labelled 
telecommunications rooms, it can be diﬃcult to trace where a speciﬁc wire terminates. A tone 
generator and tone locator—often sold together as a toner and probe kit—solve this problem 
eﬃciently. The tone generator sends an electrical signal through the wire, and the tone locator 
detects this signal at the other end. Through trial and error, a technician can match cable ends, 
aiding signiﬁcantly in organizing, maintaining, and repairing physical connections. 
However, these tools are limited in scope. They don’t provide information about cable quality 
or whether the cable supports a given data rate. Furthermore, they should never be 
connected to live equipment, as the electrical current they emit can damage network 
hardware.
