6. Clean Up the Workspace 
A tidy environment prevents future mistakes or hazards. For example, if patch cables 
were fabricated, remove leftover insulation or crimping materials from the ﬂoor or 
server racks. 

7. Document the Final Results 
Record what was changed, how the issue was ﬁxed, and any side eﬀects or secondary 
observations. These records are useful not only for future troubleshooting, but also 
for team collaboration and audits. 

8. Verify Post-Implementation Success 
Especially for signiﬁcant or large-scale ﬁxes, revisit the site or system after a day or 
two. Conﬁrm that the solution is holding and has not created new problems, such as 
conﬁguration conﬂicts or user access issues.
