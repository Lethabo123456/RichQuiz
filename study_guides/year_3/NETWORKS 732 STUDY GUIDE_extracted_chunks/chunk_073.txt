Problem Tracking Systems 
 
Many organizations use call tracking systems—software tools also known as help desk 
software—that guide technicians through the problem documentation process. These 
systems typically assign each issue a unique ID and record information such as: 

• The user's identity and department 
• Nature of the problem (software or hardware) 
• Time spent troubleshooting 
• Steps taken and ﬁnal resolution 
 
These systems are often customizable, allowing organizations to tailor data ﬁelds to suit 
speciﬁc operational environments. For example, an oil reﬁnery might include ﬁelds for ﬂow- 
control software issues. If no formal system is in place, a simple digital form with standard 
ﬁelds should be used to document each problem systematically.
