5. Voltage Event Recorders 

Network hardware is highly sensitive to power ﬂuctuations, and even brief voltage 
irregularities can cause malfunctions or hardware damage. A voltage event recorder is a 
 
diagnostic tool that continuously monitors the quality of electrical power supplied to devices 
like servers, switches, or routers. It is plugged into the same outlet as the network device and 
records data over time. Later, this data is analysed using included software, which ﬂags voltage 
anomalies such as dips below standard frequency levels (e.g., below 60 Hz in North America) 
or spikes above safe thresholds. This helps technicians identify unstable power sources before 
they lead to outages or equipment failure.
