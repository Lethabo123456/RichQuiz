Using Vendor Resources 
 
Experienced network professionals may prefer to rely on intuition and experience, but 
product documentation and vendor resources should not be overlooked. Manuals often 
contain: 

• Speciﬁc conﬁguration instructions 
• Compatibility guidelines 
• Troubleshooting advice 
 
In addition, reputable vendors maintain online knowledge bases. Companies like Microsoft, 
Cisco, HP, and Red Hat oﬀer searchable web portals that contain up-to-date information, 
FAQs, and solutions.  
Call vendor support only after exhausting available documentation. Be aware that some 
vendors require a support agreement or charge per-incident or hourly fees.
