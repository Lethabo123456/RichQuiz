1.8 Verify Full System Functionality 
 
After implementing a network solution, the next critical step is to conﬁrm its eﬀectiveness. 
This involves thorough testing and evaluation to ensure the issue has been fully resolved 
without causing new complications. The nature of this testing will vary depending on the 
scope and complexity of the ﬁx. However, the ultimate goal remains the same: to restore 
reliable, eﬃcient network operations while avoiding unintended disruptions. 
 
Testing Based on the Nature and Scope of the Problem 

Testing should be directly aligned with the type of issue you resolved. For instance, if a faulty 
patch cable was replaced, a basic test would involve conﬁrming network connectivity from 
the connected device. If the device still fails to connect, the problem may persist due to a 
diﬀerent underlying issue, perhaps relating to logical conﬁgurations or NIC performance. In 
such cases, utilizing advanced hardware and software diagnostic tools—like cable testers or 
protocol analysers—may provide deeper insights. 
When a larger network segment or device is involved, such as a switch servicing multiple 
departments, it is essential to verify that each aﬀected workstation across all areas can 
connect and function as expected. A multi-point validation ensures that the replacement or 
ﬁx is eﬀective throughout the network and not just at one node.
