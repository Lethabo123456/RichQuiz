1.6 Establish a Plan of Action to Resolve the Problem 
 
Once the root cause of a network issue has been diagnosed, the next critical step is to devise 
and implement a strategic solution. However, implementation should not occur in haste. A 
sound plan requires careful consideration of its broader impacts, from user accessibility and 
security to cost and scalability (Dean, 2013, p. 617). 
Assessing Scope of the Solution 
 
The scope of a proposed solution deﬁnes how many users or systems it will aﬀect. A ﬁx applied 
to a single workstation might have minimal consequences, but replacing a cable or component 
linked to a server can disrupt access for the entire organization. Before making changes, 
evaluate whether the action will impact: 

• A single user 
• A department or workgroup 
• The entire enterprise 
 
To minimize disruptions, implement broad changes during oﬀ-peak hours when fewer users 
are active on the network.
