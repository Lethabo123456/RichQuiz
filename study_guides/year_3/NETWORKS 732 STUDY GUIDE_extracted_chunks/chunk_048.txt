Diagnostic Questions for Physical Layer Testing: 
 
• Is the device powered on? 
• Is the NIC ﬁrmly seated? 
• Is the antenna (for wireless NICs) active? 
• Are network cables securely connected to both ends? 
• Are patch panels and switches properly wired? 
• Are cables undamaged and connectors intact? 
• Are segment lengths within IEEE standards? 
• Are all devices compatible with the network’s speed and type? 

Start diagnostics by tracing the cable path—from the user’s NIC to the switch or server. Pay 
attention to computers that were recently relocated or recabled, as these are common 
sources of physical errors.
