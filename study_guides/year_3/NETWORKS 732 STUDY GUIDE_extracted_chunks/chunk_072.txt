1.9 Document Findings, Actions, and Outcomes 
 
Thorough documentation is essential in network troubleshooting and support. Whether part 
of a large IT team or a solo technician, it is crucial to record all symptoms, causes, and 
resolutions of network issues. The high volume and complexity of network problems make it 
impractical to rely on memory alone. Moreover, documentation ensures continuity when IT 
staﬀ change roles or leave the organization.
