Examples of Swapping for Testing: 
 
• Swap the user’s network cable to check for physical wear or failure. 
• Change the patch port or data jack. 
• Swap NICs between devices or install a new one (ensuring compatibility). 
• In some cases, replace a switch or router if an entire segment is aﬀected, though this 
may involve more downtime and conﬁguration work. 

To avoid service interruptions altogether, redundancy can be built into the network. For 
example, a server with dual NICs allows automatic failover without downtime—a more robust 
solution than hot-swapping components (Dean, 2013, pp. 614–615). 
Note: Always ensure the replacement component has the same speciﬁcations as the original 
to avoid incompatibility or hardware damage.
