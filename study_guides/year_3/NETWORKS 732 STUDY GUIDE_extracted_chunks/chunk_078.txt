Multimeter 

A multimeter is a fundamental instrument in both electrical and networking work. It combines 
the functionality of a voltmeter, ohmmeter, and often an ammeter into one compact device. 
In a network setting, a multimeter can verify whether a cable conducts electricity correctly, 
detect noise on the line, and test for resistance or shorts that may aﬀect signal quality. 
Resistance and impedance are particularly critical measurements. Impedance reﬂects how the 
cable resists signal ﬂow at higher frequencies and is key to identifying faults such as poor 
terminations or cable damage. High-end multimeters oﬀer features like noise detection and 
greater precision, though basic models suﬃce for most network troubleshooting tasks.
