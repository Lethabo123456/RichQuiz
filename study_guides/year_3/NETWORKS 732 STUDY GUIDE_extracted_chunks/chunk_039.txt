Determining the Scope of the Problem 
 
When a problem is not immediately obvious, determine the demographic and geographic 
impact. Questions to consider: 

• Is one user or workstation aﬀected? 
• A workgroup or department? 
• One physical location or the entire organization? 
 
Understanding when the problem began is just as important: 
 
• When did the problem start?
• Has it occurred intermittently? 
• Does it only happen during certain times or days? 

Knowing where and when the issue occurs can help distinguish a workstation issue from a 
network infrastructure problem. For example, if only one department is aﬀected, examine 
local switches, cabling, or a departmental server. If all users are aﬀected, investigate core 
switches, WAN links, or backbone equipment.
