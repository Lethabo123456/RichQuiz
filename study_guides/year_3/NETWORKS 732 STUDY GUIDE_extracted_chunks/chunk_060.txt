Maintaining Vendor Contact Lists 
 
Maintaining a centralized, up-to-date vendor support list can streamline troubleshooting and 
procurement. The list should include:
 
• Vendor name 
• Tech support number 
• Contact person (if available) 
• Website or support portal 
• Support policies and payment structures 
• Current agreement status 
 
This information should be accessible to all IT staﬀ, especially during emergencies. 
 
Seek Peer Review When Unsure 
 
If you're uncertain about the proposed action plan—even after thorough diagnostics— 
collaboration is encouraged. Consult colleagues or external experts. Often, shared experience 
leads to smarter, less disruptive decisions and can uncover risks or alternatives you might 
not have considered.
