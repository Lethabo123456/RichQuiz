Chapter 1: Troubleshooting 
 
1.1 Troubleshooting Methodology 
 
Successful network troubleshooting relies on a logical and methodical approach. According 
to CompTIA guidelines, following a structured process helps ensure eﬀective problem-solving 
and is essential for achieving Network+ certiﬁcation. These steps form a repeatable 
framework applicable across most network environments. 
CompTIA’s Recommended Troubleshooting Steps 
 
• Identify the Problem 
 
o Gather relevant information (logs, user reports, error messages). 
o Identify visible symptoms. 
o Ask users detailed questions. 

o Investigate if any changes have occurred (e.g., conﬁguration, hardware). 
 
• Establish a Theory of Probable Cause 
o Consider the most obvious explanations ﬁrst. 
o Eliminate unlikely causes based on available data. 

• Test the Theory 
o Conﬁrm or reject the hypothesis using targeted tests. 
o If the theory is correct, plan the solution. 
o If the theory is incorrect, revise it or escalate the issue. 
 
• Establish a Plan of Action 
o Formulate a solution. 
o Anticipate any potential consequences of the ﬁx. 
o Escalate if the ﬁx exceeds your access or expertise.
