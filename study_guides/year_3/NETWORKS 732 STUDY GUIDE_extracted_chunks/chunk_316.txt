How Wi-Fi Extenders deal with Attenuation 
The Functioning Mechanism of WiFi Extenders and How to setup Wi-Fi extender 
• The WiFi extenders function by capturing the existing WiFi signal emitted by your router 
or Industrial Routers. These devices are strategically placed in an area where the signal 
remains strong enough to be eﬀectively caught, while also being near the dead zone that 
requires coverage. 
• The extender plays a crucial role in enhancing the signal. It captures the signal, ampliﬁes 
it, and then retransmits it. This ampliﬁcation process signiﬁcantly improves the strength 
of the signal, thus extending its reach to areas that may have had weak or non-existent 
coverage. 
• The signal is ampliﬁed and then redistributed, eﬀectively creating a new WiFi hotspot that 
extends its range. As a result, devices in previously weak or dead zones can now connect 
to a stronger signal, improving their connection speed and reliability. 
• Wireless extenders today come in dual-band or tri-band variations. Dual-band extenders 
work seamlessly on both the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz frequencies, ensuring optimal 
performance across a range of devices. On the other hand, tri-band extenders go a step 
further by incorporating an additional 5 GHz band. This not only alleviates network 
congestion but also enhances overall speed and reliability to a greater extent.
