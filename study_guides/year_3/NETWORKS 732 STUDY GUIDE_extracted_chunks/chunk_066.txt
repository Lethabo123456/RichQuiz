Knowing When to Escalate 
 
Even with a well-planned implementation, you may encounter unresolved problems. When 
your skills, time, or tools fall short, the issue should be escalated. This might involve: 

• A more experienced technician within your team 
• A second-level support analyst 
• Or, for highly complex problems, a third-level specialist
 
Escalation ensures that the problem is handled eﬃciently and reduces the risk of introducing 
further damage through trial-and-error ﬁxes. Make your escalation decision based on: 
• User impact 
• Severity and urgency 
• Familiarity with the issue 
• Your technical expertise
