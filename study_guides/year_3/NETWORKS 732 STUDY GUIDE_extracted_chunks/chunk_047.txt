Verify Physical Layer Connectivity 

The Physical Layer (Layer 1 of the OSI model) accounts for over half of all network issues. This 
layer includes cabling, connectors, NICs, and signal transmission. Faults here are often simple 
but can lead to intermittent or total connectivity loss. 
 
Common Symptoms of Physical Layer Problems: 
 
• Inconsistent or no connection to the network 
• Intermittent performance drops 
• Inability to access speciﬁc servers or services 

Possible Physical Causes: 
 
• Cable segments exceeding IEEE standards (e.g., Ethernet > 100m) 
• EMI or cross talk disrupting signals 
• Faulty terminations or loose connectors 
• Damaged or bent cables 
• Failing NICs or transceivers 
 
Note: While software errors generally don’t indicate physical problems, they sometimes do. 
For instance, database access errors may stem from a faulty connection to the database server.
