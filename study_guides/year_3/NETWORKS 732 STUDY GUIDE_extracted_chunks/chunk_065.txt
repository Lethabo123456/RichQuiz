Handling Large-Scale Changes Strategically 
 
When applying wide-reaching changes—such as a ﬁrmware upgrade across a network of VPN 
routers—it is best to deploy the solution in stages. This cautious approach oﬀers several 
beneﬁts: 
• It reduces the risk of an organization-wide outage. 
• Any issues that arise can be localized and corrected before spreading. 
• You can evaluate the solution’s eﬀectiveness and make adjustments. 
 
For example, if a VPN router conﬁguration must be changed across all oﬃces, it may be 
tempting to apply these changes remotely. However, this could raise security concerns, 
especially if remote access is compromised. In these situations, you might prefer to: 

• Visit each site personally 
• Or coordinate with a trusted local IT technician to apply the ﬁx on site.
