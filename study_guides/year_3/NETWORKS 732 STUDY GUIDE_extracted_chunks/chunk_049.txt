Verify Logical Connectivity 
 
Once physical integrity is conﬁrmed, focus turns to Logical Layer issues. This includes: 
 
• Software and OS settings 
• Network services 
• Device drivers 
• Protocols 
• IP and routing conﬁgurations 
 
Questions to Identify Logical Issues: 
 
• Do error messages indicate missing or corrupted ﬁles or drivers? 
• Do they reference resource issues (e.g., memory)? 
• Has the system been recently altered, updated, or conﬁgured? 
• Is the issue tied to speciﬁc applications? 
• Is it consistent or intermittent? 
• Does it aﬀect just one user, a group, or all users?
