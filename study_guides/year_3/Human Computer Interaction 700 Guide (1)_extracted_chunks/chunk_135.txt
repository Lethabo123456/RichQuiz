3.5.1 Instructing   
 
In the instructing interaction type, users carry out tasks by giving the system specific instructions on what 
to do. This model is commonly seen in home entertainment systems, consumer electronics, and 
computers. The actions can range from simple commands like "tell the time" or "print a file" to more 
complex operations such as setting notifications for appointments. 
The method by which instructions are issued can vary, including pressing buttons, using touch gestures, 
or even giving voice commands. 

One of the key advantages of an instructing-based interaction is its speed and efficiency. It is particularly 
effective when repetitive actions are required, such as saving, deleting, or organizing files. This kind of 
interaction model is highly beneficial in scenarios where users need to perform the same task on multiple 
objects, making the process seamless and quick.
