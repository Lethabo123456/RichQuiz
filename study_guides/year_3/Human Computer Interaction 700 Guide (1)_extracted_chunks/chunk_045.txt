• Considering what people are good and bad at  
• Considering what might help people with the way they currently do things

1.5 What Is Interaction Design?  
 
Interaction design involves crafting interactive products to facilitate how people communicate and 
interact in their daily and professional lives. It focuses on creating experiences that improve and enrich 
the way individuals work, connect, and engage with technology. In broader terms, it is about designing 
tools and systems that align with human behaviors and needs. Terry Winograd succinctly described 
interaction design as "designing spaces for human communication and interaction"
