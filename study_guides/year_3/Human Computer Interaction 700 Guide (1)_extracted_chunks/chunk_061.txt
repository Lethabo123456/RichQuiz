1.9.2 User Experience Goals 
 
A diversity of user experience goals have been articulated in interaction design, which covers a range of 
emotions and felt experiences. These include desirable and undesirable aspects, as shown in Table 1.1. 

Many qualities related to user experience are subjective and focus on how a system feels to the user. 
These differ from usability goals, which assess how useful or productive a system is from a more objective 
standpoint. User experience encompasses a broader range of emotions, sensations, and feelings that a 
person has when interacting with a system. The language used to describe user experience is vast and 
often overlaps, offering different ways to express how an experience varies depending on factors like 
time, place, and technology. 
For instance, listening to music may feel highly pleasurable in the shower but enjoyable in the car. The 
emotional impact also varies based on the context, such as listening to music on a powerful system versus 
on a smartphone with shuffle mode, where the unpredictability of the song can be serendipitously 
enjoyable.
