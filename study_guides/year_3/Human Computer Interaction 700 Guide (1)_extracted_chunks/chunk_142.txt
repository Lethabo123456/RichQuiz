3.5.4 Exploring   
 
Exploring interaction involves moving through either virtual or physical environments, leveraging 
people's existing knowledge of how they navigate spaces. Users can interact with digital 3D environments 
or physical spaces embedded with sensing technologies that trigger digital or physical responses when 
detecting movement or presence. 

Examples include: 
• Virtual 3D environments, like the Metaverse, virtual conferences, or video games (e.g., Fortnite), 
where users explore buildings, rooms, or landscapes. 
• Augmented environments designed for home use, where holograms or virtual animals appear in 
real-world settings (e.g., in living rooms). 
• Virtual worlds that offer experiences beyond the physical world, allowing users to engage with 
scenarios or landscapes that would normally be impossible or invisible. 
• Architectural VR representations, where designers use virtual environments to let clients explore 
and understand spatial layouts. 
• 3D visualizations of complex datasets, where scientists manipulate data points through hand 
gestures in virtual environments. 
This form of interaction capitalizes on people's ability to explore and navigate spaces intuitively, offering 
immersive and interactive experiences in both digital and real-world contexts.
