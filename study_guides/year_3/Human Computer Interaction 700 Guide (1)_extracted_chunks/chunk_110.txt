Usability engineering, a practice that has emerged over the years, focuses on formalizing and measuring 
usability criteria. By specifying quantifiable performance measures and assessing products against them, 
usability engineering ensures that the final product aligns with user needs and expectations. This 
methodology is widely applied, particularly in fields such as health informatics.
