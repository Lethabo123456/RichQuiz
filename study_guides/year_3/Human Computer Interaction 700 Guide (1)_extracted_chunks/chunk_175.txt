4.2.6 Problem-Solving, Planning, Reasoning, and Decision-Making  
 
Problem-solving, planning, reasoning, and decision-making are reflective cognitive processes that involve 
evaluating available options and considering the potential consequences of different actions. These 
processes often require conscious thought, discussions (either internal or with others), and the use of 
tools like maps, books, or writing materials. Reasoning, in particular, entails evaluating various scenarios 
and choosing the best solution. For instance, when planning a vacation, individuals assess various factors 
such as cost, weather, accommodations, flights, local amenities, and entertainment options before 
making their decision. 
When faced with numerous choices, such as when shopping, people often stick with familiar products 
rather than exploring new options. This tendency has been explained through evolutionary theories, 
suggesting that humans have evolved to make quick, "good enough" decisions using fast and frugal 

heuristics. Instead of considering all available information, individuals focus on key cues, like recognizing 
a brand name. This behaviour is also linked to foraging instincts, where animals, like bears, stay in one 
area until resources are depleted before moving to a new one. Similarly, shoppers may stay with familiar 
brands to avoid the costs of switching, such as the time it takes to find and adapt to a new product. 
This resistance to change presents challenges for advocates promoting healthier or more sustainable 
choices. To address this, technology could be designed to encourage consumers to explore alternatives. 
For example, mobile augmented reality (AR) apps could be developed to draw attention to key qualities 
of new products, such as their health benefits or environmentally friendly packaging. These AR apps could 
present minimalist, appealing visuals to capture the shopper's interest without overwhelming them. 
However, the challenge lies in encouraging users to download and try these apps in the first place.
