The benefit of assembling diverse teams is the potential for generating innovative ideas and original 
designs, but this diversity can also lead to communication challenges. Team members from different fields 
may have varying perspectives and use the same terms with different meanings, causing 
misunderstandings and confusion. The more varied the backgrounds and expertise within the team, the 
harder it can be to align on design processes and tasks, especially if team members have not previously 
worked together. Research has shown that integration across disciplines can be difficult, and the greater 
the diversity in culture and organizational structure, the more complex collaboration becomes.

Interaction Design Consultancies   
 
Interaction design has become an integral part of product and service development, with UX consultants 
and the tech industry recognizing its crucial role in creating successful interactive products. Its value 
extends beyond IT companies, as sectors like finance, retail, government, marketing, and even video and 
film production now appreciate the importance of good interaction design. In the competitive 
smartphone app market, demonstrating that a product is easy, effective, and engaging has become key 
to standing out. Companies recognize that usability directly impacts customer satisfaction, engagement, 
and retention, with marketing departments relying on tools like A/B testing to measure the effectiveness 
of various UI designs on business metrics.
