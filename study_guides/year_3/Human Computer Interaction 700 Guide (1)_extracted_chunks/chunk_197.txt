Chapter 5: SOCIAL INTERACTION  
 
5.1 Introduction   
 
Humans are inherently social beings, thriving on living, working, learning, playing, interacting, conversing, 
and socializing with one another. A wide array of technologies has been created to help us maintain social 
connections even when we are physically separated. These technologies—such as smartphones, 
videoconferencing, social media, gaming platforms, messaging apps, and telepresence—have become 
integral to the fabric of modern society, each offering unique ways to facilitate human connection. 
Understanding social behaviour can be approached from various perspectives. This chapter focuses on 
how people communicate and collaborate both face-to-face and remotely in their personal, professional, 
and everyday lives. The goal is to offer insights and guidance for designing social technologies that 
enhance and extend these interactions. The chapter also explores communication technologies that have 
transformed how people stay connected, build friendships, and manage their social and professional 
networks. 
The mechanisms traditionally used in face-to-face conversations are examined, particularly in the context 
of how they have been adapted for technology-based interactions. Additionally, the growing popularity 
of social games as a medium for fostering interaction is discussed, shedding light on their appeal and role 
in modern social dynamics.
