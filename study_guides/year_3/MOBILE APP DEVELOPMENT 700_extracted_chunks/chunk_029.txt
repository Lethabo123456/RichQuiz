Chapter 1: Introduction to Mobile Apps & Your First App 

1.1 Introduction 
Mobile, mobile, mobile! Mobile technology is certainly receiving a lot of attention in the IT world as 
well as the general business world right now. It seems everyone is executing a mobile strategy, 
designing a mobile app, or worrying about managing mobile devices. But why all the buzz? What 
makes mobile so special that it garners this much attention? In this chapter, you explore some of the 
key reasons behind the hype. It really is not “much ado about nothing”! 
 
Transformative Devices 
For all the hype, there must be something that makes these devices important. There is! Mobile 
devices add a host of new possibilities for business and personal software because they are truly the 
first mobile computing platforms. Although laptops and netbooks are moveable, their size significantly 
affects how easily they are transported. Very few people carry a laptop during their every waking hour 
to every location they visit. However, if this were their only advantage, mobile devices would not be 
causing such a stir. There are many technical and nontechnical factors fueling mobile’s surging 
popularity.
