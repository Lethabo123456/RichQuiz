▪ Lines 25–27—The margin attributes layout_marginLeft, layout_marginStart, and 
layout_marginTop tell Android how much space to put between the widget and the referenced 
locations. MarginLeft and Start mean the same thing. However, before API 21, only marginLeft was 
available. If your app has a minimum of 21, you will only have to use marginStart. The default distance 
of 8 is the current value. Change the left/start margins to 16dp and the top margin to 32dp. You will 
often have to tweak these values to get the layout to look exactly the way you want it to.

Locate the Text folder in the Palette. (You may have to switch to Design view.) A number of widgets 
for entering information are displayed. 

The widget for entering data in Android is called an EditText. 
Each of the EditText widgets listed is configured for the entry of a different type of data. The different 
configurations dictate what soft keyboard is displayed when the widget is clicked and, in some cases, 
how the text is formatted as it is entered. For example, the EditText named Number will display a 
keyboard with only numbers on it, whereas the EditText named Plain Text will display a full keyboard. 
Drag the Plain Text EditText to the right of the Name: TextView. Use the left circle on the EditText to 
create a left constraint to the Name: TextView. Go to the Attributes panel and expand All Attributes. 
Scroll down until you find layout_constraints. Expand it and scroll down to 
layout_constraintBaseline_toBaselineOf. Select @id/textView from the dropdown. This will center the 
EditText vertically with the TextView.
