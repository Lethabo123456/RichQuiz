Create the Data Entry Form 
The data entry portion of the MainActivity allows users to enter 
information on their contacts. The data entry form primarily 
relies on the EditText and TextView widgets that were introduced in the Hello World app. New concepts introduced 
include configuring the EditTexts to limit and format the input, 
movement (tabbing) through the data entry widgets, using a custom pop-up window to enter the birthday with a 
CalendarView widget, and using a ScrollView to expand the available screen real estate. 

Open the activity_main.xml file (if it is not already open) and     
switch to Design view. Scroll to the Containers folder in the Palette. Drag a ScrollView to the middle 
of the Design or Blueprint views anywhere between the toolbar and the navigation bar. Alternatively, 
you can drag it to the Component Tree below the navbar and toolbar (Figure 4.7). If the Component 
Tree is not displayed below the Palette, it is likely minimized and can be opened by selecting it in the 
vertical bar directly to the left of the Palette. 

Figure 4.7 Component Tree with ScrollView added 
 
Click on all four buttons in the Layout diagram. Change the left and right values to 0. Leave the top 
and bottom at 8. Change layout_width and layout_height to match_constraint in the Attributes panel. 
The values will then change to 0dp. Next, turn your attention to the Component Tree. Notice that 
the ScrollView has a LinearLayout embedded beneath it. By default, the ScrollView has no contents. 
ScrollViews can have only one widget as their content. However, if that widget is some type of 
layout, more widgets can be added as long as they are within that layout. Since a LinearLayout 
allows  very   limited  control  of where our widgets are placed, we will change it to a ConstraintLayout 
to get that control. Click on the LinearLayout in the Component Tree and delete it with the delete key. Next, locate a ConstraintLayout 
in the Layouts folder of the Palette and drag it to the same location of the LinearLayout. 
Switch to Text view and examine the XML associated with the ScrollView (Listing 4.2).
