How long will it take to access a record? If one complete revolution takes 16.8 ms, then the average 
rotational delay, as shown in Figure 4.8(b), is 8.4 ms. The data-transfer time varies from device 
to device but let us say the value is 0.00094 ms per byte, the size of the record dictates this value. 
These are traditional values. The chapter exercises offer an opportunity to explore current rates.
