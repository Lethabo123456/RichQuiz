iii. Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS) 

RTOS ensures immediate processing and response to inputs, crucial for time-sensitive 
applications. Processes tasks within strict time constraints (e.g., milliseconds or microseconds). 
Divided into Hard Real-Time Systems (strict deadlines) and Soft Real-Time Systems (fewer rigid 
deadlines). Found in critical systems like medical devices, industrial automation, and robotics. 

Advantages: 
➢ Ensures high reliability and predictability. 
➢ Optimal for environments where timing is critical.

Disadvantages: 
 
➢ Limited multitasking; prioritizes real-time tasks. 
➢ Complex design and higher cost.
