v. Embedded Operating Systems

These are specialized OS designed for embedded devices with dedicated functionality. Small, 
efficient, and tailored for specific hardware and applications. Found in devices like IoT gadgets, 
automotive systems, home appliances, and medical equipment. Often incorporates real-time 
operating capabilities. 

Advantages: 
➢ Low resource consumption. 
➢ Optimized for reliability and performance in specific tasks. 

Disadvantages: 
➢ Limited flexibility; not suitable for general-purpose computing. 
➢ Typically, difficult to upgrade or modify.
