6.1.1.3 Generalisation and Overfitting 
A key challenge in supervised learning is generalization: how well the model performs on new 
data, not just the training examples. 
• Overfitting: When a model learns the training data too well — including noise — and fails 
to generalize. 
• Underfitting: When the model is too simple to capture patterns in the data. 
Strategies like cross-validation, regularization, and early stopping help strike a balance between 
accuracy and generalization.

Conclusion 
This case study demonstrates how supervised learning enables practical robotics applications. 
The face mask detection robot showcases: 
• Feasibility: Standard ML algorithms solve real problems 
• Efficiency: SVM provides excellent accuracy/speed trade-off 
• Scalability: System easily deployed across multiple locations
