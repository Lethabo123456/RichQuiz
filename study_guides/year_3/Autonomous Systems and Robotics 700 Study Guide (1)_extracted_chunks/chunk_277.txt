5.3  Consensus Algorithms in Distributed Robotics 

Consensus algorithms form the mathematical and computational backbone of reliable multi-
agent systems, enabling distributed robots to reach agreement on shared information, 
coordinated decisions, and system states despite the inherent challenges of distributed 
computing environments. These algorithms are essential for maintaining coherent collective 
behaviour when individual agents may experience communication delays, hardware failures, or 
conflicting sensor readings that could otherwise lead to system fragmentation or inconsistent 
behaviour. 

The fundamental challenge addressed by consensus algorithms stems from the distributed nature 
of multi-agent systems, where individual robots must make decisions based on incomplete local 
information while contributing to globally coherent system behaviour. Unlike centralised systems 
where a single controller can maintain perfect knowledge of system state, distributed robotic
