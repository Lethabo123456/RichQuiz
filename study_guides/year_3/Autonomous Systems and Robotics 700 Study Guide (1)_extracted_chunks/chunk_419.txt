What the Model Learns 
The model learns patterns like: 
• Repeated jerks in IMU data = likely obstacle → turn or stop 
• Smooth forward acceleration = safe to continue 
• Spinning detected → realign by turning left/right

Sensor Fusion Using Deep Learning 

Real-world robots rarely rely on a single sensor. Effective navigation requires fusing information 
from multiple sources: 
• Cameras: Rich visual information but affected by lighting 
• LIDAR: Accurate depth but sparse data 
• Ultrasonic: Reliable close-range but limited range 
• IMU: Motion tracking but subject to drift
