2.3.2 Types of LiDAR Systems 
LiDAR systems are typically classified into three types: 

1. Airborne LiDAR 
In the instance of mapping extensive geographic regions, the airborne lidar comes in handy 
during the mapping of terrain such as thick forests, urban zones, or coastal areas. This type of 
LiDAR is cleaned up and installed on a plane or drone. Most effective for surveying large areas 
2. Terrestrial LiDAR 
This one is practical in use applied on ground level and can be used to target small regions like 
the study of small areas, structures, infrastructural developments and even archaeological 
studies. 
3. Bathymetric LiDAR 
This system can do underwater mapping, incorporates a green-type laser light that can penetrate 
water and therefore allow successful measurements of the depth of rivers, lakes and even the 
near coastal water.
