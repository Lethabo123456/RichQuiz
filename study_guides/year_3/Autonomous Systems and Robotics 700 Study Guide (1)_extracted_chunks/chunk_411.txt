Numbers? The scores are cumulative future rewards. The AI thinks long-term, 
calculating total points it will earn over many future actions, not just immediate rewards. 

Bottom Line: The AI discovered the optimal strategy: maintain a cycle of 
High→Deliver→Medium→Recharge→High, maximizing rewards over time. 

6.1.3.3 Q-Learning vs DQN 

Feature                Q-Learning                          DQN (Deep Q-Learning)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
State Space            Discrete, small                     High-dimensional, continuous 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Memory                 Tabular                             Neural network weights 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Speed of Learning      Fast in small domains               Slower but scalable 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stability              Can converge with enough episodes   Requires careful tuning (e.g., replay) 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Robotic Applicability  Ideal for toy/grid worlds           Preferred for real-world robot control
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
 
Reinforcement learning provides robots with the capability to learn from interaction, adapt over 
time, and improve their performance autonomously. Q-learning is suitable for small, manageable 
environments and can be used to teach robots decision-making strategies in energy, motion, or 
task execution. DQN extends these principles to more complex, real-world robotic tasks where 
learning must occur from raw or structured sensor data. 
Together, Q-learning and DQN form a conceptual and practical foundation for intelligent, 
autonomous robot behaviour.
