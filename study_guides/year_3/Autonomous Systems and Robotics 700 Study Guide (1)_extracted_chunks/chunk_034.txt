1.8  Where Robotics and Automated Systems are Used 

8.1.1 Healthcare 
- Surgical Robots: Robots like the da Vinci Surgical System assist surgeons in performing 
minimally invasive procedures with precision. 
- Rehabilitation Robots: Devices like Lokomat support physical therapy for patients recovering 
from injuries or neurological disorders. 
- Patient Care Robots: Robots such as PARO provide emotional support, particularly in elderly 
care settings. 
- Robotic Prosthetics: Advanced prosthetic limbs powered by AI and neural interfaces allow 
amputees to regain mobility and dexterity. 
- Autonomous Delivery Robots: Hospitals use robots like TUG to transport medication and 
supplies. 
- Benefits to society: 
• Enhanced precision and reduced human error. 
• Faster recovery times due to minimally invasive techniques. 
• Improved access to healthcare in underserved regions through telepresence robots.
