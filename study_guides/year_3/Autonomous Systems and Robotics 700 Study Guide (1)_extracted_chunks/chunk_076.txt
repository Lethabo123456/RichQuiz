2.5.5.7 Cropping an Image 
Cropping is useful for removing unwanted outer areas from an image or for focusing on a 
particular part of the image. Cropping in OpenCV is easily achieved by slicing arrays, since images 
are stored as NumPy arrays. 
 
2.5.2 Introduction to Face Recognition 
Face recognition technology identifies or verifies a person from a digital image or a video source. 
It is distinct from face detection, which merely locates the presence of faces in an image. Face 
recognition involves more complex processes that include identifying unique features of a face 
and comparing them to a database of known faces.
 
Face recognition uses machine learning algorithms to learn the distinctive features of faces during 
a training phase. Once trained, the system can recognise the same faces in new images. OpenCV 
supports several algorithms for face recognition, such as Eigenfaces, Fisherfaces, and Local Binary 
Patterns Histograms (LBPH), each suitable for different types of applications. 

Eigenfaces for Face Recognition 
Eigenfaces is one of the simplest face recognition approaches. It involves reducing the 
dimensionality of the image data using Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to the most significant 
features, which are called eigenfaces. The eigenfaces capture the main variations in the face 
images, which can then be used to compare and recognise individual faces.
