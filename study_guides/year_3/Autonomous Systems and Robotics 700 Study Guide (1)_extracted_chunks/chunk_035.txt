8.1.2 Agriculture 
- Precision Farming: Robots like Agrobot harvest crops with minimal waste by using AI and 
machine vision. 
- Weed Control: Autonomous robots such as the ecoRobotix identify and remove weeds 
without herbicides. 
- Livestock Monitoring: Drones and robotic devices monitor livestock health and behaviour. 
- Harvesting Robots: Machines like Strawberry-picking robots efficiently harvest delicate crops 
without damage. 
- Soil Analysis Robots: Sensors-equipped robots analyse soil conditions to optimize planting. 
- Benefits to farming communities: 
• Higher crop yields through optimized farming practices. 
• Reduced environmental impact with targeted pesticide use. 
• Labor cost savings and increased productivity. 

8.1.3 Manufacturing 
- Assembly Line Robots: Collaborative robots (cobots) work alongside humans to assemble 
products efficiently. 
- Quality Control: Robots equipped with AI and sensors inspect products for defects. 
- Logistics: Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) transport materials across factories. 
- 3D Printing Robots: Robotic arms are used for additive manufacturing in industries like 
aerospace and automotive. 
- Painting Robots: Automotive industries employ robots to achieve uniform paint applications. 
- Benefits to manufacturing companies: 
• Reduced operational costs and consistent quality. 
• Improved workplace safety by automating hazardous tasks. 
• Flexibility to adapt to production changes.
