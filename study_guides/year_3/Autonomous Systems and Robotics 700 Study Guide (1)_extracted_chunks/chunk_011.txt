1.2  Different Types of Robots and their Applications  
The world of robotics is as diverse as it is fascinating. From humanoid robots that resemble 
humans to autonomous mobile robots that navigate on their own, robots come in various forms, 
each designed to tackle specific tasks. 

Below are the different types of robots and the unique roles they play in our world: 
1.2.1 Humanoid Robot: A humanoid robot is a robot that 
is designed to resemble a human being in appearance 
and behaviour. Humanoid robots are often used in 
research and development, as well as in customer 
service and entertainment. 
1.2.2 Cobot: A cobot is a robot that is designed to work 
safely alongside humans. Cobots are typically equipped 
with sensors and safety features that prevent them from 
harming humans. Cobots are utilised in various 
industries, including manufacturing and healthcare. 
1.2.3 Domestic Robot: A domestic robot is a robot that is 
designed to perform household tasks, such as cleaning, cooking, and laundry. Domestic robots 
are becoming increasingly popular as they can help people save time and energy. 
1.2.4 Hospital Robots: Hospital robots are used for a variety of tasks in hospitals, such as 
assisting with surgery, delivering medication, and transporting patients. Hospital robots can 
help improve patient care and reduce the risk of infection.
