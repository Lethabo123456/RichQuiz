Summary: Understanding the Learned Q-Table 
The Q-table represents the robot's learned values for each possible action in different battery 
states, as a result of training with Q-learning. Each entry in the table corresponds to a numerical 
value indicating the expected long-term reward for taking a specific action in a given state. 

The robot has two available actions: 
• deliver: proceed with the delivery task 
• recharge: return to charge its battery 
And three discrete battery levels as states: 
• low 
• medium 
• high

Based on the final Q-values: 
• When the battery is low, the Q-value for recharge is higher than for deliver. This 
indicates that the robot has learned it is generally better to recharge when battery 
levels are low to avoid penalties or task failure. 
• At medium battery, both actions have similar Q-values, but deliver has a slightly higher 
value, suggesting it is usually more beneficial to continue the task than to recharge. 
• With a high battery level, deliver has the highest Q-value across all states, showing 
that delivery is the most rewarding action in this situation.
