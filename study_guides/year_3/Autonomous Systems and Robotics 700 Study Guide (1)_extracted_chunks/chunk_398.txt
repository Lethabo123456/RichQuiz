Common Applications in Robotics: 
1. Clustering: Grouping similar environments or objects 
2. Dimensionality Reduction: Simplifying complex sensor data 
3. Anomaly Detection: Identifying unusual situations 
4. Feature Learning: Discovering important patterns in raw data 

Strengths: 
• Works with readily available sensor data 
• No expensive labeling process 
• Discovers patterns humans might miss 
 
Limitations: 
• No direct feedback on correctness 
• Difficult to evaluate performance 
• May find patterns that aren't useful
 
While unsupervised learning helps robots understand their environment by finding patterns, it 
doesn't tell them what to do. This is where we need a different approach: 
The Gap:
 
• Supervised Learning: "This is a kitchen" ✓ 
• Unsupervised Learning: "These rooms are similar" ✓ 
• Missing: "What actions should I take in each situation?"
