1.5  Software and Programming of Robotic and Automated Systems 
 
Software plays a pivotal role in the functionality of 
modern industrial robots. While the complex CPU (or 
PLC) can be thought of as the brain, it’s the software that 
acts as the robot’s mind and soul. Without solidly built 
software systems, robots would be little more than 
heavy mechanical paperweights.

Let’s explore this further:  
• Versatile operating systems lie at the core: They provide the foundation for integration 
and communication between a robot’s hardware components, though these systems are 
often customised or proprietary depending on the application. These OS platforms 
provide a stable foundation for developing and deploying robotic applications. 
• Programming languages drive the robot’s behaviour: From low-level languages for precise 
control to high-level ones for rapid prototyping, a diverse array of options empowers 
developers to create sophisticated robotic programs tailored to specific industrial needs. 
• User interfaces for total control: These bridge the gap between human operators and 
robotic systems. Well-designed UIs allow for intuitive programming, monitoring, and 
control of robots, optimising operations and enhancing productivity on the factory floor. 
Thoughtful interface design is crucial for harnessing the full potential of these advanced 
machines.
