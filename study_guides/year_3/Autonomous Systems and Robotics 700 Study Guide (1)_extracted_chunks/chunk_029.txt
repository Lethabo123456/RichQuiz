To sum it up, manual systems and fully automated systems represent two contrasting approaches 
to task execution and control, each with unique benefits and limitations. Manual systems rely 
entirely on human input, emphasising adaptability and low-cost operation, but face challenges 
related to inefficiency and error-proneness. Fully automated systems, in contrast, leverage 
cutting-edge technology to achieve superior efficiency, precision, and scalability, albeit with high 
costs and potential risks. 

Understanding the trade-offs between these systems is essential for 
determining their suitability across various domains, from low-resource settings to high-tech 
industries. By integrating the strengths of both approaches or transitioning gradually, 
organisations can strike a balance between human ingenuity and technological innovation, 
ensuring optimised outcomes while addressing challenges such as cost, safety, and ethical 
considerations.
