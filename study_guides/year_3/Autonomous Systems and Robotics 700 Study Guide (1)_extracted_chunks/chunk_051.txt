2.3.3 LiDAR Sensors and Systems 
LiDAR sensors comprise the most important parts of any LiDAR system, as they emit laser pulses 
and catch the reflected light afterwards. These sensors have various forms according to the type 
of application that the sensor is targeted for: 

1. Topographic LiDAR 
The system has normally been employed in the mapping of the Earth’s topographic surfaces, 
where near-infrared laser pulses are employed in the gathering of information regarding terrain. 
2. Bathymetric LiDAR 
The system developed is aimed at working underwater and will use green lasers to filter through 
water to determine the depth of the water. 
3. Mobile LiDAR 
The Mobile LiDAR systems are mounted on vehicles and, as such, allow for the capture of real-
time data and are therefore very useful for road and infrastructure mapping, even for 
city mapping. 
4. Flash LiDAR 
That is very useful in applications, including obstacle detection for self-driving cars where an 
entire scene needs to be scanned using only one laser pulse.
