1 Robotics and AS Concept Introduction and Foundations 
 
1.1  What is Robotics: Terminology and Definitions 
Robotics is the science of creating, designing, and using robots. Robots are real-world machines 
programmed to do things on their own or with a bit of human help. It is like a tech dance between 
computers and machines. Picture giving everyday things the superpower to move and do stuff all 
on their own. That's the magic of robotics! 

Ever seen a Roomba zooming around your house? That's a robot! Those drones doing tricks in 
the sky? Also, part of the robot family. Whether they're cleaning up or exploring new places, 
robots are the tech wizards making things happen. 
Robotics and automated systems are two closely related yet distinct fields that are transforming 
industries and daily life. Robotics involves the creation of physical machines equipped with 
sensors, actuators, and intelligent software to perform tasks autonomously or with human 
guidance. These robots can range from simple industrial arms to complex humanoid machines. 
Automated systems, on the other hand, encompass a broader spectrum of technologies that 
operate with minimal human intervention. These systems can be mechanical, digital, or a 
combination of both, and they are designed to carry out specific tasks efficiently and reliably. 
While both fields share the goal of increasing efficiency and productivity, they differ in the level 
of autonomy and decision-making capabilities. Robots, particularly advanced ones, possess a 
higher degree of autonomy, allowing them to adapt to changing environments and make real-
time decisions. Automated systems, while capable of executing tasks independently, often rely 
on predefined instructions and human oversight for complex operations.
