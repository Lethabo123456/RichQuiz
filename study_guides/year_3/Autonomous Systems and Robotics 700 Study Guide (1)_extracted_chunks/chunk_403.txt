Environment Design 

States: 
Let’s define three battery levels: 
• 0 = low 
• 1 = medium 
• 2 = high 

Actions: 
• 0 = deliver 
• 1 = recharge 

Rewards: 

Battery Level    Action     Reward    Explanation 
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Low              deliver    -10       Dangerous (risk of shutdown) 
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Low              recharge   +5        Safe decision 
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Battery Level    Action     Reward    Explanation 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medium           deliver    +10       Acceptable 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medium           recharge   0         Wasteful recharge 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
High             deliver    +15       Efficient and preferred 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
High             recharge   -5        Wasteful (battery already full) 
------------------------------------------------------------------------


Transition Dynamics: 
• If action = recharge, battery increases 
• If action = deliver, battery decreases 
• If already at min/max, battery stays at limit
