The Real-World Expansion 
Now, let's make this realistic for an actual delivery robot fleet: 
1. Battery Precision Matters 
• Problem: A robot at 31% battery might complete a delivery, but at 29% it might fail 
• Q-Learning Issue: Both are just "Medium" - can't distinguish 
• Need: Track exact battery percentage (0-100%) 

Neural Network Solution: Instead of storing values for every possible state, DQN: 
• Takes continuous inputs: [battery%, temperature, distance, weight, hour] 
• Processes through neural network layers 
• Outputs Q-values for each action 
• Learns patterns: "Low battery + long distance = bad" applies to all similar situations
