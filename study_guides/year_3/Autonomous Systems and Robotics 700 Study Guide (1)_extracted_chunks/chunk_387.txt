6 Machine Learning in Robotics 
 
In modern robotics, machines are no longer limited to fixed, pre-programmed instructions. 
Instead, they must operate in uncertain, dynamic environments where adaptability is critical. 
This is where machine learning (ML) plays a transformative role. 
Machine learning empowers robots to: 

• Learn from data rather than relying solely on human-coded logic. 
• Adapt to new environments, tasks, and sensory inputs over time. 
• Improve decision-making through experience, not just rules. 
• Handle uncertainty, such as noisy sensor data or unpredictable objects. 

According to Russell & Norvig (2021), learning systems extend the reach of the designer by 
allowing robots to operate effectively in unknown or unstructured environments. 
Without ML, robots are rigid. With ML, they become intelligent agents capable of learning, 
generalising, and evolving — essential qualities for autonomy in fields such as self-driving cars, 
delivery drones, and industrial automation.
