Explanation: 
1. Camera Branch (CNN) 
Input: RGB image (shape: 3×64×64) 
Layers: 
• Convolutional layers to extract spatial features 
• ReLU activations to introduce non-linearity 
• Flattened output 
Purpose: Recognizes patterns, edges, obstacles, terrain. 

2. LIDAR Branch (FC Layer) 
Input: 1D array of distance readings (e.g., 64 points) 
Layers: 
• Fully connected layer with ReLU 
Purpose: Understands the shape and spacing of nearby obstacles at medium range. 

3. Ultrasonic Branch (FC Layer) 
Input: 4 distance readings (front, back, left, right) 
Layers: 
• Fully connected layer with ReLU 
Purpose: Detects very close obstacles like walls or furniture. 

4. IMU Branch (FC Layer) 
Input: 6-axis motion data (accelerometer + gyroscope) 
Layers: 
• Fully connected layer with ReLU 
Purpose: Understands the robot’s movement or orientation (e.g., tilting, acceleration). 

5. Fusion Layer 
• All outputs from the four branches are concatenated into one vector. 
• Passed through two fully connected layers: 
o First with 128 units and ReLU 
o Final layer with 4 units (one per possible action) 
Purpose: Combines all sensor insights to decide an action like: 
• Move forward 
• Turn left 
• Turn right 
• Stop 

6. Softmax Output 
• Converts the final scores into probabilities over the four actions 
• Ensures that the output reflects a confident, normalized decision
