1.4.1.4 The future of robotics: Artificial intelligence in Robotics 

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into robotics has significantly expanded the 
capabilities of robots. No longer confined to pre-programmed tasks, robots can now learn, adapt, 
and make decisions autonomously. AI-powered robots are being utilized across various industries, 
from autonomous vehicles to customer service, where they interact with humans in natural 
language and navigate complex environments. 

This fusion of AI and robotics has opened up new possibilities, particularly in areas that demand advanced 
decision-making and adaptability. For example, in the industrial sector, autonomous robots like Keyper can 
conduct inspections and maintenance rounds without human intervention. Using AI, Keyper can recognise its 
surroundings, determine the quickest routes, avoid obstacles, and trigger alerts when it detects anomalies 
such as equipment malfunctions, temperature spikes, or safety hazards. This not only increases operational 
efficiency but also enhances workplace safety by reducing the need for human workers to operate in potentially 
dangerous environments.
