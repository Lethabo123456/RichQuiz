Explanation:  
a) The VisualNavigationCNN(nn.Module) class defines a neural network model that takes 
an RGB image from a robot camera and outputs a probability distribution over possible 
navigation actions, such as:
 
• Move forward 
• Turn left 
• Turn right 
• Stop 

b) Convolutional Layers (Feature Extraction): These layers extract useful features from the 
raw image. They progressively reduce spatial dimensions while increasing feature 
complexity. Typical use in obstacle detection:  
• identify shapes 
• edges 
• movement in the camera frame. 

c) Fully Connected Layers (Decision Making):  After flattening the extracted features, the 
model passes them through these layers to decide what action to take. num_actions=4 
corresponds to the robot’s possible directions: [forward, left, right, stop] 
d) Output: The model returns a probability distribution over the 4 actions (e.g., 80% chance 
of moving forward, 5% chance of stopping, etc.). 
In summary the model: 
• Takes a live image from the robot camera 
• Processes it using convolutional layers to extract visual information 
• Uses this information to choose a safe and efficient movement direction, based on learned 
patterns (like avoiding walls or recognizing open paths)
