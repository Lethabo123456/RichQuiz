CHAPTER 1: INSTALLING CABLING 
 
1.1 Understand Cable Safety Measures 
 
When installing cable and wireless networking, safety is a crucial component to prevent 
electrical shock or equipment damage. All levels of technicians should know the hazards 
before working with network cable and equipment. Warning: When handling cable, always 
wear eye protection. Never touch the ends of any type of cable with bare skin. One needs to 
ensure all devices are switched off from power. Copper cables can also be dangerous to 
handle. When you cut copper cable, the small copper strands can puncture your skin or cut 
you. The small pieces that come off after cutting cables often fly into the air. Remember to 
always wear safety glasses when cutting any type of cable.
