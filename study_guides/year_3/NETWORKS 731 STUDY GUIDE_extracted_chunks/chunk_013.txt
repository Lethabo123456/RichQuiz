A cabling installation involves five steps: 

1.2.1 Design the cabling system 
If you were to tour hundreds of data centers and equipment rooms at established enterprises, 
you would see similar cabling arrangements. That’s because most organizations follow a 
cabling standard. One popular standard is TIA/EIA’s Commercial Building Wiring Standard, also 
known as structured cabling, for uniform, enterprise-wide, multivendor cabling systems. The 
standard suggests how networking media can best be installed to maximize performance and 
minimize upkeep.

1.2.2 Schedule the installation 
After abiding by the Structured cabling standardization, engineers should know approximately 
that the installation will last for 15-25 years if properly installed and maintained according to 
TIA/EIA standards. This substantial lifespan is achievable when proper installation practices 
are followed, including using certified components, maintaining appropriate cable distances, 
proper termination techniques, and adhering to industry standards for environmental 
conditions
