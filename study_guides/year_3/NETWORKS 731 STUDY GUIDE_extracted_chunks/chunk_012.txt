1.2 Demonstrate Cable Installation Procedures  
 
There is a saying that any job worth doing is worth doing correctly. This adage is particularly 
relevant while installing cabling because poor quality work might result in issues with data 
transfer, which will reduce the network's effective throughput. You will typically have to redo 
some or all the cabling work if you attempt to rush it to meet a deadline. A network will work 
best if it is designed to meet the needs of your customer. This can be achieved through 
analyzing the environment, understanding network options by interviewing the customer and 
the people involved, listing hardware and software to be used, and considering future growth 
of the company and the network. Figure 1.2 represents a network diagram from a small 
company Legends enterprise.

A structured cabling installation project typically takes 6-7 weeks to complete. The timeline 
includes planning and site surveys (week 1), initial setup (week 2), cable installation (weeks 3-
4), termination work (week 5), testing and certification (week 6), and final inspection and sign-
off (week 7). The exact duration may vary based on project scope and site conditions.
