Fiber cables are more secure from a security standpoint because they are harder to tap and 
don't release electromagnetic radiation. Their dependability is further increased by their 
resistance to weather and lack of grounding requirements. Fiber optic infrastructure has 
outstanding future-proofing capabilities for supporting new technologies and offering a longer 
service life with more room for capacity increase. 
 
The characteristics of various media make them more appropriate for a given kind of 
installation. For instance, due to their affordability and ease of installation, certain copper 
media types may be a superior option for a straightforward, low-budget installation.

designate different throughput amounts. For example, the prefix kilo combined with the word 
bit (as in kilobit) indicates 1000 bits per second Table 1.4 summarizes the terminology and
