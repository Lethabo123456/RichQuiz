1.2.3. Install the cables 
The first step in a structured cabling installation procedure is to establish conduits and cable 
paths, making sure that the right support and routing systems are in place. Pulling cables to 
telecommunications rooms starts in the main distribution region and travels via backbone 
pathways. After that, horizontal cables are drawn from telecoms rooms to specific work areas 
while avoiding possible interference sources and preserving the appropriate bend radius. 
After using the proper hardware and connectors to terminate cables at both ends, all cable 
lines and connecting points are systematically labelled. 

1.2.4. Terminate the cables 
A connector allows the devices that will be using the cabling system to access it. The quality 
of the termination has a significant impact on the quality of the signal being broadcast, this is 
the area of cable system installation that demands the most thorough attention to detail. An 
installation that does not enable higher-speed technologies will result from careless 
termination.

Fiber Optic Cable  
A cable type which is very common and used quite often. It is used to communicate between 
network devices. They are different from those wires that uses copper as they send electrical 
signals. However, Fiber uses light, LED and lasers to send protons from one side of a cable to
