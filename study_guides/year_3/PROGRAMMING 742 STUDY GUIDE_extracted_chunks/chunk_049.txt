2.1 Inheritance 
In C#, the specialization relationship is typically implemented using inheritance. This is not the 
only way to implement specialization, but it is the most common and most natural way to 
implement this relationship. 
Saying that ListBox inherits from (or derives from) Window indicates that it specializes Win-
dow. Window is referred to as the base class, and ListBox is referred to as the derived class. 
That is, Li s tBox derives its characteristics and behaviors from Window and then specializes 
to its own particular needs.
