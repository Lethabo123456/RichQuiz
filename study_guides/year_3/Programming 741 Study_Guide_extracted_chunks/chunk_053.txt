In Example 1-7, the first if statement tests whether valueOne is greater than valueTwo. The 
relational operators such as greater than (>), less than (<), and equal to (==) are fairly intuitive 
to use. The test of whether valueOne is greater than valueTwo evaluates false (because 
valueOne is 10 and valueTwo is 20, so valueOne is not greater than valueTwo). The else 
statement is invoked, printing the statement: 
ValueTwo: 20 is larger than ValueOne: 10 
The second if statement evaluates true and all the statements in the if block are evaluated,
