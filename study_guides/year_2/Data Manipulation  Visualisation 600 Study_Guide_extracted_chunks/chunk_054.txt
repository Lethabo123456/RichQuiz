3.6 Computation on NumPy Arrays 
3.6.1 Element-wise Operations: 
Element-wise operations in NumPy refer to operations that are applied individually to each element in an array. These 
operations allow you to perform mathematical operations, functions, or comparisons on each pair of corresponding 
elements in two or more arrays, without the need for explicit loops. Element-wise operations take advantage of the 
ability of NumPy to broadcast arrays of different shapes when necessary. Broadcasting is discussed in 3.7. 

3.6.2 Aggregation Functions 
Aggregate functions in NumPy are functions that perform a computation over the entire array or along a specified 
axis. These functions allow you to summarize or aggregate information from the elements of an array. Some common 
aggregate functions in NumPy include sum, mean, minimum, maximum, standard deviation, and variance. 

3.6.3 Statistical Functions 
Statistical functions in NumPy are functions that provide insights into the statistical properties of an array. They allow 
you to compute various statistical measures such as mean, median, standard deviation, variance, and percentiles. 
These functions are essential for analyzing data and understanding the distribution of values within an array.
