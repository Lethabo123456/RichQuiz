These components leverage the popular Bootstrap CSS framework, enabling developers to create responsive 
and attractive designs effortlessly. With dbc.Container, dbc.Row, dbc.Col, and more, developers can craft 
layouts that adapt seamlessly to various screen sizes and devices. Central to the interactivity of Dash 
applications are callbacks. Dash callbacks are functions that automatically execute in response to user input,
