6.2 Why Plotly 
Plotly is a popular choice for data visualization in Python for several reasons. Interactivity: Plotly allows you to create 
interactive visualizations, enabling users to zoom, pan, and hover over data points to explore details. This interactive 
nature is particularly useful for conveying complex information or allowing users to interact with the data directly. 
Versatility: Plotly supports a wide variety of chart types, including scatter plots, line charts, bar charts, pie charts, 
bubble charts, 3D plots, and more. This versatility makes it suitable for a wide range of data visualization needs. Web-
