“author” = > “Dickens”, 
“year” = > 1853 );  
  
// Displays “Array ( [author] = > Dickens [title] = > Bleak House [year] = > 1853 )” 
ksort( $myBook );  
print_r( $myBook );

krsort( $myBook );

In this example, ksort() has sorted the array by key in ascending order ( “ author ”, “ title ”, “ year “ ), 
whereas krsort() has sorted by key in the opposite order.
