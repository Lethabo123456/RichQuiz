< ?php  
$widgetsLeft = 10; while ( 
$widgetsLeft > 0 ) { echo 
“Selling a widget... “;  
$widgetsLeft - - ;  
echo “done. There are $widgetsLeft widgets left. < br / > ”;  
}  
echo “We ’ re right out of widgets!”;  
? >  
  
First a variable, $widgetsLeft , is created to record the number of widgets in stock ( 10 ). Then the 
while loop works through the widgets, “ selling ” them one at a time (represented by decrementing 
the $widgetsLeft counter) and displaying the number of widgets remaining. Once $widgetsLeft 
reaches 0 , the expression inside the parentheses ( $widgetsLeft > 0 ) becomes false , and the loop 
exits. Control is then passed to the echo() statement outside the loop, and the message “ We ’ re 
right out of widgets! ”
