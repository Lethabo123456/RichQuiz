4.18 Making A Connection   
To make a connection to a MySQL database in your PHP script, all you need to do is create a new  
PDO object. When you create the object, you pass in three arguments: the DSN, which describes the 
database to connect to; the username of the user you want to connect as; and the user’s password. 
The returned PDO object serves as your script’s connection to the database:   
   
$conn = new PDO( $dsn, $username, $password );
