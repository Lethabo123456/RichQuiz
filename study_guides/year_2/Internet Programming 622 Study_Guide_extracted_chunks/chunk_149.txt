RDBMSs are often expensive and complex to set up and administer. The widely acknowledged big 
three   
in this field are Oracle, DB2 (from IBM), and SQL Server (from Microsoft). All three are massive,  
feature-rich systems, seemingly capable of just about any kind of data storage and processing that a 
modern business could need. The flip side of the coin is that these systems are big and expensive, 
and may contain more functionality than you will ever require. Fortunately, alternatives are 
available, such as PostgreSQL and MySQL, which are both open source relational database systems 
that have proven very popular with PHP developers for many years. They are fast, stable, easily meet 
the needs of most small-to-medium sized projects, and, to top it all off, they are free!
