3.1 Understanding Files And Directories   
Everything on your hard drive is stored as a file of one kind or another, although most folks think in 
terms of files and directories. There are ordinary program files, data files, files that are directories, 
and special files that help the hard drive keep track of the contents of folders and files. PHP has 
functions that can work with any file type, but typically you will be working with text files that 
contain data.
