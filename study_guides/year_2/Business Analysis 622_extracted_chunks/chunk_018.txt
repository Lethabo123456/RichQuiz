the appropriate degree is a skill found in experienced business analysts. You must decide

Model requirements Models are a simplified representation or diagram of something real. 
They can be textual, graphical, or a combination of both. The phrase “a picture is worth a 
thousand words” is certainly true when it comes to analyzing and documenting your 
requirements. Many analysts find using models—formal or informal—to be helpful during 
their requirements elicitation efforts, as well as during requirements analysis.
