1.7Analyze Potential Value and Recommend Solution 
The final task in the Requirements Analysis and Design definition knowledge area is 
estimating and modelling the potential value of each design option and figuring out which 
option is the most appropriate option for the enterprise. Potential value is typically analyzed 
many times over the course of a change. Remember the “do nothing” option may also be the 
best recommendation based upon what you know.
