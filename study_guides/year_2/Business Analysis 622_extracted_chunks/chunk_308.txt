Business analysts must also assess the risks and uncertainties relative to the change scope and 
desired future state. Risks and potential benefits should be identified and defined. Parameters 
for variance in known processes and operations should be established. There may also be 
external vendor risks present. Don’t forget to consider the systems and any technical risks. 
Solution scalability should be evaluated along with any additional process or system changes 
that may be required.
