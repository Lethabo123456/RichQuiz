Recommended Technique: Vendor Assessment 
Vendor assessments allow you to assess an external vendor’s ability to provide all or part of 
your solution. You look at their technical ability, financial stability, staff skills, and 
reputation in their workspace. External vendors are often involved in the design, construction, 
implementation, and maintenance activities on our projects. 
Here is a quick list of things you may want to consider: 
§ 
Knowledge and expertise 
§ 
Experience, reputation, and stability 
§ 
Licensing and pricing models 
§ 
Product reputation and market position 
§ 
Contractual terms and conditions 
Vendor assessments are used to ensure that your vendors are reliable and that they will be 
able to meet your expectations.
