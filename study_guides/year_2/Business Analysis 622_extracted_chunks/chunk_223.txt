Solution Knowledge

within the organization. This allows them to effectively identify, assess, and implement 
changes to those solutions. These changes can range from simple alterations to complex 
replacement projects. When change is requested, your role is to make certain the request is 
accompanied by a cost-benefit analysis so the change request can be justified by the business
