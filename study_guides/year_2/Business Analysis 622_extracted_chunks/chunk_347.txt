Analysis Position Business analysts on BPM projects may assume many roles. 
Process architects find themselves modelling, analyzing, deploying, monitoring, and 
continuously improving business processes using a technology-based BPM platform. Process 
analysts or designers are the experts in documenting and understanding process design and 
performance trends using various BPM frameworks. Process modellers capture and document 
as-is and to-be business processes for implementation or support by an IT system.
