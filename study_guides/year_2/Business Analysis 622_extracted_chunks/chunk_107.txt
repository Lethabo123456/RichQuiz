Assess Design Options and Recommending Solution Each design option is assessed based 
on its potential value. Understanding the cost and effort required for each solution component 
is an essential step in evaluating and recommending what should be done. 
The BABOK® Guide recommends taking three factors into account during this process: 
available resources, solution constraints, and requirements dependencies. 
Table 1.21 takes a closer look at each factor.
