Even if you are on the right track, you will get run over if you just sit there.

The knowledge and skills found in the underlying competencies are used by business analysts 
across the project life cycle as well as in their day-to-day work that is not related to a specific 
project. You might find yourself selecting a project approach by facilitating a meeting of key 
stakeholders and reaching agreement on the best way to get the project underway and 
completed. You might be called on to resolve a conflict between two opposing stakeholder 
groups about the relative priorities of solution requirements. You might build and deliver 
training on new solution capabilities to your end users.
