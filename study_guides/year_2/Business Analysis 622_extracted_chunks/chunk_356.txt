The business analyst looks at changing and improving an existing process as they gather 
business analysis information. Managing stakeholders relative to the resulting changes is 
another aspect of the business analyst’s job during elicitation. 
Requirements Life Cycle Management BPM and the requirements life cycle may come into 
conflict during a BPM project. BPM looks at ways to deliver value in a process-focused way, 
while the generic requirements life cycle is a bit more linear and predictable in nature. 
Rework, revision, and new requirements can result from BPM project activities. Business 
processes should be documented so all stakeholders can review and understand them.
