2.6 Techniques to Consider 
 
The BABOK® Guide recommends the use of one or more techniques when you are assessing 
solution limitations. These techniques are summarized for you here: 
Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria These criteria define the acceptable solution 
performance. The degree of variance in the collected data is used in analyzing the solution 
performance and assessing solution limitations. 
Benchmarking and Market Analysis Benchmarking can be used to observe the results of 
other organizations using similar solutions. This includes looking at risks, trends, and 
variances relative to the solution being analyzed.
