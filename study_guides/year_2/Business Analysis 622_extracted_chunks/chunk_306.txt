Elicitation and Collaboration Eliciting business analysis information on IT projects 
involves both the business and the technical stakeholders for the project. Scheduling a 
workshop or group session with the technical and business stakeholders can help identify 
additional solution impacts on technology as well as on the business side of things. Effective 
business analysts facilitate collaboration between the business and technical staff members on 
their projects. 
Eliciting requirements on IT projects can require the business analyst to work across 
organizational boundaries. This adds risk to the effort by potentially causing communication 
breakdowns and rework. It is essential to keep your key stakeholders engaged, informed, and 
active during elicitation.
