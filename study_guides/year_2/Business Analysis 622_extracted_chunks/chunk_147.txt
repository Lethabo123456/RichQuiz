When analyzing performance measures, you can select from many recommended techniques. 
You can use one or several of these techniques to produce the solution performance analysis 
that is generated as an output by this task. 
 
2.4 Techniques to Consider

are analyzing performance measures. They are summarized for you here: 
Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria These criteria define the acceptable solution 
performance. The degree of variance in the collected data is used in analyzing the solution 
performance.
