2.1 Solution Evaluation 
The Solution Evaluation knowledge area focuses on ensuring that the solution can be 
successfully implemented within the organization in order to meet the business need driving 
your project. You must have knowledge of your business environment and be able to assess 
how each of your project’s proposed solutions would affect that environment.
