Investigating Solution Problems Business analysts often use problem analysis techniques to 
investigate solution performance problems. It is essential to identify the source of the 
problem so the problem can be addressed and corrected. Problems may be indicated by an 
inability to meet a goal or objective or by a failure to realize a benefit. Solution outputs may 
not be of sufficient quality, or potential value may not be realized.
