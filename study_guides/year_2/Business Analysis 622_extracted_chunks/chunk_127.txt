These tasks focus on making sure you select the best solution to meet your project’s business 
need and that you define, design, and implement that solution well. Your ultimate goal is to 
deploy the right solution that meets the business need and adds value to the business. You 
know your business environment and can assess how each proposed solution would affect 
that environment. You must also ensure that your project stakeholders fully understand the 
prioritized and approved solution requirements. Any implementation decisions that are being 
made during Solution Evaluation should align with those requirements.
