Recommended Technique: Data Modelling 
Data models visually represent the people, places, things, and concepts that are important to 
the business. The two most common types of data models are the ERD and the class diagram. 
ERDs are often used for projects where a relational database is part of the solution, while 
class diagrams are a better fit for object-oriented software development efforts.
