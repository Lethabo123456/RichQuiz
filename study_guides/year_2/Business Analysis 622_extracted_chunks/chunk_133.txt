Let’s take a closer look at each of these task elements now: 
Defining Solution Performance Measures The first step in defining solution performance 
measures is to take a look at any existing performance measures and methods. Make sure the 
existing measures are both accurate and relevant to the new solution. Common sources for 
performance measures include business goals, objectives, and processes as well as any legal 
or regulatory constraints on the solution. 
Validating Solution Performance Measures Solution performance measures should be 
validated with key stakeholders to ensure the measures are useful and relevant. More detailed 
performance measures should align with the higher-level performance measures for the 
solution or for the enterprise.
