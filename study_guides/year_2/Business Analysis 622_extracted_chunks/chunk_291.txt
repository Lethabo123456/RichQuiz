Requirements Analysis and Design Definition Business analysts use data-oriented 
modelling techniques when specifying the data requirements for a business intelligence 
solution. Techniques include data modelling, data dictionaries, decision modelling, and 
business rules analysis. The first step is modelling the existing system’s data so you can 
define availability, identify redundancies, and discover any data quality issues. Sometimes 
this requires reverse engineering if no documentation exists.
