.Understanding How This Applies to Your Projects 
Adjusting what you do and how you do it based upon the type of project you are working on 
is an essential skill for successful business analysts. Your ability to recognize your project’s 
context and use the correct tasks, techniques, methods, and tools will help keep your business 
analysis efforts on target and in sync. The project types or perspectives in the BABOK® 
Guide are a great starting point: agile, business intelligence, information technology, business 
architecture, and business process management.
