Define Measurable Evaluation Criteria One item of interest found in your business case is 
the business benefit that the project will provide to the organization once the project is 
complete and the solution is up-and-running. Business benefits can be measured only if you 
define the measurement criteria and how you will evaluate whether you have achieved them. 
If you are lucky, they were defined in the business case. If not, you will need to define them 
now during requirements validation.
