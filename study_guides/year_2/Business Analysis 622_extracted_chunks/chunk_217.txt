 Organization and Time Management 
Experienced business analysts can quickly locate needed information and use work time 
efficiently. Your ability to effectively manage your time, tasks, and information has an 
impact on how your team members and other stakeholders perceive you. A disorganized 
business analyst is not viewed as an effective business analyst. You should set your work 
priorities, be clear about what needs to be done, and get that work done quickly and well.
