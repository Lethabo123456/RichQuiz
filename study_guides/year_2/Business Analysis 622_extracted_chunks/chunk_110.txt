Estimation Estimating techniques are used to forecast the cost and effort of meeting

Financial Analysis There are many financial techniques business analysts can use to evaluate 
the financial return of different design options and choose the best possible return on 
investment (ROI). 
Focus Groups Focus groups allow you to get stakeholder input on which design options best 
meet the requirements and to understand the stakeholder’s value expectations relative to the 
solution. 
Interviews Interviews are a face-to-face way to get stakeholder input on which design 
options best meet the requirements and to understand the stakeholder’s value expectations of 
the solution.
