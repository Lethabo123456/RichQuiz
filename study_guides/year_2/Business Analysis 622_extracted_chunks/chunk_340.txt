Change Scope 
 
Business analysts may find themselves working on a single process or all of the processes 
within an organization. The business analyst’s focus in all cases is to change processes in 
order to improve and meet an organization’s objectives. Table 5.6 contains the four activities 
found in a business process management life cycle.
