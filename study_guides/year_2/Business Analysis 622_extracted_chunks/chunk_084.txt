Produce the Requirements Architecture 
The requirements architecture defines an organized structure for stakeholder and solution 
requirements and the documented relationships between them. The requirements structure 
defines the scope of each specific model or set of requirements, and provides a location 
where each specific requirement can be found. The requirements structure is not the same as
