Solution Evaluation Many business intelligence solutions are implemented and then not 
used to the full extent of their capabilities. Many stakeholders use the solution the “old way” 
and don’t even try the new capabilities that are present. Business analysts need to be aware of 
this underutilization and encourage stakeholders to use the solution to the fullest. Training 
and a good set of user documentation may be helpful in this regard.
