Predictive methodologies have structured processes that emphasize planning and formal 
documentation. Each phase of a predictive project must be completed before the next phase 
can begin. Adaptive processes are more iterative and incremental in nature. They allow for 
rework and greater flexibility across the project life cycle. Some organizations use a blend, or 
hybrid, of these two solution development methodologies.
