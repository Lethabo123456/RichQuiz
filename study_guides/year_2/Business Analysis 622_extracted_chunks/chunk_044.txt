Recommended Technique: User Stories 
User stories are commonly used for change-driven requirements development. They briefly 
describe the functionality your solution provides to its users. You should define acceptance 
and evaluation criteria for each user story so you can prove that its functionality has been met 
by the solution. Business analysts write user stories from the user’s point of view. User 
stories contain the following: 
§ 
The actor or stakeholder benefiting from the user story 
§ 
A high-level description of the functionality in the story 
§ 
The business benefits that the story delivers
