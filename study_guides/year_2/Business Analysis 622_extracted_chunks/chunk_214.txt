Ethics 
To be respected and trusted by your team and your stakeholders, you must behave ethically. 
That’s easy enough to say, but what exactly does it mean? Ethical people know the difference 
between moral and immoral behaviour and understand the standards that govern their own 
behaviour. They act in a moral way to meet those behavioural standards.
