Visual Thinking 
Effective communication is often aided by connecting the content receiver with as many 
different touch points as possible. You have no doubt heard the saying “A picture is worth a 
thousand words.” Visual thinking allows a business analyst to communicate complex ideas 
through visual representation, such as models, graphics, diagrams, and illustrations. Some 
stakeholders may prefer nonvisual and descriptions; others may really grasp the ideas you are 
trying to convey once they see the visual. Both together will give the greatest opportunity to 
convey your concepts in a way that both right-brain and left-brain thinkers will be able to 
understand.
