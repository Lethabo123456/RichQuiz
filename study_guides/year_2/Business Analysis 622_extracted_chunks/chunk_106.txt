Expected Costs Expected costs are the “flip side” of expected benefits. They are the negative 
value associated with a solution, such as the cost to acquire, build, or maintain that solution. 
Expected, or negative, costs include many things, such as resources, operating costs, purchase 
costs, and maintenance costs. 
Determine Value The potential value of a solution equals the sum of the expected benefits 
and the associated costs of those benefits. A positive value means that the benefits exceed the 
costs, while a negative value means that the costs exceed the benefits. The value should be 
determined at the enterprise level versus for a single group of stakeholders. This potential 
value is uncertain because of the tangible and intangible costs that are part of the estimating 
process.
