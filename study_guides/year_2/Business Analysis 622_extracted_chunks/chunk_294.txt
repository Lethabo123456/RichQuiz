Business analysts working on IT projects do their work while keeping an eye on three factors. 
The first factor to watch is the solution’s impact, which includes the value and risk of the 
solution to the business. The second factor consists of the maturity, formality, and flexibility 
of your organization’s change processes. The third factor is the scope of the proposed change 
that is being addressed.
