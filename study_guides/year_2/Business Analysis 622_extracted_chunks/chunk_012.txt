The tasks in the Requirements Analysis and Design Definition knowledge area begin by 
developing the project’s stakeholder requirements and continue until the more detailed 
solution requirements are completed. Typically, defining the stakeholder and solution 
requirements on your projects takes place in the controlled middle of the project life cycle as 
part of the project’s requirements development or definition phase.
