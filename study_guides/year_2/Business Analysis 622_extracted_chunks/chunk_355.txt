Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring Planning business analysis work on BPM 
projects is an exercise in progressive elaboration. Continuous improvement activities are also 
heavily involved as part of this knowledge area. Initially, business analysts focus on 
analyzing and improving the business process before considering the technology and revised 
work procedures that are actually required to improve that process.
