Approaches and Techniques 
 
Business intelligence projects do not have any formal methodologies that impact business 
analysis work. However, there are many informal and overlapping approaches you can take 
for business intelligence projects and initiatives. Business analysis is a component in all 
business intelligence projects, although many of the approaches take an informal and flexible 
view of the business analyst role. Table 4.5lists the approaches, brief descriptions of each 
approach, and the business analyst’s focus in each area.
