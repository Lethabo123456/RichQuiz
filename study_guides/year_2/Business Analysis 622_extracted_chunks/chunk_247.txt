3.5 How This Applies to Your Projects 
Professional detachment is another essential skill for business analysts. Your ability to remain 
in control of yourself will be tested regularly when people become angry or frustrated when 
the way things are done changes, when people don’t get their way, or when your fellow team 
members and stakeholders have straightforward personality clashes. Being self-aware and 
regulating your own emotional responses in such situations will serve you well.
