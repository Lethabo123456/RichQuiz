Achievement Theory People are motivated by a need for achievement, power, and 
affiliation. Teams populated by achievement-motivated team members are a dream come 
true. Many folks work hard and motivate themselves because it is important to them that they 
do a good job. Other folks may be motivated by more tangible things, such as compensation, 
bonuses, and possibilities of advancement.
