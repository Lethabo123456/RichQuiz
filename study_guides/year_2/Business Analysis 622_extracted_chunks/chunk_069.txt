2 Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria Acceptance criteria are the quality metrics that 
must be met by a requirement, a solution component, or the solution itself in order to be 
accepted by the project stakeholders. Evaluation criteria are used to measure how 
successful a deployed or operational solution is in providing business benefits. 
3 Document Analysis Document analysis is used during requirements validation activities 
to identify previously documented business needs. 
4 Financial Analysis This technique allows the business analyst to define and confirm the 
expected financial benefits associated with the validated requirements. 
5 Item Tracking Item tracking makes sure that any problems or issues related to validation 
are managed and resolved as part of the issue management process.
