Organization Knowledge 
Understanding your organization and how things get done enables you to get your own work 
done and make good decisions within that organization. This includes understanding how 
your organization generates their profits and how goals are communicated and accomplished. 
Your organization provides the primary context for your work efforts. This definition of 
organization includes the entire business architecture: business models, management structure 
and how that relates to the organizational structure, business unit relationships, and your key 
project stakeholders.
