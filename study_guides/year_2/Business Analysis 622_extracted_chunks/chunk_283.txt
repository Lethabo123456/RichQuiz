Underlying Competencies 
Business analysts on business intelligence projects should possess good communication skills 
and analytical competencies. These skills are essential when you find yourself coordinating 
business information requirements with business intelligence systems outcomes. Business 
analysts will find themselves collaborating with and liaising between the technical project 
team and the business stakeholders. According to the BABOK® Guide, the required
