Requirements Analysis and Design Definition This knowledge area focuses on defining the 
to-be process model on BPM projects. The requirements architecture will include the process 
model, associated business rules/decisions, information requirements, and the organizational 
structure. Solution options on BPM projects typically include any changes needed to support 
the new or revised process.
