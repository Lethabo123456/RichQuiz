Let’s take a look at the guidelines and tools that may also be used as inputs when analyzing 
solution performance: 
Change Strategy The change strategy is a high-level plan of key activities and events 
relative to a proposed change. Be sure to reference this information when analyzing solution 
performance using the defined performance measures. 
Future State Description The future state description provides a definition of the boundaries 
for the solution scope and all of the solution components. It also links back to the potential 
value expected from this future state. 
Risk Analysis Results The risk analysis results from Strategy Analysis provide a look at 
both the overall level of solution risk and the more specific individual risks inherent to the 
solution and its components.
