When Does Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring Take Place? 
The tasks in the Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring knowledge area occur throughout 
the project life cycle. Many of these tasks are done as a part of pre-project activities as the basis 
of a project’s controlled start. The business analysis deliverables created at the beginning of a 
project are used to define, govern, and monitor the performance of all other business analysis 
tasks across the project life cycle. The plans and approaches developed for the overall project 
may require updates and additional details as each subsequent phase of the project life cycle is 
planned.
