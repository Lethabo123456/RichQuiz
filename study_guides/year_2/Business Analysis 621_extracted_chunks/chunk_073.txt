Once the initial stakeholder list, map, or persona information is complete, you use it as an input 
for a number of other business analysis tasks summarized in Table 2.7. They include tasks from 
a number of knowledge areas such as Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring, Elicitation 
and Collaboration, and Requirements Analysis and Design Definition. 
A number of stakeholders are involved with conducting stakeholder analysis for the business 
analysis activities of a project. Remember that the business analyst shares responsibility for 
analyzing business analysis stakeholders with the project manager. This means that any 
stakeholder analysis results should align with the project stakeholder analysis results in both 
structure and content.
