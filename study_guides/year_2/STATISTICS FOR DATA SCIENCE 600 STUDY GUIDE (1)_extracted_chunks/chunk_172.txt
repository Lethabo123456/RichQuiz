Chapter 5: Inferential Statistics 
 
5.1 Introduction  
We are concerned with making inferences about entire populations based on certain samples 
of data. We will be using hypothesis tests along with different estimation tests to gain a better 
understanding of populations, given samples of data. 

5.2 Point Estimates  
Recall that it is difficult it was to obtain a population parameter; so, we use sample data to 
calculate a statistic that is an estimate of a parameter. When we make these estimates, we 
call them point estimates.
