Correlation vs. Causation: 
• Correlation: Measures how two variables move together, quantified between -1 and 
1. 
• Causation: Indicates that one variable directly affects another. 
• The chapter provides an example showing a strong negative correlation between 
hours of TV watched and work performance, cautioning that correlation does not 
imply causation. 
  
Linear and Multiple Regression: 
Linear Regression: 
o Models the relationship between a dependent variable and a single 
independent variable using a straight line. 
o An example is predicting house prices based on their size. 
  
Multiple Regression: 
o Models the relationship between a dependent variable and multiple 
independent variables. 
o An example is predicting house prices based on size, number of bedrooms, and 
age.
