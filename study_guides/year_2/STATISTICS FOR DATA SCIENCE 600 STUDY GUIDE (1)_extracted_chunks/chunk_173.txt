point estimates to estimate population means, variances, and other statistics. To obtain these 
estimates, we simply apply the function that we wish to measure for our population to a 
sample of the data. For example, suppose there is a company of 9,000 employees and we are 
interested in ascertaining the average length of breaks taken by employees in a single day. As 
we probably cannot ask every single person, we will take a sample of the 9,000 people and 
take a mean of the sample. This sample mean will be our point estimate.
