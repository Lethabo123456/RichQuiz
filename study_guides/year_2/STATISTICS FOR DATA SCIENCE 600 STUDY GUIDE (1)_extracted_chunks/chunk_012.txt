CHAPTER 1: Types of Data and the Steps of Data Science  
 
Introduction  
Understanding the Data Revolution 
In the past, the world revolved around massive machines during the Industrial Age, 
transitioning to the Information Age where small, fast machines gathered data. Now, we're 
in the Data Age, where the focus is on understanding and utilizing this abundant data. The 
sheer volume of data generated is staggering, increasing from trillions of gigabytes in the early 
21st century to an estimated 40 trillion gigabytes by 2020. This exponential growth in data 
production and consumption has ushered in the era of data science. 

1.The Evolution of Data Science 
Data science is about making sense of data. This field has evolved significantly: 
1. From ENIAC to Modern Computers: The journey started with machines like ENIAC, 
the first computer, which revolutionized data processing. 
2. The Information Age: This era saw the development of technologies like mobile 
phones and televisions, but also the challenges of managing vast amounts of data. 
3. The Data Age: We now combine the machinery of the 19th century with the data of 
the 20th century to create insights for everyone. This age has seen the rise of roles 
like chief data scientists in governments and businesses. 
Core Components of Data Science 
Data science is not just about handling data; it involves specific skills and knowledge: 
1. Basic Terminology: Understanding what data is, including organized (structured) and 
unorganized (unstructured) data. 
2. The Art and Science of Data: Data science is about using data to gain knowledge, 
make decisions, predict the future, and understand the past. 
3. Why Data Science? With an abundance of data, traditional methods of analysis are 
inadequate. Data science helps in making sense of large, complex data sets.
