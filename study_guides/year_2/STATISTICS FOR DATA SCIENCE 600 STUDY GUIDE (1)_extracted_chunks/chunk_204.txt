Chi-square Goodness of Fit Test 
The one-sample t-test was used to check whether a sample mean differed from the 
population mean. The chi-square goodness of fit test is very similar to the one sample t-test 
in that it tests whether the distribution of the sample data matches an expected distribution, 
while the big difference is that it is testing for categorical variables. 
For example, a chi-square goodness of fit test would be used to see if the race demographics 
of your company match that of the entire city or the U.S. population. It can also be used to 
see if users of your website show similar characteristics to average Internet users.
