Summation Symbol (Σ) 
The summation symbol, denoted by the uppercase Greek letter sigma (Σ), is widely used in 
data science for denoting the sum of a series of numbers. It is particularly useful in handling 
vectors and arrays, common data structures in data science. 
Definition and Usage: 
 
The symbol Σ represents the process of adding up a sequence of numbers. 
• The numbers to be summed are often expressed as a sequence or a set of numbers. 
Example with a Vector: Consider a vector X = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]. This vector represents a sequence 
of numbers. To find the sum of the numbers in this vector, we use the summation symbol as 
follows: 
• ΣX = Σ[1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
• This implies: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5
