Jaccard Measure 
• A method to quantify similarity between two sets. 
• Defined as the ratio of the magnitude of the intersection to the magnitude of the 
union of the sets. 
• Example: Calculating similarity between user1 and user2. 
def jaccard(user1, user2):  
 
return len(user1 & user2) / float(len(user1 | user2))  
 
# Jaccard similarity for user1 and user2  
 
jaccard(user1, user2) # Output: 0.2 or 1/5  
  
Linear Algebra: Matrix Multiplication and Its Applications 
Introduction to Linear Algebra Linear algebra is a branch of mathematics focusing on matrices 
and vectors. It plays a critical role in various applications, such as in machine learning, 
engineering, and data science. 

Matrix Multiplication: The Basics 
• Non-Commutative Operation: Unlike scalar numbers, the order of multiplication in 
matrices is crucial. The product of matrix A and matrix B (AB) generally differs from 
the product of BA. 
• Dimension Matching: To multiply two matrices, the number of columns in the first 
matrix must equal the number of rows in the second. For instance, a matrix of 
dimensions 3x2 can be multiplied with a 2x2 matrix. 
How to Multiply Matrices 
• The result of matrix multiplication is a new matrix whose elements are derived from 
dot products of rows and columns from the original matrices. 
• The resulting matrix's dimensions will be a combination of the outer dimensions of 
the multiplied matrices. For example, multiplying a 3x2 matrix with a 2x2 matrix yields 
a 3x2 matrix.
