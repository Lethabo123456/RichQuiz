1.2 The Data Science Venn Diagram 

Data science sits at the intersection of: 
1. Math/Statistics: Understanding equations and formulas for analysis. 
2. Computer Programming: Using code to manipulate and analyse data. 
3. Domain Knowledge: Applying data science effectively within a specific field. 
Exploring Python for Data Science

The Importance of Domain Knowledge 
Being knowledgeable in the field you're working in is crucial. This includes understanding the 
specific challenges and nuances of the field and how to present data effectively. 
The Role of Mathematics in Data Science 
Mathematics is the backbone of data science. It formalizes relationships between variables, 
allowing us to apply concepts across different domains. This chapter will cover the spectrum 
from basic algebra to advanced probabilistic and statistical modelling. 
 
Understanding Data Models 
Definition and Importance 
A data model is an organized representation of data elements, simulating real-world 
phenomena. Through math, we establish formal relationships between these elements. 
The Scope of Mathematical Concepts in Data Science
