 Key Terms  
  
1. A queue is a data structure in which the items are added at one end and removed from the other end.  
2. A queue is a First In First Out (FIFO) data structure.  
3. The basic operations on a queue are as follows: Add an item to the queue, remove an item from the 
queue, retrieve the first and last element of the queue, initialize the queue, check whether the queue 
is empty, and check whether the queue is full.
