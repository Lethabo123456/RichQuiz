In 1850, the 8-queens puzzle was considered by the great C. F. Gauss, who was unable to obtain a 
complete solution. The term backtrack was first coined by D. H. Lehmer in 1950. In 1960, R. J. Walker gave 
an algorithmic account of backtracking. A general description of backtracking with a variety of applications 
was presented by S. Golomb and L. Baumert.
