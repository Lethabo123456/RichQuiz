To design a recursive function, you must do the following:  
1. Understand the problem requirements.  
2. Determine the limiting conditions. For example, for a list, the limiting condition is the number of 
elements in the list.  
3. Identify the base cases and provide a direct solution to each base case.  
4. Identify the general cases and provide a solution to each general case in terms of smaller versions 
of itself.
