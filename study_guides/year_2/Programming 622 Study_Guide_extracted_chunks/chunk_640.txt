Suppose that L is a list of n elements, where n> 0. In the worst case, the number of key comparisons in 
heapsort to sort L (the number of comparisons in heapSort and the number of comparisons in buildHeap) 
is 2nlog2n + O(n). Also, in the worst case, the number of item assignments in heapsort to sort L is nlog2n + 
O(n). On average, the number of comparisons made by heapsort to sort L is of O(nlog2n).
