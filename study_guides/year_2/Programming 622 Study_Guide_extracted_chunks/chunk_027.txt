1.2.2  Design  
  
After you carefully analyze the problem, the next step is to design an algorithm to solve the problem. If 
you broke the problem into subproblems, you need to design an algorithm for each subproblem.  
  
Algorithm: A step-by-step problem-solving process in which a solution is arrived at in a finite amount of 
time.
