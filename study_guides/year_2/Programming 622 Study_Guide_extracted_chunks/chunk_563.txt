which slots to probe, the hash function includes the probe number as a second input.  
 
Thus,  
the  
hash  
function  
becomes   
 h: × {0, 1, . . . m -1 }--> {0, 1, . . . , m-1}  
  
and the probe sequence  
  
< h(k, 0), h(k, 1), . . . , h(k, m-1) >  
in which every slot is eventually considered.
