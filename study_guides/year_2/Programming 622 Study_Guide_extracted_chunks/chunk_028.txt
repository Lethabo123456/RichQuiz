1.2.2.2 Object-Oriented Design  
  
In object-oriented design (OOD), the first step in the problem-solving process is to identify the 
components called objects, which form the basis of the solution, and determine how these objects 
interact with one another. For example, suppose you want to write a program that automates the video 
rental process for a local video store. The two main objects in this problem are the video and the customer.
