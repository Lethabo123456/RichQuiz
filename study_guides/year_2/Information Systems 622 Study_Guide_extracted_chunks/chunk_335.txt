Interviews can either be structured or unstructured. In a structured interview, the questions are 
written in advance. In an unstructured interview, the questions are not written in advance; the 
interviewer relies on experience in asking the best questions to uncover the inherent problems 
of the existing system. An advantage of the unstructured interview is that it allows the 
interviewer to ask follow-up or clarifying questions immediately.
