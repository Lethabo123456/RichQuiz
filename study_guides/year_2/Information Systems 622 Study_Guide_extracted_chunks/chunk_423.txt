Most companies use both approaches. A billing application, for example, might be reviewed 
once a year for errors, inefficiencies and opportunities to reduce operating costs. This is a time- 
driven approach. In addition, the billing application might be redone after a corporate merger 
if one or more new managers require different information or reports, or if laws on bill 
collecting and privacy change. This is an event-driven approach.
