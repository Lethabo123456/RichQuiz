Computer use can affect physical health as well. Strains, sprains, tendonitis, tennis elbow, the 
inability to hold objects and sharp pain in the fingers can result. Also common is repetitive 
strain injury (RSI), including carpal tunnel syndrome (CTS), which is the aggravation of the 
path- way for nerves that travel through the wrist (the carpal tunnel). CTS involves wrist pain, 
a feeling of tingling and numbness, and difficulty grasping and holding objects. It can be caused 
by many factors, such as stress, lack of exercise and the repetitive motion of typing on a 
computer keyboard. Decisions on workers’ compensation related to RSI have been made both 
for and against employees.  
Avoiding Health and Environmental Problems  
Many computer-related health problems are caused by a poorly designed work environment. 
The computer screen can be hard to read, with glare and poor contrast. Desks and chairs can 
also be uncomfortable. Keyboards and computer screens might be fixed
