Data Storage  
Data storage involves updating one or more databases with new transactions. As has already 
been emphasized several times in this chapter, this data can be further processed and 
manipulated by other systems so that it is available for management reporting and decision 
making. Thus, although transaction databases can be considered a by-product of transaction 
processing, they have a pronounced effect on nearly all other information systems and decision-
making processes in an organization.
