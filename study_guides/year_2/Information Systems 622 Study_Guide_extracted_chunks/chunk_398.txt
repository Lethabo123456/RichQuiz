As with users, the eventual success of any system depends on how it is used by the IS personnel 
within the organization. Training programmes should be conducted for the IS personnel who 
will be looking after the new computer system. These programs are similar to those for the 
users, although they can be more detailed in the technical aspects of the systems. Effective 
training will help IS personnel use the new system to perform their jobs and support other users 
in the organization.
