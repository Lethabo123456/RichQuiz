‘swipes’ their card when they enter the train station and they ‘swipe’ it again when they leave. 
The correct fare is then debited from their prepaid account. ‘Swiping’ the card merely involves 
waving it near a reader – direct contact is not required. In fact the card doesn’t even have to be
