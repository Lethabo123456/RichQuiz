A use case model consists of two parts – a diagram showing each process and the ‘actors’ who 
use them. An actor is someone who gets something out of the process. Typical actors are 
customers and suppliers. ‘Buy a product’ is a typical process, or ‘Reorder stock’. The second 
part of the model is a text description of each process broken down into numbered steps.
