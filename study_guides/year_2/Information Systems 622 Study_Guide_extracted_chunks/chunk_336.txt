Direct observation requires a certain amount of skill. The observer must be able to see what is 
really happening and not be influenced by attitudes or feelings. In addition, many people don’t 
like being observed and may change their behaviour when they are. However, observation can 
reveal important problems and opportunities that would be difficult to obtain using other data 
collection methods.
