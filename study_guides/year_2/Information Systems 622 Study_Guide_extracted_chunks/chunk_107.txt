pair maps or map outlines with tabular data to describe aspects of a particular geographic 
region. For example, sales managers might want to plot total sales for each region in the 
countries they serve. Using a GIS, they can specify that each region be shaded to indicate the 
relative amount of sales – no shading or light shading represents no or little sales, and deeper 
shading represents more sales. Staples Inc., the large office supply store chain, used a
