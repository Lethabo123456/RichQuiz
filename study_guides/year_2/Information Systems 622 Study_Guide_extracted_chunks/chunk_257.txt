Many companies that already use the Internet for their private exchanges have no desire to 
share their expertise with competitors. At the US shopping giant Wal-Mart, the world’s 
number- one retail chain, executives turned down several invitations to join exchanges in the 
retail and consumer goods industries. Wal-Mart is pleased with its in-house exchange, Retail 
Link, which connects the company to 7000 worldwide suppliers that sell everything from 
toothpaste to furniture.
