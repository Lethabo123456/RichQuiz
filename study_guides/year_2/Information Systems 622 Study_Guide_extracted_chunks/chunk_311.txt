Degree of Change  
A major factor that affects the quality of systems development is the degree of change 
associated with the project. The scope can vary from implementing minor enhancements to an 
existing system, up to major reengineering. The project team needs to recognize where they are 
on this spectrum of change.  
Continuous improvement projects do not require significant busi- ness process or IS changes, 
or retraining of people; thus, they have a high degree of success. Typically, because continuous 
improvement involves minor improvements, these projects also have relatively modest 
benefits.
