ip Between Maintenance and Design  
Programs are expensive to develop, but they are even more expensive to maintain. Programs 
that are well designed and documented to be efficient, structured and flexible are less expensive 
to maintain in later years. Thus, there is a direct relationship between design and maintenance. 
More time spent on design up front can mean less time spent on maintenance later.  
In most cases, it is worth the extra time and expense to design a good system. Consider a system 
that costs €250 000 to develop. Spending 10 per cent more on design would cost an additional 
€25 000, bringing the total design cost to €275 000. Maintenance costs over the life of the 
program could be €1 000 000. If this additional design expense can reduce maintenance costs 
by 10 per cent, the savings in maintenance costs would be €100 000. Over the life of the 
program, the net savings would be €75 000 (€100 000 – €25 000).
