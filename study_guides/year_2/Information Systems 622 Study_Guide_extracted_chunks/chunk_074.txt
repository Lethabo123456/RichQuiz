SAR often requires nimble organizations that replace traditional lines of authority with those 
that are flexible and dynamic. IBM, for example, used SAR with its microelectronics division 
to help with inventory control. It used mathematical models and optimization routines to 
control inventory levels. The models sensed when a shortage of inventory for customers was 
likely and responded by backlogging and storing extra inventory to avoid the shortages. In this 
application, SAR identified potential problems and solved them before they became a reality.
