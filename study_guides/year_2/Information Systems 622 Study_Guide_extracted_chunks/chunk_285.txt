manufacturing plant, for example, might determine that minimizing the total cost of owning 
and operating its equipment is critical to meeting production and profit goals. Critical success 
factors (CSFs) are factors that are essential to the success of certain functional areas of an 
organization. The CSF for manufacturing – minimizing equipment maintenance and operating
