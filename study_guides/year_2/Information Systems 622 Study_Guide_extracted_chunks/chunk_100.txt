 Product pricing. Product pricing is another important and complex marketing function. 
Retail price, wholesale price and price discounts must be set. Most companies try to develop 
pricing policies that will maximize total sales revenues. Computers are often used to 
analyze the relationship between prices and total revenues. Some companies are using 
Internet behavioural pricing, where the price customers pay online depends on what they 
might be willing to pay based on information on past transactions and Internet searches that 
reveal individual shopping behaviours.
