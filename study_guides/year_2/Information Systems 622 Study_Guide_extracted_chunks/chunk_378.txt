The Final Evaluation  
The final evaluation begins with a detailed investigation of the proposals offered by the 
remaining vendors. The vendors should be asked to make a final presentation and to fully 
demonstrate the system. The demonstration should be as close to actual operating conditions 
as possible. Applications such as payroll, inventory control and billing should be tested using 
a large amount of test data. After the final presentations and demonstrations have been given, 
the organization makes the final evaluation and selection. Cost comparisons, hardware 
performance, delivery dates, price, flexibility, back-up facilities, availability of software 
training and maintenance factors are considered. In addition to comparing computer speeds,
