The Financial Implications of Maintenance  
The cost of maintenance is staggering. For older programs, the total cost of maintenance can 
be up to five times greater than the total cost of development. In other words, a program that 
originally cost €25 000 to develop might cost €125 000 to maintain over its lifetime. The 
average programmers can spend more than half their time on maintaining existing programs
