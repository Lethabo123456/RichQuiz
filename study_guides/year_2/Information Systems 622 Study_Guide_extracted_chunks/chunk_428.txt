Preventing Computer-Related Waste and Mistakes  
To remain profitable in a competitive environment, organizations must use all resources wisely. 
Preventing computer-related waste and mistakes like those just described should therefore be 
a goal. To achieve it involves (1) establishing, (2) implementing, (3) monitoring and (4) 
reviewing effective policies and procedures.
