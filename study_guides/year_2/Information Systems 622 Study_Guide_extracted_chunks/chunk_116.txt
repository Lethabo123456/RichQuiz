a-kind to repetitive decisions. One-of-a-kind decisions are typcally handled by an ad hoc 
DSS. An ad hoc DSS is concerned with situations or decisions that come up only a few 
times during the life of the organization; in small businesses, they might happen only once. 
For example, a company might need to change the layout of its open plan offices. Repetitive 
decisions are addressed by an institutional DSS. An institutional DSS handles situations or
