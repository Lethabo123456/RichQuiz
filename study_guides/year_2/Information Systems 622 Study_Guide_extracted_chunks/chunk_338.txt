Data Analysis  
The data collected in its raw form is usually not adequate to determine the effectiveness of the 
existing system or the requirements of the new system. The next step is to manipulate the 
collected data so that the development team members who are participating in systems analysis 
can use the data. This manipulation is called data analysis.
