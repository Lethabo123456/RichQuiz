6.1 Systems Design   
  
The purpose of systems design is to answer the question ‘how will the information system 
solve the problem?’ The primary result of the systems design phase is a technical design that 
details system inputs and the processing required to produce outputs, user interfaces, hardware, 
software, databases, telecommunications, personnel and procedures, and shows how these 
components are related. The system that is designed should meet all the requirements specified
