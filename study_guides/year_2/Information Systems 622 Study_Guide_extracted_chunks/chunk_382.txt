Freezing Design Specifications  
Near the end of the design stage, some organizations prohibit further changes in the design of 
the system. Freezing systems design specifications means that the user agrees in writing that 
the design is acceptable. Other organizations, however, allow or even encourage design 
changes. These organizations often use the rapid systems development approaches.
