Design of System Security and Controls  
  
In addition to considering the system’s interface and user interactions, designers must also 
develop system security and controls for all aspects of the system, including hardware, 
software, database systems, and telecommunications and Internet operations. These key 
considerations involve error prevention, detection and correction; system controls; and disaster 
planning and recovery.
