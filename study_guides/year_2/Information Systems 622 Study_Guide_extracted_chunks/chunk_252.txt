Retail and Wholesale  
E-commerce is being used extensively in retailing and wholesaling. Electronic retailing, 
sometimes called e-tailing, is the direct sale of products or services by businesses to consumers 
through electronic shops, which are typically designed around the familiar electronic catalogue 
and shopping cart model. Tens of thou- sands of electronic retail websites sell a wide range. In 
addition, cyber shopping centres, or ‘cybermalls’, are another means to support retail shopping. 
A cyber- mall is a single website that offers many products and services at one Internet location.
