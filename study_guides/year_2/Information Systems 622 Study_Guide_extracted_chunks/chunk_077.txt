Inputs to a Management Information System  
As shown in Figure 2.2, data that enters an MIS originates from both internal and external 
sources, including the company’s supply chain. The most significant internal data sources for 
an MIS are the organization’s various TPS and ERP systems. Companies also use data 
warehouses to store valuable business information. Other internal data comes from specific 
functional areas throughout the firm. External sources of data can include customers, suppliers,
