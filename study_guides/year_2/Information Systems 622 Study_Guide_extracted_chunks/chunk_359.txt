Logical design describes the functional requirements of a system. That is, it conceptualizes 
what the system will do to solve the problems identified through earlier analysis. Without this 
step, the technical details of the system (such as which hardware devices should be acquired) 
often obscure the best solution. Logical design involves planning the purpose of each system 
element, independent of hardware and software considerations. The logical design 
specifications that are determined and documented include output, input, process, file and 
database, telecommunications, procedures, controls and security, and personnel and job 
requirements.
