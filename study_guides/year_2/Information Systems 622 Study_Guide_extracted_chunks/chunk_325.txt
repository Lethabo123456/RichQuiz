Ideally, functional managers are heavily involved during the investigation phase. Other 
members could include users or stakeholders outside management, such as an employee who 
helps initiate systems development. The technical and financial expertise of others participating 
in investigation help the team to determine whether the problem is worth solving. The members 
of the development team who participate in investigation are then responsible for gathering and 
analyzing data, preparing a report justifying systems development and presenting the results to 
top-level managers.
