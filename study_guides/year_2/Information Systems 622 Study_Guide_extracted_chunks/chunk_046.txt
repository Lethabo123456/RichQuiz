-consumer (B2C) e-commerce   
  
A form of e-commerce in which customers deal directly with an organization and avoid 
intermediaries.   
  
B2Me e-commerce.  
  
A form of e-commerce where the business treats each customer as a separate market segment. 
Typical B2Me features include customizing a website for each customer, perhaps based on 
their previous purchases and personalized (electronic) marketing literature.
