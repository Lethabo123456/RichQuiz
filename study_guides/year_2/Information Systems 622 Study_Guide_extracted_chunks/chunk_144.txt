Defining knowledge is difficult. One definition is that knowledge is the awareness and under- 
standing of a set of information and the ways that information can be made useful to support 
a specific task or reach a decision. Knowing the procedures for ordering more inventory to 
avoid running out is an example of knowledge. In a sense, information tells you what has to be 
done (low inventory levels for some items), while knowledge tells you how to do it (make two 
important phone calls to the right people to get the needed inventory shipped overnight). A 
knowledge management system (KMS) is an organized collection of people, procedures,
