In contrast, some companies use a continuous improvement approach to systems development. 
With this approach, an organization makes changes to a system even when small problems or 
opportunities occur. Although continuous improvement can keep the system current and 
responsive, repeatedly designing and implementing changes can be both time consuming and 
expensive.  
  
A time-driven review is performed after a specified amount of time.

existing system is monitored on a schedule. If problems or opportunities are uncovered, a new
