Key-Indicator Reports  
A key-indicator report summarizes the previous day’s critical activities and is typically 
available at the beginning of each workday. These reports can summarize inventory levels, 
production activity, sales volume and the like. Key-indicator reports are used by managers and 
executives to take quick, corrective action on significant aspects of the business.
