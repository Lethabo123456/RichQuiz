The lack of a robust or common information infrastructure can also create problems. For ex- 
ample, much of Latin America lags behind the rest of the world in Internet usage, and online 
marketplaces are almost non-existent there. This gap makes it difficult for multinational com- 
panies to get online with their Latin American business partners. Even something as mundane 
as the fact that the power plug on a piece of equipment built in one country might not fit into 
the power socket of another country can affect the infrastructure.
