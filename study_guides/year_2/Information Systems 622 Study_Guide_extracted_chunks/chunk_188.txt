The Knowledge Acquisition Facility   
A difficult task in developing an expert system is the process of creating and updating the 
knowledge base. In the past, when more traditional programming languages were used, 
developing a knowledge base was tedious and time consuming. Each fact, relationship and rule 
had to be programmed into the knowledge base. In most cases, an experienced programmer 
had to create and update the knowledge base.
