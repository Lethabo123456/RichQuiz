information and decision support system . A knowledge base must be developed for each 
unique application. For example, a medical expert system contains facts about diseases and 
symptoms. The following are some tools and techniques that can be used to create a knowledge 
base.  
■ Assembling human experts. One challenge in developing a knowledge base is to assemble 
the knowledge of multiple human experts. Typically, the objective in building a knowledge 
base is to integrate the knowledge of people with similar expertise (for example, many doctors 
might contribute to a medical diagnostics knowledge base).
