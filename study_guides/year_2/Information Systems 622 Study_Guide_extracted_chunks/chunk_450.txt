Privacy and the Government  
Many people are suspicious of the government when it comes to information that is stored 
about them. In the UK, the government wanted an identity card scheme which, it is claimed, 
will help fight international terrorism and identify theft and other fraud. The card would be 
linked to a database, which would hold names, addresses and biometric information on all 
citizens. Expected to cost many billions of euros, some people have pledged never to carry
