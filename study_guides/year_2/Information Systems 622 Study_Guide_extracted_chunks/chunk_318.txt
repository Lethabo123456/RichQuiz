In systems development, each activity has an earliest start time, earliest finish time and slack 
time, which is the amount of time an activity can be delayed without delaying the entire project. 
The critical path consists of all activities that if delayed, would delay the entire project. These 
activities have zero slack time. Any problems with critical-path activities will cause problems 
for the entire project. To ensure that critical-path activities are completed in a timely fashion, 
formalized project management approaches have been developed. Tools such as Microsoft 
Project are available to help compute these critical project attributes.
