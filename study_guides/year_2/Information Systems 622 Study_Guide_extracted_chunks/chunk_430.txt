Monitoring Policies and Procedures  
To ensure that users throughout an organization are following established procedures, the next 
step is to monitor routine practices and take corrective action if necessary. By under- standing 
what is happening in day-to-day activities, organizations can make adjustments or develop new 
procedures. Many organizations implement internal audits to measure actual results against 
established goals, such as percentage of end-user reports produced on time, percentage of 
datainput errors detected, number of input transactions entered per eight-hour shift and so on.
