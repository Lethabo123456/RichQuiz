Point Evaluation  
One of the disadvantages of cost–benefit analysis is the difficulty of determining the monetary 
values for all the benefits. An approach that does not employ monetary values is a point 
evaluation system. Each evaluation factor is as- signed a weight, in percentage points, based 
on importance. Then each proposed information system is evaluated in terms of this factor and 
given a score, such as one ranging from 0 to 100, where 0 means that the alternative does not 
address the feature at all and 100 means that the alternative addresses that feature perfectly.
