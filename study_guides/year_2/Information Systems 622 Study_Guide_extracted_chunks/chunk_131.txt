higher-level decision making in the organization. The two systems are, however, different in 
important ways. DSSs provide a variety of modelling and analysis tools to enable users to 
thoroughly analyze problems – that is, they allow users to answer questions. ESSs present 
structured inform. about aspects of the organization that executives consider important. In other 
words, they allow executives to ask the right questions. The following are general
