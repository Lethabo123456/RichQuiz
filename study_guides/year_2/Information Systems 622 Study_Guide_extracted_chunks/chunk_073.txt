Heuristics, often referred to as ‘rules of thumb’ – commonly accepted guidelines or procedures 
that usually find a good solution – are often used in decision making. An example of a heuristic 
is to order four months’ supply of inventory for a particular item when the inventory level drops 
to 20 units or less; although this heuristic might not minimize total inventory costs, it can serve 
as a good rule of thumb to avoid running out of stock without maintaining excess inventory. 
Trend Micro, a provider of antivirus software, has developed an antispam product that is based 
on heuristics. The software examines emails to find those most likely to be spam. It doesn’t 
examine all emails.
