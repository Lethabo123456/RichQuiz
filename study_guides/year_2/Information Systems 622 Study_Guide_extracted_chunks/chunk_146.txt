and typically is not objective or formalized. Knowing the best way to negotiate with a foreign 
government about nuclear disarmament or deal with a volatile hostage situation often requires 
a lifetime of experience and a high level of skill.

that would always work in every hostage situation. Many organizations actively attempt to
