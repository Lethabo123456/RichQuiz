These systems use voice recognition and natural language processing to let customers access 
pension accounts, check balances and find stock quotes. Eventually, the technology may allow 
people to make transactions using voice commands over the phone and to use search engines 
to have their questions answered through the brokerage firm’s call centre. One of the big
