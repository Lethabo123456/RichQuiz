Expert Systems Development   
Like other computer systems, expert systems require a systematic development approach for 
best results. This approach includes determining the requirements for the expert system, 
identifying one or more experts in the area or discipline under investigation, constructing the 
components of the expert system, implementing the results, and maintaining and reviewing the 
complete system.
