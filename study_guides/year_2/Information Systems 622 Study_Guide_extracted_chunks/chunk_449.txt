Even with these precautions, computers and networks can never be completely protected 
against crime. One of the biggest threats is from employees. Although firewalls provide good 
perimeter control to prevent crime from the outside, procedures and protection measures are 
needed to protect against computer crime by employees. Passwords, identification numbers and 
tighter control of employees and managers also help prevent Internet-related crime.  
  
6.8 Privacy  
Privacy is a big issue for many people. When information is computerized and can be processed 
and transferred easily, augmented and collated, summarized and reported, privacy concerns 
grow. The European Union has a data-protection directive
