LUUP10 is a payment system (the developers call it a ‘digital wallet’) that works using the text 
feature on mobile phones to transfer funds from buyer to seller (see Figure 4.4). The buyer 
sends LUUP a text with the format ‘PAY USERNAME AMOUNT’. LUUP then transfers the 
specified amount from the buyer’s account to the seller’s ac- count. For example a buyer might 
text ‘PAY 10943933 EUR10’. This would cause a transfer of €10 to be transferred to account
