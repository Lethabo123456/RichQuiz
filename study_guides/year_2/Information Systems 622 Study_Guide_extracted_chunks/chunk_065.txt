identifying a problem, you begin the problem-solving process with decision making. A 
wellknown model developed by Herbert Simon divides the decision-making phase of the 
problemsolving process into three stages: intelligence, design and choice. This model was later 
incorporated by George Huber into an expanded model of the entire problem-solving process 
(see Figure 2.1)
