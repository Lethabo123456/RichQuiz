business functions of the firm. As a result, costs were higher and customer service suffered. 
This led firms to start to create new systems, which came to be known as ERP systems. Large 
organizations, especially members of the Fortune 1000, were the first to take on the challenge 
of implementing ERP. An ERP is a system that manages an entire company’s vital business 
information. Many firms consider themselves to have an ERP if the system manages most, 
rather than all, of their information.
