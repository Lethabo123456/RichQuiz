The need for good design goes beyond mere costs. Companies risk ignoring small system 
problems when they arise, but these small problems can become large in the future. As 
mentioned earlier, because maintenance programmers spend an estimated 50 per cent or more 
of their time deciphering poorly written, undocumented program code, they have little time to 
spend on developing new, more effective systems. If   put to good use, the tools and techniques 
discussed in this chapter will allow organizations to build longer-lasting, more reliable systems.
