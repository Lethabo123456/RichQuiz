In some cases, the achievement of performance objectives can be easily measured (e.g. by 
tracking the time it takes to determine product availability). In other cases, it is sometimes more 
difficult to ascertain in the short term. For example, it might be difficult to determine how many 
customers are lost because of slow responses to customer enquiries regarding product 
availability. These outcomes, however, are often closely associated with business goals and are 
vital to the long-term success of the organization. Senior management usually dictates their 
attainment.
