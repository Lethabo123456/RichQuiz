Email Privacy  
Email also raises some interesting issues about work privacy. A company has the right to look 
at any data stored on its servers, which includes its email servers and therefore all messages 
sent by or to its employees. Many companies routinely store all emails sent or received for 
several years and many employees have lost their jobs for forwarding inappropriate messages. 
Others have sent embarassing messages that have been forwarded exponentially by recipients
