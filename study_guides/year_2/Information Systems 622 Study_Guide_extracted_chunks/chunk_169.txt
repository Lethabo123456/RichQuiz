■ In military applications, robots are becoming real weapons. The US Air Force is developing 
a smart robotic jet fighter. Often called ‘unmanned combat air vehicles’ (UCAVs), these robotic 
war machines, such as the X-45A, will be able to identify and destroy targets without human 
pilots. UCAVs send pictures and information to a central command centre and can be directed 
to strike military targets. These new machines extend the current Predator and Global Hawk
