Using biometrics is another way to protect important data and information systems. Biometrics 
involves the measurement of one of a person’s traits, whether physical or behavioural. 
Biometric techniques compare a person’s unique characteristics against a stored set to detect 
differences between them. Biometric systems can scan fingerprints, faces, handprints, irises 
and retinal images to prevent unauthorized access to important data and computer resources. 
Most of the interest among corporate users is in fingerprint technology, followed by face 
recognition. Fingerprint scans hit the middle ground between price and effectiveness (see 
Figure 13.2). Iris and retina scans are more accurate, but they are more expensive and involve 
more equipment.
