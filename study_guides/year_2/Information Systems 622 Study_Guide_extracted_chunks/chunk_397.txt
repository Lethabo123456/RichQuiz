IS Personnel: Hiring and Training  
Depending on the size of the new system, an organization might have to hire and, in some cases, 
train new IS personnel. An IS manager, systems analysts, computer programmers, data- entry 
operators and similar personnel might be needed for the new system.  
As with users, the eventual success of any system depends on how it is used by the IS personnel
