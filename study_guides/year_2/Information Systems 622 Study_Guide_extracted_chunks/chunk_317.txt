Use of Project Management Tools  
Project management involves planning, scheduling, directing and controlling human, financial 
and technological resources for a defined task whose result is achievement of specific goals 
and objectives. Even small systems development projects must employ some type of project 
management.  
A project schedule is a detailed description of what is to be done. Each project activity, the use 
of personnel and other resources, and expected completion dates are described. A project 
milestone is a critical date for the completion of a major part of the project. The completion of 
program design, coding, testing and release are examples of milestones for a programming
