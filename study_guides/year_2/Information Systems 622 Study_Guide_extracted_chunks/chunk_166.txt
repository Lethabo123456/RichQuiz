The Major Branches of Artificial Intelligence    
AI is a broad field that includes several specialty areas, such as expert systems, robotics, vision 
systems, natural language processing, learning systems and neural networks. Many of these 
areas are related; advances in one can occur simultaneously with or result in advances in others.    
  
Expert Systems    
An expert system consists of hardware and software that stores knowledge and makes 
inferences, similar to those of a human expert. Because of their many business applications, 
expert systems are discussed in more detail in their own section later in this chapter.
