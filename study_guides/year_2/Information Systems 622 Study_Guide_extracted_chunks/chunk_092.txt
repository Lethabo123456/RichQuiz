 Master production scheduling and inventory control. Scheduling production and 
controlling inventory are critical for any manufacturing company. The overall objective of 
master production scheduling is to provide detailed plans for both short-term and longrange 
scheduling of manufacturing facilities. Many techniques are used to minimize inventory 
costs. Most determine how much and when to order inventory. One method of determining 
how much inventory to order is called the economic order quantity (EOQ). This quantity 
is calculated to minimize the total inventory costs. The when-to-order question is based on 
inventory usage over time. Typically, the question is answered in terms of a reorder point 
(ROP), which is a critical inventory quantity level. When the inventory level for a particular
