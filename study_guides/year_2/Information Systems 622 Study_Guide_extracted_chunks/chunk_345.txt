customer. The purpose of requirements analysis is to capture these requests in detail. Questions 
that should be asked during requirements analysis include the following:  
■Are these stakeholders satisfied with the current accounts payable application?   
■What improvements could be made to satisfy suppliers and help the purchasing department?
