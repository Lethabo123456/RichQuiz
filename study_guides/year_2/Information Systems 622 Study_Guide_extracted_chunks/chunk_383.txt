are designed to protect the vendor, not necessarily the organization buying the computer 
equipment.  
Organizations often use outside consultants and legal firms to help them develop their contracts. 
Such contracts stipulate exactly what they expect from the system vendor and what interaction 
will occur between the vendor and the organization. All equipment specifications, software, 
training, installation, maintenance and so on are clearly stated. Also, the contract stipulates 
deadlines for the various stages or milestones of installation and implementation, as well as 
actions that the vendor will take in case of delays or problems. Some organizations include 
penalty clauses in the contract, in case the vendor does not meet its obligation by the specified
