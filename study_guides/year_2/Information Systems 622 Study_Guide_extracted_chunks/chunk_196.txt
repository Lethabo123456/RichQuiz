■ Games. Some expert systems are used for entertainment. For example, 20Q (www.20Q.net).   
■ Information management and retrieval. The explosive growth of information available to 
decision makers has created a demand for devices to help manage the information. Bots can 
aid this process. Businesses might use a bot to retrieve information from large distributed 
databases or a vast network like the Internet.   
■ AI and expert systems embedded in products. The antilock braking system on today’s cars is 
an example of a rudimentary expert system. A processor senses when the tyres are beginning 
to skid and releases the brakes for a fraction of a second to prevent the skid. AI researchers are 
also finding ways to use neural networks and robotics in everyday devices, such as toasters, 
alarm clocks and televisions.
