Risks in Using One Vendor  
The high cost to switch to another vendor’s ERP system makes it extremely unlikely that a firm 
will do so. After a company has adopted an ERP system, the vendor has less incentive to listen 
and respond to customer concerns. The high cost to switch also comes with the risk that the 
ERP vendor allows its product to become outdated or goes out of business.  
Selecting an ERP system involves not only choosing the best software product but also the right 
long-term business partner. It was unsettling for many companies that had implemented
