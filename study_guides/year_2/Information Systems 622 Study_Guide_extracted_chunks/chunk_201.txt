3.4 Virtual Reality   
  
The term ‘virtual reality’ was initially coined by Jaron Lanier, founder of VPL Research, in 
1989. Originally, the term referred to immersive virtual reality in which the user becomes fully 
immersed in an artificial, 3D world that is completely generated by a computer. Immersive 
virtual reality can represent any 3D setting, real or abstract, such as a building, an 
archaeological excavation site, human anatomy, a sculpture or a crime scene reconstruction.
