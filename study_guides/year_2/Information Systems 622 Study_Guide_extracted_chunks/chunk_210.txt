A ‘Soft’ Octopus Robot   
When you think of robots, the images that come to mind are probably heavy bomb disposal 
units, expensive NASA equipment on Mars or even the Lego Mindstorms kit that was 
mentioned in this chapter. Octobot is nothing like these. Its designers sources to create a pliable 
robot that operates with- out rigid parts. Less than 10cm long and 2cm tall, Octobot is made of 
silicone rubber and is intended to squeeze into tight spaces, mould to its surroundings and handle 
delicate objects safely. Robot re- searcher Michael Wehner said that Octobot could ‘either 
handle something that’s very delicate, or move the body around to get into tight spaces in search 
and rescue, or maybe internal medicine.
