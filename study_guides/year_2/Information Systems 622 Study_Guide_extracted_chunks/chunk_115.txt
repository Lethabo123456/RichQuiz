Capabilities of a Decision Support System  
Developers of decision support systems strive to make them more flexible than management 
information systems and to give them the potential to assist decision makers in a variety of 
situations. DSSs can assist with all or most problem-solving phases, decision frequencies and 
different degrees of problem structure. DSS approaches can also help at all levels of the 
decision- making process. A single DSS might provide only a few of these capabilities, 
depending on its uses and scope.

assist decision makers with the phases of problem solving. As previously discussed, these
