 Parallel communication. With traditional group meetings, people must take turns 
addressing various issues. One person normally talks at a time. With a GSS, every group 
member can address issues or make comments at the same time by entering them into a PC 
or workstation. These comments and issues are displayed on every group member’s PC or 
workstation immediately. Parallel communication can speed meeting times and result in 
better decisions.
