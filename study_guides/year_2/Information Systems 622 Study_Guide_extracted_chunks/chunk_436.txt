As a result of the increasing threat of viruses and worms, most computer users and 
organizations have installed antivirus programs on their computers. Such software runs in the 
background to protect your computer from dangers lurking on the Internet and other possible 
sources of infected files. Some antivirus software is even capable of repairing common virus 
infections automatically, without interrupting your work. The latest virus definitions are 
downloaded automatically when you connect to the Inter- net, ensuring that your PC’s 
protection is current. To safeguard your PC and prevent it from spreading viruses to your 
friends and co-workers, some antivirus software scans and cleans both incoming and outgoing 
email messages
