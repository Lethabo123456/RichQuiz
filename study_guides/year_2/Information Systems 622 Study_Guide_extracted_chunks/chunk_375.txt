Determining which option is best for a particular company in a given situation can be difficult.  
Financial considerations, tax laws, the organization’s policies, its sales and transaction growth, 
marketplace dynamics and the organization’s financial resources are all important factors. In 
some cases, lease or rental fees can amount to more than the original purchase price after a few 
years. As a result, some companies prefer to purchase their equipment.
