Test the Database During this step, the DBA tests the database to ensure that it maintains the 
integrity and security of the data. Data integrity is enforced by the DBMS through the proper use 
of primary and foreign key rules. Many DBMSs also support the creation of domain constraints and 
database triggers. Testing will ensure that these constraints were properly designed and 
implemented. Data integrity is also the result of properly implemented data management policies, 
which are part of a comprehensive data administration framework.
