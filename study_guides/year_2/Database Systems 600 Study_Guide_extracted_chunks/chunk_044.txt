Procedures. Procedures are the instructions and rules that govern the design and use of the 
database system. Procedures are a critical component of the system because they enforce the 
standards by which business is conducted within the organization and with customers. 
Procedures also help to ensure that companies have an organized way to monitor and audit the 
data that enter the database and the information generated from those data.  
• 
Data. The word data covers the collection of facts stored in the database. Determining which 
data to enter into the database and how to organize that data is a vital part of the database 
designer’s job.
