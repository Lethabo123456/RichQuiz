Data Security, Privacy, and Integrity Data security, privacy, and integrity are of great concern to 
DBAs who manage DBMS installations. The DBA must use the security and integrity mechanisms 
provided by the DBMS to enforce the database administration policies defined in the previous 
section. In addition, DBAs must team up with Internet security experts to build security mechanisms 
that safeguard data from possible attacks or unauthorized access.
