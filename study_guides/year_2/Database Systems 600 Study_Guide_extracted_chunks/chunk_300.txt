Aggregate functions (COUNT, MIN, MAX, and AVG) are special functions that perform 
arithmetic computations over a set of rows, often used in conjunction with the GROUP BY 
clause to group the output of aggregate computations by one or more attributes.  
 
 
Subqueries and correlated queries are used when it is necessary to process data based on 
other processed data. That is, the query uses results that were previously unknown and that 
are generated by another query.

Most subqueries are executed in a serial fashion. That is, the outer query initiates the data 
request, and then the inner subquery is executed. In contrast, a correlated subquery is a 
subquery that is executed once for each row in the outer query.
