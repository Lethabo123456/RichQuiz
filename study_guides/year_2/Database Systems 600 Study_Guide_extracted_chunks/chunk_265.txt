The HAVING clause operates very much like the WHERE clause in the SELECT statement. 
However, the WHERE clause applies to columns and expressions for individual rows, whereas 
the HAVING clause is applied to the output of a GROUP BY operation. For example, suppose 
that you want to generate a listing of the number of products in the inventory supplied by each 
vendor. However, this time you want to limit the listing to products whose prices average less 
than $10. The query requires both a GROUP BY clause and a HAVING clause.  
SELECT   
V_CODE, COUNT(P_CODE) AS NUMPRODS 
FROM  
 
PRODUCT  
GROUP  
 
BY V_CODE  
HAVING   
AVG(P_PRICE) < 10  
ORDER BY  
V_CODE;
