users. (It does not take much imagination to predict the likely results if students have access to a 
student database or if employees have access to payroll data!) Consequently, you must test for at 
least the following:  
 
Physical security allows only authorized personnel physical access to specific areas. Depending 
on the type of database implementation, however, establishing physical security might not 
always be practical. For example, a university student research database is not a likely candidate 
for physical security.  
 
Password security allows the assignment of access rights to specific authorized users. Password 
security is usually enforced at login time at the operating system level.
