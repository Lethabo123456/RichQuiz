The success of the overall information systems strategy and data administration strategy depends 
on several critical success factors that the DBA needs to understand. Critical success factors include 
the following managerial, technological, and corporate culture issues: 
 
Management commitment.  
 
Thorough analysis of the company situation. 
 
End-user involvement.  
 
Defined standards.  
 
Training.  
 
A small pilot project.  
This list of factors is not comprehensive, but it does provide the framework for developing a 
successful strategy. Remember that no matter how comprehensive you make the list, it must be 
based on developing and implementing a data administration strategy that is tightly integrated with 
the organization’s overall information systems planning.
