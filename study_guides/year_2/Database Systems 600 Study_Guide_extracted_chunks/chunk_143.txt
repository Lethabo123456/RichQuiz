Such a relationship means that both conditions have been met for the weak entity definition—the 
related entity is existence-dependent, and the PK of the related entity contains a PK component of 
the parent entity. Remember that the weak entity inherits part of its primary key from its strong 
counterpart.  
Keep in mind that the database designer usually determines whether an entity can be described as 
weak based on the business rules. An examination of Figure 4.8 might cause you to conclude that 
CLASS is a weak entity to COURSE. After all, it seems clear that a CLASS cannot exist without a 
COURSE, so there is existence dependence. The second weak entity requirement has not been met; 
therefore, by definition, the CLASS entity in Figure 4.8 may not be classified as weak.
