Completeness Constraint 
The completeness constraint specifies whether each entity supertype occurrence must also be a 
member of at least one subtype. The completeness constraint can be partial or total. Partial 
completeness means that not every supertype occurrence is a member of a subtype; some supertype 
occurrences may not be members of any subtype. Total completeness means that every supertype 
occurrence must be a member of at least one subtype.
