Confidentiality deals with ensuring that data is protected against unauthorized access, and if 
the data is accessed by an authorized user, that it is used only for an authorized purpose. Data 
must be evaluated and classified according to the level of confidentiality: highly restricted (very 
few people have access), confidential (only certain groups have access), and unrestricted (can 
be accessed by all users). The data security officer spends a great amount of time ensuring that 
the organization is in compliance with desired levels of confidentiality. Compliance refers to 
activities that meet data privacy and security reporting guidelines.
