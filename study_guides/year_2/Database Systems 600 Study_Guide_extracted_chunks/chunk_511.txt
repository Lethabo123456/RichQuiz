 
The DA and DBA functions tend to overlap. Generally speaking, the DA has more 
managerial tasks than the more technically oriented DBA. 
 
A DBA’s managerial services include supporting end users; defining and enforcing policies, 
procedures, and standards for the database; ensuring data security, privacy, and integrity; 
providing data backup and recovery services; and monitoring distribution and use of the data 
in the database.  
 
The DBA’s technical role requires involvement in at least the following activities: 
evaluating, selecting, and installing the DBMS; designing and implementing databases and 
applications; testing and evaluating databases and applications; operating and maintaining 
the DBMS, utilities, and applications; and training and supporting users.
