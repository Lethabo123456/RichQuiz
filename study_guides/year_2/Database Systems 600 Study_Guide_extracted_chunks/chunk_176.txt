Design Case 2: Maintaining History of Time-Variant Data 
Company managers generally realize that good decision making is based on the information 
generated through the data stored in databases. Such data reflects both current and past events. 
Company managers use the data stored in databases to answer questions. In other words, the data 
stored in databases reflects not only current data but also historic data.

attribute must be preserved. From a data-modelling point of view, time-variant data refers to data
