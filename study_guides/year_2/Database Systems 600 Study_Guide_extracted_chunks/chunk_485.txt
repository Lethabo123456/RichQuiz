The following sections examine the services in greater detail. 
End-User Support The DBA interacts with end users by providing data and information support 
services which include the following:  
 
Gathering user requirements. The DBA must work with end users to help gather the data 
required to identify and describe their present and future information needs.  
 
Building end-user confidence. Finding adequate solutions to end users’ problems increases 
their trust and confidence in the DBA.
