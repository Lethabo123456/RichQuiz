The Need for Normalization 
Normalization is typically used by database designers in conjunction with the ERM; when 
designing a new database structure based on the business requirements of the end users and to 
analyse the relationships among the attributes within each entity, of an existing database, and 
determine if the structure can be improved through normalization. In either case, the normalization 
process is the same.
