The UNION statement can be used to unite more than just two queries. For example, assume that 
you have four union-compatible queries named T1, T2, T3, and T4. With the UNION statement, 
you can combine the output of all four queries into a single result set. The SQL statement will be 
similar to this:

UNION

UNION  
SELECT  column-list FROM T4;
