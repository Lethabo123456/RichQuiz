The ER model is based on the following components:  
 
Entity. Earlier in this chapter, an entity was defined as anything about which data will be 
collected and stored. An entity is represented in the ERD by a rectangle, also known as an 
entity box. The name of the entity, a noun, is written in the centre of (ER) model (ERM) the 
rectangle. The entity name is generally written in capital letters and in singular form. 
Usually, when applying the ERD to the relational model, an entity is mapped to a relational 
table. Each row in the relational table is known as an entity instance or entity occurrence in 
the ER model. A collection of like entities is known as an entity set. Technically speaking, 
the ERD (1:1, 1:M, and M:N) depicts entity sets. 
 
Each entity consists of a set of attributes that describes characteristics of the entity.
