5.8 Centralized Versus Decentralized Design 
 
The two general approaches to database design (bottom-up and top-down) can be influenced by 
factors such as the scope and size of the system, the company’s management style, and the 
company’s structure (centralized or decentralized). Depending on these factors, the database design 
may be based on two very different design philosophies: centralized and decentralized. Centralized 
design is productive when the data component has a relatively small number of objects and 
procedures. The design can be carried out and represented in a fairly simple database. Centralized 
design is typical of relatively simple, small databases and can be successfully done by a single 
database administrator or by a small, informal design team. The company operations and the scope 
of the problem are sufficiently limited to allow even a single designe
