Entity subtypes inherit all relationships in which the supertype entity participates. Through 
inheritance, all subtypes also participate in that relationship. In specialization hierarchies with 
multiple levels of supertype and subtypes, a lower-level subtype inherits all of the attributes and 
relationships from all of its upper-level supertypes. Inheriting the relationships of their supertypes 
does not mean that subtypes cannot have relationships of their own.
