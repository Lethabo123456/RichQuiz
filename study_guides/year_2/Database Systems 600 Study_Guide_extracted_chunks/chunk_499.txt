Two main types of data dictionaries exist: integrated and standalone. An integrated (built-in) data 
dictionary is included with the DBMS. Other DBMSs, especially older types, do not have a built-
in data dictionary; instead, the DBA may use third-party standalone systems.  
Data dictionaries can also be classified as active or passive. An active data dictionary is 
automatically updated by the DBMS with every database access to keep its access information up 
to date. A passive data dictionary is not updated automatically and usually requires running a 
batch process. Data dictionary access information is normally used by the DBMS for query 
optimization.
