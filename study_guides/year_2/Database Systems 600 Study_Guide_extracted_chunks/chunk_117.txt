Formal Definitions and Terminology

since the mathematical terms need to be precise, we will use the more specific term relation when 
discussing the formal definitions of the various relational algebra operators. One important aspect 
of using the specific term relation is that it acknowledges the distinction between the relation and 
the relation variable, or relvar, for short. A relation is the data that we see in our tables. A relvar is
