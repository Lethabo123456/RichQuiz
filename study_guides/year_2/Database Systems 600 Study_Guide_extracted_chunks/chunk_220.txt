Fourth Normal Form (4NF) 
You might encounter poorly designed databases, or you might be asked to convert spreadsheets into 
a database format in which multiple multivalued attributes exist.  
There is a problem with the tables in Figure 6.11. The attributes ORG_CODE and ASSIGN_NUM 
each may have many different values. In normalization terminology, this situation is referred to as 
a multivalued dependency, which occurs when one key determines multiple values of two other 
attributes and those attributes are independent of each other.
