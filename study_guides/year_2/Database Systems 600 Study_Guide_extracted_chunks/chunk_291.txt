Inserting Rows with Null Attributes In the previous example you entered rows in which all of the 
attribute values are specified. But what do you do if, for example, a product does not have a vendor 
or if you do not yet know the vendor code? In those cases, you would want to leave the vendor code 
null. To enter a null, use the following syntax:  
INSERT INTO PRODUCT  
VALUES ('BRT-345','Titanium drill bit','18-Oct-17', 75, 10, 4.50, 0.06, NULL);
