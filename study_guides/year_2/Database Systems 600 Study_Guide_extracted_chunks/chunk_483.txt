The goals that the DA sets are defined by issues such as:  
 
Data “sharability” and time availability  
 
Data consistency and integrity  
 
Data security and privacy  
Naturally, the list can be expanded to fit an organization’s specific data needs. Regardless of how 
data management is conducted—and despite the fact that great authority is invested in the DA or 
DBA to define and control the way company data is used—the DA and DBA do not own the data.
