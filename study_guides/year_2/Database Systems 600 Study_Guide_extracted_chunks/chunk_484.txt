 Database users might be classified by the following criteria:  
 
Type of decision-making support required (operational, tactical, or strategic)  
 
Degree of computer knowledge (novice, proficient, or expert)  
 
Frequency of access (casual, periodic, or frequent)  
These classifications are not exclusive and usually overlap. The DBA must be able to interact with 
all of them, understand their different needs, answer questions at all levels of expertise, and 
communicate effectively. The DBA activities emphasises the need for a diverse mix of skills.
