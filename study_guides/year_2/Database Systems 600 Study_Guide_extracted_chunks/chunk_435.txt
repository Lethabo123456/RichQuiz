OLE-DB and ADO 
 

 
176 
ODBC, DAO, and RDO do not provide support for nonrelational data. To answer that need and to 
simplify data connectivity, Microsoft developed Object Linking and Embedding for Database 
(OLE-DB). Based on Microsoft’s Component Object Model (COM), OLE-DB is database 
middleware that adds object-oriented functionality for access to relational and nonrelational data.  
 
OLE-DB is composed of a series of COM objects that provide low-level database connectivity for 
applications. The OLE-DB model is better understood when you divide its functionality into two 
types of objects:
