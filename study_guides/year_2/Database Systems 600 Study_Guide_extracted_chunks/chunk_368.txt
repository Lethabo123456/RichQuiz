Map the Conceptual Model to the Logical Model 
Recall that the second phase of the DBLC is database design, which comprises three stages: 
conceptual design, logical design, and physical design, plus the critical decision of DBMS selection. 
Conceptual design is the first stage in the database design process. The goal at this stage is to design 
a database that is independent of database software and physical details. The output of this process 
is a conceptual data model that describes the main data entities, attributes, relationships, and 
constraints of a given problem domain. This design is descriptive and narrative in form. In other 
words, it is generally composed of a graphical representation as well as textual descriptions of the
