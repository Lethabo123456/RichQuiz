Relationship Strength 
The concept of relationship strength is based on how the primary key of a related entity is defined. 
To implement a relationship, the primary key of one entity (the parent entity, normally on the “one” 
side of the one-to-many relationship) appears as a foreign key in the related entity (the child entity, 
mostly the entity on the “many” side of the one- to-many relationship). Sometimes, the foreign key
