Developing the Conceptual model using some of the steps listed in Table 9.3 take place

As you will likely discover, the initial ER model may be subjected to several revisions before it 
meets the system’s requirements. Such a revision process is quite natural. Remember that the ER 
model is a communications tool as well as a design blueprint. Therefore, when you meet with the 
proposed system users, the initial ER model should give rise to questions such as “Is this really 
what you meant?”
