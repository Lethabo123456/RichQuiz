Analyse the Company Situation   the company situation describes the general conditions in which 
a company operates, its organizational structure, and its mission. To analyse the company situation, 
the database designer must learn the company’s operational components, how they function, and 
how they interact.  
The following issues must be resolved:  
 
What is the organization’s general operating environment, and what is its mission within that 
environment?  
 
What is the organization’s structure? Knowing who controls what and who reports to whom is 
quite useful when you need to define required information flows, specific report and query 
formats, and so on.
