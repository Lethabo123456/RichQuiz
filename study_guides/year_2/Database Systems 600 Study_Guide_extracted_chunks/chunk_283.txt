The final SQL command sequence creates the LINE table. The LINE table has a composite primary 
key (INV_NUMBER, LINE_NUMBER) and uses a UNIQUE constraint in INV_NUMBER and 
P_CODE to ensure that the same product is not ordered twice in the same invoice.
