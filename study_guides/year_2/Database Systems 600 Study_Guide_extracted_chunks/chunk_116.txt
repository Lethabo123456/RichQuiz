The next section (Chapter 2.2), Entity Relationship (ER) Modelling, discusses several ways to 
handle nulls. Other integrity rules that can be enforced in the relational model are the NOT NULL 
and UNIQUE constraints. The NOT NULL constraint can be placed on a column to ensure that 
every row in the table has a value for that column. The UNIQUE constraint is a restriction placed 
on a column to ensure that no duplicate values exist for that column.
