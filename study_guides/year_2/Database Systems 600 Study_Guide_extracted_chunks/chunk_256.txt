example, consider the following query:  
SELECT  P_DESCRIPT, P_QOH, P_PRICE, V_CODE 
FROM  
PRODUCT 
WHERE  
V_CODE = 21344;  
This query returns the description, quantity on hand, price, and vendor code for products with a
