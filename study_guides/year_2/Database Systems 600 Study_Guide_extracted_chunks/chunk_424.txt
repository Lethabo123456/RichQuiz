During query optimization, the DBMS must choose what indexes to use, how to perform 
join operations, which table to use first, and so on. Each DBMS has its own algorithms for 
determining the most efficient way to access the data. The two most common approaches 
are rule-based and cost-based optimization.  
 
 
A rule-based optimizer uses pre-set rules and points to determine the best approach to 
execute a query. A cost-based optimizer uses sophisticated algorithms based on statistics 
about the objects being accessed to determine the best approach to execute a query.  
 
 
SQL performance tuning deals with writing queries that make good use of the statistics. In 
particular, queries should make good use of indexes.
