The data anomalies created by the database organization shown in Figure 6.4 are easily eliminated 
by completing the following two steps:  
Step 1: Make New Tables to Eliminate Transitive Dependencies For every transitive 
dependency, write a copy of its determinant as a primary key for a new table. A determinant is 
any attribute whose value determines other values within a row. If you have three different transitive 
dependencies, you will have three different determinants. As with the conversion to 2NF, it is 
important that the determinant remain in the original table to serve as a foreign key.  
Step 2: Reassign Corresponding Dependent Attributes Using Figure 6.4, identify the attributes 
that are dependent on each determinant identified in Step 1. Place the dependent attributes in the 
new tables with their determinants and remove them from their original tables.
