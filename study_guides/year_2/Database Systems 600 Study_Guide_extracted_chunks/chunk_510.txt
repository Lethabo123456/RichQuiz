Chapter Summary/Review 
 
 
 
Data management is a critical activity for any organization, so data must be treated as a 
corporate asset.  
 
Data quality is a comprehensive approach to ensure the accuracy, validity, and timeliness of 
data. Data quality focuses on correcting dirty data, preventing future inaccuracies in the data, 
and building user confidence in the data.  
 
The DBMS is the most commonly used tool for corporate data management. The DBMS 
supports strategic, tactical, and operational decision making at all levels of the organization.
