Each system has a predetermined operational life span, but its actual life span depends on its 
perceived utility. There are several reasons for reducing the operational life of certain systems. 
Rapid technological change is one reason, especially for systems based on processing speed and 
expandability. Another common reason is the cost of maintaining a system.
