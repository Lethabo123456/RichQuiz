ata Model Basic Building Blocks 
The basic building blocks of all data models are entities, attributes, relationships, and constraints. 
An entity is a person, place, thing, or event about which data will be collected and stored. An entity 
represents a type of object in the real world, and each entity occurrence is unique and distinct. For 
example, a CUSTOMER entity would have many distinguishable customer occurrences, such as 
John Smith, Pedro Dinamita, and Tom Strickland. Entities may be physical objects and may also be 
abstractions.  
An attribute is a characteristic of an entity. For example, a CUSTOMER entity would be described 
by attributes such as customer last name, customer first name, customer phone number, customer 
address, and customer credit limit. Attributes are the equivalent of fields in file systems.
