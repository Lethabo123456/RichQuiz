Although some cloud services require some degree of customization on the customer’s part, other 
cloud computing services are more transparent to the user and require less customization. For 
example, Dropbox and Microsoft OneDrive are simple cloud services that let you synchronize your 
documents, photos, music, and other files transparently over the Internet across many devices. As 
you can see, cloud computing implementations vary; the next section explains the basic types.
