The most widely used conceptual model is the ER model. With the ERD being used to graphically 
represent the conceptual schema. The conceptual model yields some important advantages. First, it 
provides a bird’s- eye (macro level) view of the data environment that is relatively easy to 
understand.  
Second, the conceptual model is independent of both software and hardware. Therefore, changes in 
either the hardware or the DBMS software will have no effect on the database design at the 
conceptual level. Generally, the term logical design refers to the task of creating a conceptual data 
model that could be implemented in any DBMS.
