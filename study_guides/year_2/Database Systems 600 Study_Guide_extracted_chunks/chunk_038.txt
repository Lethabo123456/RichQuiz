Data Anomalies

in only a single place. Data redundancy, however, fosters an abnormal condition by forcing field 
value changes in many different locations. If agent Leah F. Hahn, stored in a CUSTOMER file, 
decides to get married and move, the agent’s name, address, and phone number are likely to change,
