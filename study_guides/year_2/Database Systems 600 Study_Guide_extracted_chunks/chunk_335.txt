Evaluate the Database and Its Application Programs 
 
 As the database and application programs are created and tested, the system must also be evaluated 
using a more holistic approach. Testing and evaluation of the individual components should 
culminate in a variety of broader system tests to ensure that all of the components interact properly 
to meet the needs of the users. At this stage, integration issues and deployment plans are refined, 
user training is conducted, and system documentation is finalized. Once the system receives final 
approval, it must be a sustainable resource for the organization. To ensure that the data contained 
in the database is protected against loss, backup and recovery plans are tested.
