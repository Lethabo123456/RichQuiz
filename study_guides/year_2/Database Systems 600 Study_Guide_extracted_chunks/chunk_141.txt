Strong (Identifying) Relationships A strong (identifying) relationship exists when the primary 
key of the related entity contains a primary key component of the parent entity. The Crow’s Foot 
notation depicts the strong (identifying) relationship with a solid line between the entities. 
In summary, whether the relationship between COURSE and CLASS is strong or weak depends on 
how the CLASS entity’s primary key is defined.
