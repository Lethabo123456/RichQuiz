Entity Integrity: Selecting Primary Keys 
Arguably, the most important characteristic of an entity is its primary key, which uniquely 
identifies each entity instance. The primary key’s function is to guarantee entity integrity. 
Furthermore, primary keys and foreign keys work together to implement relationships in the 
relational model. Therefore, the importance of properly selecting the primary key has a direct 
bearing on the efficiency and effectiveness of database implementation.
