Knowing the data volume will help you determine how much storage space to reserve for the 
database. To do this, the designer follows a process similar to the one used during ER model 
verification. For each table, identify all possible transactions, their frequency, and volume. For 
each transaction, you determine the amount of data to be added or deleted from the database. 
This information will help you determine the amount of data to be stored in the related table.  

