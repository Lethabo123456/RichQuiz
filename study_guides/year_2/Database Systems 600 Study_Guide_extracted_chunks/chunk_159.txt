       Specialization Hierarchy 
 Entity supertypes and subtypes are organized in a specialization hierarchy, which depicts the 
arrangement of higher-level entity supertypes (parent entities) and lower-level entity subtypes 
(child entities). Figure 5.2 shows the specialization hierarchy formed by an EMPLOYEE supertype 
and three entity subtypes—PILOT, MECHANIC, and ACCOUNTANT. The specialization 
hierarchy reflects the 1:1 relationship between EMPLOYEE and its subtypes. For example, a 
PILOT subtype occurrence is related to one instance of the EMPLOYEE supertype.
