Current-generation DBMSs are intelligent enough to determine the best type of index to use under 
certain circumstances, provided that the DBMS has updated data-base statistics. Regardless of 
which index is chosen, the DBMS determines the best plan to execute a given query. The next 
section guides you through a simplified example of the type of choices the query optimizer must 
make.
