Lower normalization forms occur (and are even required) in specialized databases known as data 
warehouses. The data warehouse routinely uses 2NF structures in its complex, multilevel, 
multisource data environment. Although normalization is very important, especially in the so-called 
production database environment, 2NF is no longer disregarded as it once was.
