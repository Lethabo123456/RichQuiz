As you can see in the preceding examples, the data dictionary can be a tool for monitoring database

The data dictionary can also be used to ensure that application programmers have met the naming 
standards for data elements in the database, and that the data validation rules are correct. Thus, the 
data dictionary can be used to support a wide range of data administration activities and facilitate 
the design and implementation of information systems. Integrated data dictionaries are also 
essential to the use of computer-aided systems engineering tools.
