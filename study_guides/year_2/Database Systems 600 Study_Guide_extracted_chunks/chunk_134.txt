Implementing Multivalued Attributes Although the conceptual model can handle M:N 
relationships and multivalued attributes, you should not implement them in the RDBMS. 
Remember that in the relational table, each column and row intersection represents a single data 
value. So, if multivalued attributes exist, the designer must decide on one of two possible actions:  
1. Within the original entity, create several new attributes, one for each component of the original 
multivalued attribute. Although this solution seems to work, its adoption can lead to major 
structural problems in the table and it is not always acceptable.
