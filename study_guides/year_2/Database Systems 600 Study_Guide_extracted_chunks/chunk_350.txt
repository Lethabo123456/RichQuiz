therefore of business rules—are company managers, policymakers, department managers, and 
written documentation such as company procedures, standards, and operations manuals. A faster 
and more direct source of business rules is direct interviews with end users. Unfortunately, because 
perceptions differ, the end user can be a less reliable source when it comes to specifying business 
rules. For example, a maintenance department mechanic might believe that any mechanic can
