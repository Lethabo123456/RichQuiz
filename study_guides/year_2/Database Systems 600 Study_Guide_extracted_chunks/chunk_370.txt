As indicated throughout this section, database design is an iterative process. Activities such as 
normalization take place at different stages in the design process. Each time you reiterate a step, the 
model is further refined and better documented. New attributes may be created and assigned to the 
proper entities. Functional dependencies among determinant and dependent attributes are evaluated 
and data anomalies are prevented via normalization.
