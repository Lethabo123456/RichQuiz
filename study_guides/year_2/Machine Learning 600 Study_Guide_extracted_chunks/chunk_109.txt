6.5 TF/IDF 
One useful technique is to find out how important a word or phrase is within a corpus of text 
or a collection of documents. Term Frequency/Inverse Document Frequency (TF/IDF) is a 
method of giving a numerical value to the importance of a word. TF/IDF is used widely within 
recommendation systems, and it's quite easy to implement.
