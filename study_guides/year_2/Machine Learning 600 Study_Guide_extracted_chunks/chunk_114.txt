6.6.2 Images in ML 
Most of what we are doing with ML is feeding information, usually in number form, and 
finding patterns that can then be defined into models. So, if we can convert image data into 
a grid of numbers, what we are left with is a matrix grid of numbers that a ML algorithm can 
train against. You will find that most of the ML examples use a fairly small grid of numbers; 
images that are 16 × 16 and 28 × 28 pixels are used a lot. The reason for this is processing 
time; if an image is too large, then it will take a long time, depending on machine 
performance, to convert and process the image. When you are dealing with tens of thousands 
of images, these processes can take hours. In most cases, it is prudent to be prepared and size 
the images small enough for processing. It's also important to ensure that the image set is 
using the same height and width in pixels.
