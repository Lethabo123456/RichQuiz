6.7 Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) 
CNN was introduced into the ML word in 1998 where Yann LeCun, Leon Bottou, Yoshua 
Bengio, and Patrick Haffer published a paper outlining their work on a neural network called 
LeNet-5. As is tradition, the paper was using the MNIST number recognition dataset for both 
training and evaluation. There are two parts to the CNN; the first is feature extraction, and 
the second is classification. Let's break these two elements down and examine them further.
