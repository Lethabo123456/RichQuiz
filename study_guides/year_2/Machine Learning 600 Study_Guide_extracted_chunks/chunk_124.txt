Underfitting and Overfitting 
A model that returns a bias that is too high on the training dataset is simply a model that 
doesn’t fit. Its predictions are wrong, meaning that the model is wrong—mostly, too simple. 
You need to change the features and/or change the algorithm. In the literature, this situation 
is known as underfitting. A model with a good bias on the training dataset but a high variance 
is one that is not general enough and fits too much the training data. The algorithm is unfit 
too, as you fail to capture the inner relationships between data. In the literature, this situation
