If you push 
things straight to production, then you won't really know what's going to happen. 
With testing you can get an idea of the pain points. You might find data-loading 
issues, data-processing issues, or answers that just don't make sense. When you 
test, you have time to change things. 
 Reporting: sit down with the stakeholders and discuss the test results. 
 Refining: refine code and, if possible, the algorithms. 
 Production:  be sure to give consideration to when this project will be run—is it an 
hourly/daily/weekly/monthly job? 
 Avoiding Bias:  It is important to get the teams talking to each other about how to 
avoid introducing any form of bias into the final solution. Dataset choice and ML 
techniques (supervised/ unsupervised are important).
