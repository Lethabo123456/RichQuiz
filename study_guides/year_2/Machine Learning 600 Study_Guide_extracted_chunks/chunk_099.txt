Back propagation. Within the multilayer perceptron is the concept of back propagation, short 
for the “backward propagation of errors.” Back propagation calculates the gradients and 
maps the correct inputs to the correct outputs. There are two steps to back propagation: the 
propagation phase and the updating of the weight. This would occur for all the neurons in the 
network. Propagation happens, and the training is input through the network and generates 
the activations of the output. It then backward propagates the output activations and 
generates deltas of all the output and hidden layers of the network based on the target of the
