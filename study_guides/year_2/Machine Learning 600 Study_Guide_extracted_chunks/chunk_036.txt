Presence Checks: First things first, check that data has been entered at all. The presence check is 
simple enough. If the field length is empty or null and that piece of data is important in the analysis, 
then you can't use records from which the data is missing.  The first name and e-mail are missing from 
the example in Table 2.1, so the record should really be fixed or rejected. In theory, the data could be 
used if knowing the customer was not important.
