Today, the trend is to reduce the number of management levels, or layers, in the traditional 
organizational structure. This type of structure, often called a flat organizational structure, 
empowers employees at lower levels to make decisions and solve problems without needing 
permission from mid-level managers. Empowerment gives employees and their managers
