Output Devices 
Computer systems provide output to decision makers at all levels of an organization so they 
can solve a business problem or capitalize on a competitive opportunity. In addition, output 
from one computer system can provide input into another computer system. The desired form 
of this output might be visual, audio or even digital. Whatever the output’s content or form, 
output devices are designed to provide the right information to the right person in the right 
format at the right time. 
Display Monitors

monitors used a cathode-ray tube to display images, they were sometimes called CRTs. The
