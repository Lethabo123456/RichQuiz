What is Information? 
Information is one of those concepts that we all seem intuitively able to grasp, but find tricky 
to define. In the 1940s, mathematician Claude Shannon defined it as: information is that which 
reduces uncertainty. Shannon was working on the technical problems involved in sending 
messages over communication networks, and his concept of information is actually quite 
different from what we in business information systems mean by ‘information’. Nevertheless,
