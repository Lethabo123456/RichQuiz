Earnings Growth 
Another measure of IS value is the increase in profit or earnings growth it brings. For instance, 
a mail-order company might install an order-processing system that generates a 7 per cent 
earnings growth compared with the previous year. 
Market Share 
Market share is the percentage of sales that a product or service has in relation to the total 
market. If installing a new online catalogue increases sales, it might help a company to increase
