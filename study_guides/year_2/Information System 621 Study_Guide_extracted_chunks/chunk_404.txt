hundreds of millions of data records. As this data is collected from various sources, one data 
warehouse is built that business analysts can use. To keep it accurate, the data warehouse 
receives regular updates. Old data that is no longer needed is purged. It is common for a data 
warehouse to contain from three to ten years of current and historical data. Data-cleaning tools
