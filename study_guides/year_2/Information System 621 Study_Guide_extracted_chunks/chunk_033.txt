The World Wide Web (WWW), or the web, is a network of links on the Internet to documents.

them are controlled and provided by tens of thousands of special computers called ‘web 
servers’. The web is one of many services available over the Internet and provides access to 
many hundreds of millions of documents. Widely available Internet access has allowed the
