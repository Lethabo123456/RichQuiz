Resolve Relationships 
The customer–order relationship was implemented by taking the primary key of customer and 
posting it as a foreign key in the order table. This is essentially what resolving a relationship  
 
141 

means – deciding how to implement it. Sometimes a relationship between two entities will 
result in three tables being implemented, sometimes one, most often two. There is a series of 
rules to decide what tables to implement and which primary keys to use as a foreign key.
