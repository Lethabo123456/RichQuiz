The Northern Ireland Civil Service is introducing an electronic and document records 
management system (EDRM) to improve its efficiency. Processes have been reengineered so 
that instead of records being held in different departments and in different formats (on paper, 
in databases and even in email), a centralized system will provide access to up-to- date and 
secure information. It is hoped the system will also reduce the amount of paper the service uses.
