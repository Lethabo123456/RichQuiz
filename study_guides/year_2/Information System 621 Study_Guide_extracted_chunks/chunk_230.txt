Portable Computers 
Many computer manufacturers offer a variety of portable computers, those that are small 
enough to carry easily. Portable computers include handheld computers, laptop computers, 
notebook computers, netbook computers and tablet computers. 
Handheld computers are single-user computers that provide ease of portability because of 
their small size some are as small as a credit card. These systems often include a variety of 
software and communications capabilities. Most can communicate with desktop computers 
over wireless networks. Some even add a built-in GPS receiver with software that can integrate
