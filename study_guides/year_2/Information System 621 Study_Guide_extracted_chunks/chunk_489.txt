Web Browsers 
A web browser translates HTML so you can read it. It provides a graphical interface to the 
web. The menu consists of graphics, titles and text with hyper- text links. Hypermedia links 
you to Internet resources, including text documents, graphics, sound files and newsgroup 
servers. As you choose an item or resource, or move from one document to another, you might 
be accessing various computers on the Internet without knowing it, while the web handles all 
the connections. 
The beauty of web browsers and the web is that they make surfing the Internet fun. Clicking 
with a mouse on a highlighted word or graphic whisks you effortlessly to computers halfway 
around the world. Most browsers offer basic features such as support for backgrounds and
