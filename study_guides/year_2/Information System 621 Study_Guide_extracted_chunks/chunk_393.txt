The DBA also works with programmers as they build applications to ensure that their programs 
comply with DBMS standards and conventions. After the database is built and operating, the 
DBA monitors operations logs for security violations. Database performance is also monitored 
to ensure that the system’s response time meets users’ needs and that it operates efficiently. If 
there is a problem, the DBA attempts to correct it before it becomes serious.
