Increasingly, LANs are using some form of wireless communications. An example of a 
sophisticated LAN is the one that Digital Globe uses. Digital Globe is the company 
responsible for the detailed satellite images accessed by millions of Google Earth users. The 
firm uses a high-speed LAN (110GB/sec) to connect workers to its huge file storage system 
(200 TB) so that new images can be quickly captured and added to its rapidly growing 
repository of Earth photos.
