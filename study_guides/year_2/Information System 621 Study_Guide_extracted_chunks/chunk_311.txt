Middleware 
 
Middleware is software that allows various systems to communicate and exchange data. It is 
often developed to address situations where a company acquires different types of information 
systems through mergers, acquisitions or expansion and wants the systems to share data and 
interact. Middleware can also serve as an interface between the Internet and private corporate 
systems. For example, it can be used to transfer a request for information from a corporate 
customer on the corporate website to a traditional database on a mainframe computer and return 
the results to the customer on the Internet.
