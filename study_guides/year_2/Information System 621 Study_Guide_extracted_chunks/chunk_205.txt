La Paz is an upscale Mexican restaurant in Atlanta, Georgia. Its POS was outdated, and 
workers needed hours each night to close off and balance the books. The restaurant replaced its 
POS with a new system that provides useful payroll and management reporting features such 
as tracking tips and sales. Workers also like the feature that lets them close the books rapidly 
and accurately.
