Norddeutsche Landsman (NORD/LB) is a major financial institution with headquarters in 
Hanover, Germany. The bank was suffering from slow response time for its key systems while 
trying to meet new business needs. New blade server computers were installed to improve 
system response time by 40 per cent and provide the bank’s departments with the data they 
needed on a timely basis so they could operate efficiently.
