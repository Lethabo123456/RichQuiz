A fundamental difference between NAS and SAN is that NAS uses file input/output, which 
defines data as complete containers of information, while SAN deals with block input/output, 
which is based on subsets of data smaller than a file. SAN manufacturers include EMC, Hitachi 
Data Systems Corporation, Xiotech and IBM.
