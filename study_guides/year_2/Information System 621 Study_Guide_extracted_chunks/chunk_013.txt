friction and heat generation. Effectiveness is a measure of the extent to which a system 
achieves its goals. It can be computed by dividing the goals actually achieved by the total of 
the stated goals. For example, a company might want to achieve a net profit of €100 million 
for the year with a new information system. Actual profits, how- ever, might only be €85 
million for the year. In this case, the effectiveness is 85 per cent (85/100 = 85 per cent).
