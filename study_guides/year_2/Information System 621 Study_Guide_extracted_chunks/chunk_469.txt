Securing Wireless Networks 
WEP and WPA are the two main approaches to securing wireless networks such as wi-fi and 
WiMAX. Wired equivalent privacy (WEP) used to use encryption based on 64-bit key, which 
has been upgraded to a 128-bit key. WEP represents an early attempt at securing wireless 
communications and is not difficult for hackers to crack. Most wireless networks now use the 
Wi-Fi Protected Access (WPA) security protocol that offers significantly improved protection
