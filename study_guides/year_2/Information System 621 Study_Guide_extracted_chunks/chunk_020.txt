employee worked was entered as 400 instead of 40 hours. Fortunately, most information 
systems check to make sure that data falls within certain ranges. For number of hours worked, 
the range might be from 0 to 100 hours because it is unlikely that an employee would work 
more than 100 hours in a week. The information system would determine that 400 hours is out 
of range and provide feedback. The feedback is used to check and correct the input on the 
number of hours worked to 40.
