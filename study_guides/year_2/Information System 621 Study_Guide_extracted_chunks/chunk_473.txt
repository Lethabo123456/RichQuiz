6.3 The Internet 
 
The Internet is the world’s largest computer network. Actually, the Internet is a collection of 
interconnected networks, all freely exchanging information. Nobody knows exactly how big 
the Internet is because it is a collection of separately run, smaller computer networks. There is 
no single place where all the connections are registered. Figure 6.15 shows the staggering 
growth of the Internet, as measured by the number of Internet host sites or domain names.
