Developing a seamless integration of traditional databases with the Internet is often called a 
‘semantic web’. A semantic web allows people to access and manipulate a number of traditional 
databases at the same time through the Internet. Many software vendors – including IBM, 
Oracle, Microsoft, Macromedia, Inline Internet Systems and Netscape Communications – are 
incorporating the capability of the Internet into their products. Such databases allow companies 
to create an Internet-accessible catalogue, which is nothing more than a database of items, 
descriptions and prices.
