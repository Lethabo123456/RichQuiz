Automated Teller Machine (ATM) Devices 
Another type of special-purpose input/output device, the automated teller machine (ATM), is a 
terminal that bank customers use to perform transactions with their bank accounts. Companies 
use various ATM devices, sometimes called kiosks, to support their business processes. Some 
can dispense tickets, such as for airlines, concerts and football games. Some colleges use them 
to produce transcripts.
