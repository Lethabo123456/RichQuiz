The Microsoft Surface platform is designed to help people learn, collaborate and make 
decisions. The Surface can be used as a tablet, mounted on the wall, or embedded in furniture 
or fixtures. Its large 40-inch screen is an effective way to share photos, maps, modelling and 
simulations. The Surface allows a single user or multiple users to manipulate digital content 
by motion of their hands.
