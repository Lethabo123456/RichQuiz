Aspect ratio and screen size describe the size of the display screen. Aspect ratio is the ratio of 
the width of the display to its height. An aspect ratio of 4 to 3 is common. For widescreen 
LCD monitors used for viewing DVD movies in widescreen format, playing games or 
displaying multiple screens side by-side, an aspect ratio of 16 to 10 or 15 to 9 is preferred. 
The screen size is measured diagonally from the outside of the screen casing for CRT 
monitors and from the inside of the screen casing for LCD displays.
