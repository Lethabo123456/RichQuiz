Many companies use off-the-shelf software to support business processes. Key questions for 
selecting off-the-shelf software include the following. First, will the software run on the OS 
and hardware you have selected? Second, does the software meet the essential business 
requirements that have been defined? Third, is the software manufacturer financially solvent 
and reliable? Finally, does the total cost of purchasing, installing and maintaining the software 
compare favourably to the expected business benefits?
