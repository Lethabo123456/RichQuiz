A geostationary satellite can be accessed using a dish antenna aimed at the spot in the sky where 
the satellite hovers. A low earth orbit (LEO) satellite system employs many satellites, each in 
a circular orbit at an altitude of a few hundred kilometers. The satellites are spaced so that from 
any point on the Earth at any time, at least one satellite is on a line of sight.
