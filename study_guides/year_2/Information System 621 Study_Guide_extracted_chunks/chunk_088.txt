Project and Team Organizational Structures 
A project organizational structure is centred on major products or services. For example, in 
a manufacturing firm that produces baby food and other baby products, each line is produced 
by a separate unit. Traditional functions such as marketing, finance and production are 
positioned within these major units (see Figure 2.4). Many project teams are temporary – when 
the project is complete, the members go on to new teams formed for another project.
