Can You Solve It? 
In the 2014 film The Imitation Game, Alan Turing is shown recruiting code breakers by 
publishing a crossword in the Daily Telegraph with a message asking people who could solve 
it in ten minutes or fewer to get in touch. This is based on a real event. The actual crossword 
used was published on 13 January 1942 and gave readers 12 minutes not 10. 
Clues included ‘when hammering take care not to hit this (5,4)’ and ‘those well brought up 
help these over stiles (4,4)’. It might seem like a strange way to apply for a job, but puzzles are
