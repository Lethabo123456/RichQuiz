identify particular targets, actual individuals, often on social media, who work for a particular firm, and then 
contact them with an email that appears to come from a friend.

to see his company email address, or they may be able to guess this – its often just 
‘first_name@company.com’. They send him an email, with content tailored to arouse his interest. The
