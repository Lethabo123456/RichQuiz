In the late 1980s and early 1990s, overall productivity did not seem to improve as a company 
increased its investments in information systems. Often called the productivity paradox, this 
situation troubled many economists who were expecting to see dramatic productivity gains. In 
the early 2000s, however, productivity again seemed to be on the rise. 
 
Return on Investment and the Value of Information Systems 
One measure of IS value is return on investment (ROI). This measure investigates the 
additional profits or benefits that are generated as a percentage of the investment in IS 
technology. A small business that generates an additional profit of €20 000 for the year as a 
result of an investment of €100 000 for additional computer equipment and software would
