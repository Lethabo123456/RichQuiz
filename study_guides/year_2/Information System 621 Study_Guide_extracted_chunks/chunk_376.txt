to one (1 : 1), one to many (1 : M) or many to many (M : M). 
 
Last, the optionality documents whether the relationship must exist for each entity or whether 
it is optional. For instance, an order must be placed by a customer – there is no option. An order 
can’t exist unless a customer has placed it! However, a customer can be in the database even 
though they have no current orders, so the relationship is optional for the customer. All of the
