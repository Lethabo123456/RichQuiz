Mobio, an international mobile payments and marketing company, has developed an iPhone 
application that it is testing with fans of the Jacksonville Jaguars football team. The fans use 
the application to scan a barcode at their seat and a menu of food and beverage items is 
displayed on their phone. Fans then select and pay for the items they want using the iPhone 
application, and the order is then delivered to their seat.
