Ultra Wideband 
Ultra wideband (UWB) communications involves the transmission of extremely short 
electromagnetic pulses lasting just 50 to 1000 picoseconds. (One picosecond is one trillionth 
or one-millionth of one-millionth of a second.) The pulses are capable of supporting data 
transmission rates of 480 to 1320 Mbps over relatively short ranges of 10 to 50 metres.
