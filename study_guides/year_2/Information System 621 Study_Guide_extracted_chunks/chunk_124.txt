In general, IS professionals are charged with maintaining the broadest perspective on 
organizational goals. Most medium to large organizations manage information resources 
through an IS department. In smaller businesses, one or more people might manage information 
resources, with support from outsourced services. As shown in Figure 2.5, the IS department 
has three primary responsibilities: operations, systems development and support. 
 
Operations
