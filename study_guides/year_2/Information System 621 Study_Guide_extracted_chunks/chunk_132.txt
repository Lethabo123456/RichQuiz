N Administrator 
Local area network (LAN) administrators set up and manage the network hardware, software 
and security processes. They manage the addition of new users, software and devices to the 
network. They also isolate and fix operational problems. LAN administrators are in high 
demand and often solve both technical and non-technical problems. Database administrators 
manage the use, maintenance and security of a company’s databases. Often a database 
administrator will help users extract the data they need in the format they require.
