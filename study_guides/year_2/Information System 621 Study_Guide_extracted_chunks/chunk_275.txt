In most cases, application software resides on the computer’s hard drive before it is brought 
into the computer’s memory and run. Application software can also be stored on CDs, DVDs 
and even USB flash drives. An increasing amount of application software is available on the 
web. Sometimes referred to as a rich Internet application (RIA), a web-delivered application 
combines hardware resources of the web server and the PC to deliver valuable software services 
through a web browser interface. Before a person, group or enterprise decides on the best 
approach for acquiring application software, they should analyze their goals and needs 
carefully.
