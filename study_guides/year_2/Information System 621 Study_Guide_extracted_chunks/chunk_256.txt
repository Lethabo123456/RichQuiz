They are now used more broadly for commercial purposes in the life sciences and the 
manufacture of drugs and new materials. For example, Procter & Gamble uses supercomputers 
in the research and development of many of its leading commercial brands such as Tide and 
Pampers to help develop detergent with more soap suds and improve the quality of its nappies.

(GPU) chips to perform high-speed processing.
