Google runs a server farm in Finland, using wind to power it and sea water to cool it. Google 
buys electricity from a wind farm in Northern Sweden, and brings in water from the Bay of 
Finland. The farm is based in an old paper mill which already had massive quarter-mile-long 
sea water tunnels, which had been used to cool the paper mill’s manufacturing systems.
