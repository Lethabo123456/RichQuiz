.A well-designed and well-managed database is central to almost all information systems and is 
an extremely valuable tool in supporting decision making. A DBMS is a group of programs used 
as an interface between a database and its users and other application programs. When an 
application program requests data from the database, it follows a logical access path. 
.The number and types of database applications will continue to evolve and yield real business 
benefits. Organizations are building data warehous3es, which are relational DBMSs specifically 
designed to support management decision making.
