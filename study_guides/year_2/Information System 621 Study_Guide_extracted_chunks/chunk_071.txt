■ Currency challenges: The value of different currencies can vary significantly over time, 
making international trade more difficult and complex.

car or bicycle, can be difficult to deliver to the global market. However, electronic products 
(eproducts) and electronic services (e-services) can be delivered to customers electronically, over 
the phone, networks, through the Internet or other electronic means. Software, music, books, manuals, 
and help and advice can all be delivered over the Internet.
