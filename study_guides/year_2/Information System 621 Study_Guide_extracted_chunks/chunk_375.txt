The relationship between customers and orders is definitely something that the firm will want 
to store, so that they can see which customers have placed which orders. Like identifying 
entities, identifying relationships between them is not trivial and may take several attempts to 
get right. 
 
Once you identify a relationship, there are three things you need to document about it: its 
degree, cardinality and optionality. 
 
The degree of a relationship is simply how many entities are involved, and this figure is often 
two. When the degree is two, it is known as a ‘binary relationship’.
