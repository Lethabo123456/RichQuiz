labelling programme known as Energy Star. It was conceived by the Environmental 
Protection Agency in 1992 to promote energy efficiency in hardware of all kinds. This 
programme resulted in the widespread adoption of sleep mode for electronic products. For 
example, Energy Star monitors have the capability to power down into two levels of ‘sleep’. In
