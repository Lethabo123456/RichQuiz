6.4 Internet Applications 
Many people believe the terms ‘Internet’ and ‘World Wide Web’ are synonymous. However 
the web, which is examined next, is just one application of the Internet. Others also discussed 
in this section are email, telnet and FTP. 
The World Wide Web 
The World Wide Web was developed by Tim Berners-Lee at CERN, the European 
Organization for Nuclear Research in Geneva. He originally conceived of it as an internal 
document-management system. From this modest beginning, the World Wide Web (web, 
WWW or W3) has grown to a collection of tens of thousands of independently owned 
computers that work together as one in an Internet service.
