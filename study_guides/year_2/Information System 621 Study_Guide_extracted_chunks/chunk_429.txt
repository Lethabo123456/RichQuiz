Fibre-Optic Cable 
Fibre-optic cable, consisting of many extremely thin strands of glass or plastic bound together 
in a sheathing (a jacket), transmits signals with light beams (see Figure 6.2c). These 
highintensity light beams are generated by lasers and are conducted along the transparent fibres.
