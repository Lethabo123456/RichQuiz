Scanning Devices 
Scanning devices capture image and character data. A page scanner is like a photocopier. You 
either insert a page into the scanner or place it face down on the glass plate of the scanner and 
then scan it. With a handheld scanner, you manually move or roll the scanning device over the 
image you want to scan. Both page and handheld scanners can convert monochrome or colour 
pictures, forms, text and other images into machine-readable digits. Considering that US 
enterprises generate an estimated 1 billion pieces of paper daily, many companies are looking
