Most ERP systems also have a purchasing subsystem that orders the needed items. In addition 
to these core business processes, some ERP systems can sup- port functions such as human 
resources, sales and distribution. The primary benefits of implementing an ERP system include 
easing adoption of improved work processes and increasing access to timely data for decision 
making.
