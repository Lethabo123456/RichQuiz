Introduction

been transformed almost beyond all recognition, and many workers could not operate without 
their computer. All of this happened before the rise in popularity of the Internet as a channel 
for sharing information. When that happened, the Internet changed everything all over again!

information systems every day. Marketing departments, accounts departments, order 
processing, shipping and logistics all rely on information systems. Researchers, medical 
doctors, and mechanics – it is difficult to think of a profession where the computer does not
