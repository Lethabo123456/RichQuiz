What Is An Information System? 
Now that we have defined the terms ‘system’ and ‘information’, we can define an information 
system: an information system (IS) is a set of interrelated components that collect, manipulate, 
store and disseminate information and provide a feedback mechanism to meet an objective. It 
is the feedback mechanism that helps organizations achieve their goals, such as increasing 
profits or improving customer service.
