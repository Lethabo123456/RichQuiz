2 Key operational: Key operational applications are essential. Without them the 
organization would not be able to do business. Transaction processing systems, mentioned in 
Chapter 1 and discussed fully in Chapter 8, are an example. If the checkout system at a Tesco 
shop malfunctions, Tesco would be unable to sell goods until it was repaired. The website of 
every e-commerce business is key operational.
