When two or more people work together to achieve a common goal, they form a workgroup. 
A workgroup might be a large formal, permanent organizational entity, such as a section or 
department or a temporary group formed to complete a specific project. An information 
system in the workgroup sphere of influence helps workgroup members attain their 
common goals. Often, software designed for the personal sphere of influence can extend into 
the workgroup sphere. For example, people can use online calendar software such as Google 
Calendar to store personal appointments but also to schedule meetings with others.
