Wi-Fi 
Wi-Fi is a wireless telecommunications technology brand owned by the Wi-Fi Alliance which 
consists of about 300 technology companies including AT&T, Dell, Microsoft, Nokia and 
Qualcomm. The Alliance exists to improve the interoperability of wireless local area network 
products based on the IEEE 802.11 series of telecommunications standards.
