Storage as a Service 
Storage as a service is a data storage model in which a data storage service provider rents 
space to people and organizations. Users access their rented data storage via the Internet. Such 
a service enables the users to store and backup their data without requiring a major investment 
to create and maintain their own data storage infrastructure. Businesses can also choose pay-
per-use services where they rent space on massive storage devices housed either at a service 
provider (such as Hewlett-Packard or IBM) or on the customers’ premises, paying only for the
