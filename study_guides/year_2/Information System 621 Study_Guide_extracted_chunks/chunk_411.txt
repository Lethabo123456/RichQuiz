Telecommunications systems link the computers so that users at all locations can access the

Distributed databases give organizations more flexibility in how databases are organized and 
used. Local offices can create, manage and use their own databases, and people at other offices 
can access and share the data in the local databases. Giving local sites more direct access to 
frequently used data can improve organizational effectiveness and efficiency significantly. The 
New York City Police Department, for example, has about 35 000 officers searching for 
information located in over 70 offices around the city. According to one database programmer,
