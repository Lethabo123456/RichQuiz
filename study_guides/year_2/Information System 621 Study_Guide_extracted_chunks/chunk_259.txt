that going green is in their best interests in terms of public relations, safety of employees and 
the community at large. They also recognize that green computing presents an opportunity to 
substantially reduce total costs over the lifecycle of their IS equipment. Green computing has 
three goals: reduce the use of hazardous material, allow companies to lower their power-
related costs, and enable the safe disposal or recycling of computers and computer-related 
equipment. According to Greenpeace, 50 million tons of computers, monitors, lap- tops, 
printers, disc drives, mobile phones, DVDs and CDs are discarded worldwide each year.
