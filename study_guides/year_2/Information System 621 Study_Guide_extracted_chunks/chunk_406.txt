Data mining is used extensively in marketing to improve customer retention; identify cross- 
selling opportunities; manage marketing campaigns; market, channel and pricing analysis; and 
customer segmentation analysis (especially one-to-one marketing). Data-mining tools help 
users find answers to questions they haven’t thought to ask.
