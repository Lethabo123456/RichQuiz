5.1 Data Management and Data Modelling

be processed to provide useful information. A database is used by almost every firm to record 
a history of that firm’s transactions. This historical data can be hugely useful in uncovering pat- 
terns and relationships the firm had never even considered before, a practice known as ‘data 
mining’, something that is explained later in this chapter. The most common type of database
