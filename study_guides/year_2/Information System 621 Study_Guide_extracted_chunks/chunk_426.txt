Channel Bandwidth 
Telecommunications channel bandwidth refers to the rate at which data is exchanged, usually 
measured in bits per second (bps) – the broader the bandwidth, the more information can be 
exchanged at one time. Broadband communications can exchange data very quickly, as 
opposed to narrowband communications which supports a much lower rate of data exchange. 
Tele- communications professionals consider the capacity of the channel when they 
recommend transmission media for a business. In general, today’s organizations need more 
bandwidth for increased transmission speed to carry out their daily functions. To increase 
bandwidth, first consider the different types of telecommunications media you can use.
