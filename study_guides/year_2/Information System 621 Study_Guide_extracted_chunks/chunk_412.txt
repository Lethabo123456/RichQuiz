Despite its advantages, distributed processing creates additional challenges in integrating 
different databases (information integration), maintaining data security, accuracy, timeliness 
and conformance to standards. Distributed databases allow more users direct access at different 
sites; thus controlling who accesses and changes data is sometimes difficult. Also, because 
distributed databases rely on telecommunications lines to transport data, access to data can be 
slower.
