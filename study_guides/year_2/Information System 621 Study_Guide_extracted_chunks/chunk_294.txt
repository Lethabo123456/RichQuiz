Linux is available on the Internet and from other sources. Popular versions include Red Hat 
Linux, OpenSUSE and Caldera OpenLinux. Several large computer vendors, including IBM, 
Hewlett-Packard and Intel, support the Linux operating system. Although Linux is free 
software, Red Hat had revenues of about $0.75 billion in 2011 distributing and servicing the 
software.
