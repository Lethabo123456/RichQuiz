cable makes it ideal when there is not room for bulky copper wires for example, in crowded 
conduits, which can be pipes or spaces carrying both electrical and communications wires. In

fibre-optic cables are immune to electrical interference, they can transmit signals over longer 
distances with fewer expensive repeaters to amplify or rebroadcast the data. Fibre-optic cable 
and associated telecommunications devices are more expensive to purchase and install than 
their twisted-pair wire counterparts, although the cost is decreasing.
