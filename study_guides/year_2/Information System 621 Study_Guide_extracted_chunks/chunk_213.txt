A plasma display uses thousands of smart cells (pixels) consisting of electrodes and neon 
and xenon gases that are electrically turned into plasma (electrically charged atoms and 
negatively charged particles) to emit light. The plasma display lights up the pixels to form an 
image based on the information in the video signal. Each pixel is made up of three types of 
light red, green and blue with the plasma display varying the intensities of the lights to 
produce a full range of colours. Plasma displays can produce high resolution and accurate 
representation of colours to create a high-quality image.
