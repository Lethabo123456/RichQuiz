transmission and short distances – under a few hundred metres. Infrared transmission can be 
used to connect a display screen, a printer and a mouse to a computer, meaning there are no 
wires to clutter up the desk. Some special-purpose phones can also use infrared transmission. 
You can use infrared to establish a wireless network, with the advantage that devices can be 
moved, removed and installed without expensive wiring and network connections. The Apple 
remote is a remote control made for use with Apple products with infrared capabilities. It has 
just six buttons: Menu, Play/Pause, Volume Up, Volume Down, Previous/Rewind and 
Next/Fast-forward. The Mac Mini features an infrared port designed to work with the Apple 
remote and supports Front Row, a multimedia application that allows users to access shared 
iTunes and iPhoto libraries and video throughout their home.
