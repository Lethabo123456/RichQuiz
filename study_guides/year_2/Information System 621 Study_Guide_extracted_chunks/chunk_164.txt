3.3 Secondary Storage 
 
Storing data safely and effectively is critical to an organization’s success. Driven by many 
factors such as needing to retain more data longer to meet government regulatory concerns, 
storing new forms of digital data such as audio and video, and keeping systems running under 
the onslaught of increasing volumes of email – the estimated amount of data that companies 
store digitally is growing so rapidly that by 2020 storage requirements will be 44 times what 
they were in 2009 (an average compounded growth rate of 42 per cent per year).13 International 
Data Corporation (IDC) estimates that more than 1.8 zettabytes (1021 bytes) of information 
was created and stored in 2011 alone.
