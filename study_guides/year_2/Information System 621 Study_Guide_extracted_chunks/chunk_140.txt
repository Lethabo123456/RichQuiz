Introduction 
 
Today’s use of technology is practical – it’s intended to yield real business benefits. Using the 
latest information technology and providing additional processing capabilities can increase 
employee productivity, expand business opportunities and allow for more flexibility. This 
chapter concentrates on the hardware component of a computer-based information system 
(CBIS). Recall that hardware refers to the physical components of a computer that perform the 
input, processing, output and storage activities of the computer. When making hardware
