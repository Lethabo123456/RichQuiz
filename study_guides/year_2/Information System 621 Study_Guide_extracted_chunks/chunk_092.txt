Successful virtual organizational structures share key characteristics. One strategy is to have 
in-house employees concentrate on the firm’s core businesses and use virtual employees, 
groups or businesses to do everything else. Using information systems to manage the activities 
of a virtual structure is essential, often requiring specialized software to coordinate joint work. 
Even with sophisticated IS tools though, teams may still need face-to-face meetings, especially 
at the beginning of new projects.
