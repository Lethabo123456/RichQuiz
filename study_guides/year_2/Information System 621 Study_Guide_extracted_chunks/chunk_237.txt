Tablet Computers 
Tablet computers are portable, lightweight computers with no keyboard that allow you to 
roam the office, home or factory floor, carrying the device like a clipboard. You can enter text 
with a writing stylus or finger directly on the screen thanks to built-in handwriting recognition 
software. Other input methods include an optional keyboard or speech recognition. Tablet PCs 
that support input only via a writing stylus are called slate compute
