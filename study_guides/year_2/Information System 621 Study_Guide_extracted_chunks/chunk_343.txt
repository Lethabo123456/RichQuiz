The Evolution of Programming Languages 
 
The desire for faster, more efficient, more powerful information processing has pushed the 
development of new programming languages. The evolution of programming languages is 
typically discussed in terms of generations of languages (see Table 4.7). 
 
 
Visual, Object-Oriented and Artificial Intelligence Languages 
 
Today, programmers often use visual and object-oriented languages. In the future, they may be 
using artificial intelligence languages to a greater extent. In general, these languages are easier 
for nonprogrammers to use, compared with older generation languages.
