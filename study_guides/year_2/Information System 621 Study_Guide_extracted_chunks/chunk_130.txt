The Chief Information Officer 
The role of the chief information officer (CIO) is to employ an IS department’s equipment and 
personnel to help the organization attain its goals. The CIO is a senior manager concerned with 
the overall needs of the organization who sets organization-wide policies, and plans, manages 
and acquires information systems.
