Digital Cameras 
 
Digital cameras record and store images or video in digital form, so when you take pictures, 
the images are electronically stored in the camera. You can download the images to a computer 
either directly or by using a flash memory card. After you store the images on the computer’s 
hard disc, you can then edit and print them, send them to another location or paste them into 
another application. This digital format saves time and money by eliminating the need to 
process film in order to share photos. For example, you can download a photo of your project
