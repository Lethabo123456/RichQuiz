Altering the Industry Structure 
Changing the industry to become more favourable to the company or organization is another 
strategy company’s use. The introduction of low-fare airline carriers, such as EasyJet, has 
forever changed the airline industry, making it difficult for traditional airlines to make high 
profit margins. To fight back, airlines such as British Airways cut their flight prices and started 
to emphasize their strengths over low-cost airlines in their advertising. These include landing 
in central airports rather than airports many miles out of the city they supposedly serve, and
