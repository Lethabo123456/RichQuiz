In contrast to reengineering, the idea of continuous improvement is to constantly seek ways 
to improve business processes and add value to products and services. This continual change 
will increase customer satisfaction and loyalty and ensure long-term profitability. 
Manufacturing companies make continual product changes and improvements. Service 
organizations regularly find ways to provide faster and more effective assistance to customers. 
By doing so, these companies increase customer loyalty, minimize the chance of customer 
dissatisfaction and diminish the opportunity for competitive inroads. Table 2.2 compares these 
two strategies.
