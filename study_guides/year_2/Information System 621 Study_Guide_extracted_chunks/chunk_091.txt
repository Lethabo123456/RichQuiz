In addition to reducing costs or increasing revenues, a virtual organizational structure can 
provide an extra level of security. For instance, dispersing employees and using a virtual 
structure can provide an ability to deal with a disaster at the primary location. If this happened, 
the company would still have sufficient employees at other locations to keep the business 
running. Today’s workers are performing company work at home, at a customer’s location, in 
coffee shops, on pleasure boats and at convenient work centres in suburbia. People can work 
at any time. Using the Internet and email, workers can put the finishing touches to a new 
business proposal in Europe or Asia, while co-workers in North America are sleeping.
