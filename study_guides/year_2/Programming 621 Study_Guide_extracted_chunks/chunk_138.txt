Consider the following statement:  
int list[10]; 
A loop such as the following can set the index of list out of bounds: 
 for (int i = 0; i <= 10; i++)  
          list[i] = 0;  
When i becomes 10, the loop test condition i <= 10 evaluates to true and the body of 
the loop executes, which results in storing 0 in list[10]. Logically, list[10] does not exist
