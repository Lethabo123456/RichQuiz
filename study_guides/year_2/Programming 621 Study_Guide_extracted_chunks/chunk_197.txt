myClock and yourClock can access only public members of the class clockType. Thus, 
the following statements are illegal because hr and min are declared as private 
members of the class clockType and, therefore, cannot be accessed by the objects 
myClock and yourClock: 
myClock.hr = 10;                                         //illegal  
myClock.min = yourClock.min;                   //illegal
