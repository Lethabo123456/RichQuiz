to keep track of their arrival and departure times. You can declare two arrays— 
arrivalTimeEmp and departureTimeEmp—of 100 components each, wherein each 
component is an object of type clockType 
 
Consider the following statement:  
clockType arrivalTimeEmp[100];                //Line 1
