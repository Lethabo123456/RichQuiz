If str1 = "Hello there", the statement:  
str1[6] = 'T';  
replaces the character t with the character T. Recall that the position of the first 
character in a string variable is 0. Therefore, because t is the seventh character in str1, 
its position is 6. In C++, [] is called the array subscript operator.
