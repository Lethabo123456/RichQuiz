7. In the Unified Modeling Language (UML) diagram of a class, the top box 
contains the name of the class. The middle box contains the member variables 
and their data types. The last box contains the member function name, 
parameter list, and the return type of the function. A + (plus) sign in front of a 
member name indicates that the member is a public member. A - (minus) sign 
preceding a member name indicates that the member a private member. The 
symbol # before the member name indicates that the member is a protected 
member. 
8. Constructors guarantee that the member variables are initialized when an
