Because a clock gives the time of day, let us call this class clockType. Furthermore, 
to represent time in computer memory, we use three int variables: one to represent 
the hours, one to represent the minutes, and one to represent the seconds. 
Suppose these three variables are: 
 int hr; 
 int min; 
 int sec;

4. Increment the time by one second.  
5. Increment the time by one minute.  
6. Increment the time by one hour.
