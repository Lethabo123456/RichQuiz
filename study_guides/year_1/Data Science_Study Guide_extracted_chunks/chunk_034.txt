2.1 Introduction 
Now that we have a fundamental understanding of data science concepts let's take a look at the 
various ways in which data can be formed. Specifically, in this chapter we examine the three 
fundamental classifications of data: 
• Structured versus unstructured data 
• Quantitative versus qualitative data 
• The four levels of data 
 
We will discuss each of these topics by showing examples of how data scientists analyse and work 
with data. This chapter is aimed to familiarize you with the fundamental concepts essential to data 
science. Understanding different types of data is critical in this industry for various reasons. Knowing 
whether the data is unstructured or possibly quantitative can also tell us a lot about the actual 
phenomena being examined and not just to determine the methods used to analyse and extract 
findings from it (Sinan, 2016).
