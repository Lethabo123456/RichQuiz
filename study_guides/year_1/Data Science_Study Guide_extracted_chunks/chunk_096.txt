5.1 Introduction  
Probability is a fundamental concept in data science that quantifies the likelihood of events occurring. 
In the context of data analysis, probability helps us understand uncertainty and make informed 
decisions. It provides a framework for reasoning about uncertain events based on available data. 
Probability theory involves mathematical tools and principles that assign numeric values, ranging from 
0 to 1, to events. A probability of 0 indicates the event will not occur, while a probability of 1 implies 
the event is certain to occur. The concept of probability is used to describe both simple events, such 
as the outcome of a coin toss, as well as complex events, such as the occurrence of certain patterns in 
a dataset (Chohan, 2019). 
 
In the context of data science, probability is used to model random variables and processes, allowing 
us to make predictions and infer information about the underlying data generating mechanisms. It 
provides a basis for statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and decision-making. Understanding 
probability enables data scientists to build robust models, generate reliable predictions, and quantify 
uncertainty in their analyses. In this chapter, we will look at the following topics: 
 
• What is probability? 
• The differences between the Frequentist approach and the Bayesian approach 
• How to visualize probability 
• How to utilize the rules of probability?
