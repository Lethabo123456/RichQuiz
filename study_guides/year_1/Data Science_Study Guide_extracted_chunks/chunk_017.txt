1.1 What is Data Science? 
Data Science is an interdisciplinary field that involves extracting valuable insights and knowledge from 
data. It combines techniques from various domains such as statistics, computer science, mathematics, 
and domain expertise to make data-driven decisions and predictions. With the increasing availability 
of data in today's digital world, Data Science has become a critical tool for businesses, researchers, 
and organizations to gain a competitive advantage, improve processes, and solve complex problems.
