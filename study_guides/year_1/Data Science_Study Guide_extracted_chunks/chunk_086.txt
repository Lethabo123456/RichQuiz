Advantages of Unsupervised Machine Learning: 
• No Labels Required: Unsupervised learning does not rely on labelled data, making it more 
scalable and applicable to a wide range of datasets. 
• Data Exploration: It can help discover patterns, structures, and relationships within the data, 
even when the underlying structure is not well-defined. 
• Anomaly Detection: Unsupervised learning can identify unusual patterns or outliers in the 
data, which can be valuable for anomaly detection and fraud detection (Valcheva, 2022). 
 
Disadvantages of Unsupervised Machine Learning: 
• Lack of Clear Objectives: Without predefined labels, it can be challenging to evaluate the 
performance of unsupervised learning algorithms objectively. 
• Interpretability: Unsupervised learning models are often more complex and harder to 
interpret compared to supervised models. 
• Subjectivity: The interpretation of the results from unsupervised learning can be subjective 
and may require domain expertise for meaningful analysis. 
• Difficulty in Validation: Due to the absence of ground truth labels, validating the performance 
of unsupervised learning models can be difficult (Valcheva, 2022).

notion is synonymous with making a line of best fit. In the case of linear regression, we will attempt 
to find a linear relationship between our predictors and our response variable (Sinan, 2016). Formally,
