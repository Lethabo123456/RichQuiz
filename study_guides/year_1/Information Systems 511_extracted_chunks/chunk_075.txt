3.3 
INSIDE THE SYSTEM UNIT 
 
Fig 3.14 
 
The system unit includes the chassis, microprocessor, main memory, bus, and ports, but does not 
include the keyboard or monitor, or any peripheral devices. The system unit which houses 
electronic components is a box-like case that is made of plastic or metal and is designed to protect 
the components from damage. On a desktop computer, the electronic components and storage 
devices are inside the unit and the peripherals are situated on the outside. However, on a laptop 
most of the components are housed inside.
