A graphical user interface (GUI) is a type of user interface item that allows people to interact 
with programs in more ways than typing such as computers; hand-held devices such as MP3 
Players, Portable Media Players or Gaming devices; household appliances and office equipment 
with images rather than text commands. A GUI offers graphical icons, and visual indicators, as 
opposed to text-based interfaces, typed command labels or text navigation to fully represent 
the information and actions available to a user.
