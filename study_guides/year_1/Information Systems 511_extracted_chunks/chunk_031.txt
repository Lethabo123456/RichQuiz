A hotspot  is a wireless network that provides Internet connections to users in public locations 
such as airports and airplanes, train stations, hotels, conversation centers, etc. 
 
 Networks 
 
A network is a collection of computers and devices connected together, often wirelessly via 
communication devices and transmission media. Networks allow computers to share resources 
such as hardware, software, data, and information. Advantages of sharing resources saves 
time and money.
