1.1 COMPUTER TODAY 
 
The term "computer" was originally given to humans (human computers) who performed 
numerical calculations using mechanical calculators, such as the abacus and slide rule. ...  
 
Today’s computers are an electronic device, operating under the control of instructions stored 
in its own memory, that can accept data(input), processes the data according to specified rules, 
produce  information (output), and store the results or information for future use. Computers 
process data to create information. Data is a collection of raw unprocessed facts, figures, and 
symbols. Information is data that is organized, meaningful, and useful. To process data into 
information, a computer uses hardware and software.
