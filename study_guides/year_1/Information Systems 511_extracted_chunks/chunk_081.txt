3.5 MEMORY 
Memory consists of electronic components that store instructions waiting to be executed by the 
processor, data needed by those instructions, and the results of processing the data. 
Memory stores three basic catergories of items: 
 The operating system and other programs that control or maintain the computer and its 
devices 
 Applications that carry out specific task, such as word processing 
 The data being processed by the applications and the resulting information 
 
Memory size commonly is measured in gigabytes (GB) or terabytes (TB).
