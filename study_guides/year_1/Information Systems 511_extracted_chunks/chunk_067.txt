3.2.3 Desktop Computers 
A desktop or desktop computer is a personal computer designed to be in a stationary location, 
where all of its components fit on or under a desk or table. An all-in-one (AIO) or all-in-one 
desktop, by contrast, does not have a tower and instead houses the display, system unit, and 
possibly an optical drive, in the same case.

3.2.4 Servers 
A server is a computer dedicated to providing one or more services to other computers or devices 
on a network. Services provided by servers include storing content and controlling access to 
hardware, software and other resources on a network. In many cases servers assess data, 
information, and programs on another server.
