2.4.2 Types of Websites 
 
 Web Search Engine 
A web search engine is software that finds websites, webpages, images, videos, news, maps, and 
other information related to a specific topic. Some search engines, such as Bing, Google, and 
Yahoo!, are helpful in locating information on the web for which you do not know an exact web 
address or are not seeking a specific websitye. A subject directory classifies webpages in an 
organized set of categories, such as sports or shopping, and related subcategories. 
Depending on your search text, search engines may respond with thousands to billions of search 
results, sometimes called hits.
