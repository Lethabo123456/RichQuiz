 Three popular types of messages that you can send with smart phone 
 Text Messages 
It is a short note, typically fewer than 300 charecters, sent to or from a smartphone or 
other mobile device. 
 Picture message 
It is a photo or other  image, sometimes along with sound and text, sent to or from a 
smartphone or other mobile device. A phone that can send picture messages is called 
a camera phone. 
 Video message 
Is a short video clip, usually about 30 seconds, sent to or from a smartphone or other 
mobile device
