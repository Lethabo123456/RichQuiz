4.4.4 Changing the Default Printer  
  
Your system always has a default printer, but you can change this by taking the following steps:  
 Choose start- printers and faxes. The printers and faxes window will open. Which printers 
are listed depends on how your system and network has been set up.  
 Select the printer that you want to make the default. (Perhaps you have changed your 
printer, installed another, or want to run a series of jobs on a printer that is currently not 
the default.)
