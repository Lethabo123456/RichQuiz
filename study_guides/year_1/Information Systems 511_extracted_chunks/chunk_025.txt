1.7.4 Environmental Issues 
Manufacturing processes for computers and mobile devices along with e-waste, or discarded 
computers and mobile devices, are depleting natural resoures and polluting the environment. 
Green computing involves reducing the electricity consumed and environmental waste  
generated when using a computer. Green computing is the environmentally responsible and 
eco-friendly use of computers and their resources. In broader terms, it is also defined as the 
study of designing, manufacturing/engineering, using and disposing of computing devices in a 
way that reduces their environmental impact.
