1.8.2 Applications 
Application software, usually simply called application (or app for short), consists of programs 
designed to make users more productive and/ or assist them with personal tasks. 
 

  
 
Fig 1.19 
Applications include programs stored on a computer, as well as those on a mobile device or 
delivered to your device over the Internet. Desktop app often is the term used to describe 
applications stored on a computer. Web app, is an application stored on a web server that you 
access through a browser. Mobile app is an application you download from a mobile device’s 
application store or other location on the Internet to a smartphone or other mobile device.
