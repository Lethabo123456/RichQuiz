For some models we may need a graph where some edges are undirected, 
while others are directed. A graph with both directed and undirected edges 
is called a mixed graph. For example, a mixed graph might be used to model 
a computer network containing links that operate in both directions and 
other links that operate only in one direction.

Two vertices u and v in an undirected graph G are called adjacent

is called incident with the vertices u and v and e is said to connect u and v.

the neighbourhood of v. If A is a subset of V , we denote by N(A) the set of
