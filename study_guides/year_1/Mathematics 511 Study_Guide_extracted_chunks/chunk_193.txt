Definition 45 
A square matrix A is called symmetric if A = At . Thus A = [aij ] is symmetric if 
aij = aji for all i and j with 1 ≤ i ≤ n and 1 ≤ j ≤ n. 

Note that a matrix is symmetric if and only if it is square and it is symmetric 
with respect to its main diagonal (which consists of entries that are in the
