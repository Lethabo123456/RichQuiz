 4.6.1 A Shortest-Path Algorithm 
There are several different algorithms that find a shortest path between two 
vertices in a weighted graph. We will present a greedy algorithm discovered 
by the Dutch mathematician Edsger Dijkstra in 1959. The version we will 
describe solves this problem in undirected weighted graph where all the 
weights are positive. It is easy to adapt it to solve shortest-path problems in 
directed graphs. 
Before giving a formal presentation of the algorithm, we will give an 
illustrative example.
