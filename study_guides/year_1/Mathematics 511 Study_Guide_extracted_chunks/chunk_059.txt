We can also have statements that involve more than one variable. For 
instance, consider the statement “𝑥 =  𝑦 +  3. ” We can denote this 
statement by 𝑄(𝑥, 𝑦), where 𝑥 and 𝑦 are variables and 𝑄 is the predicate. 
When values are assigned to the variables 𝑥 and 𝑦, the statement 𝑄(𝑥, 𝑦) 
has a truth value.
