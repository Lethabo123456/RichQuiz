Worked Example 
What are the contrapositive, the converse, and the inverse of the 
conditional statement “The home team wins whenever it is raining?” 

Solution:  
Because “𝑞 whenever 𝑝” is one of the ways to express the conditional 
statement 𝑝 → 𝑞, the original statement can be rewritten as: 

“If it is raining, then the home team wins.” 
Consequently, the contrapositive of this conditional statement is: 

“If the home team does not win, then it is not raining.” 
The converse is: 
“If the home team wins, then it is raining.” 
The inverse is: 
“If it is not raining, then the home team does not win.” 
Only the contrapositive is equivalent to the original statement.
