Worked Example 
Let R = {(1, 1), (2, 1), (3, 2), (4, 3)}. Find the powers Rn, n = 2, 3, 4, . . . . 
Solution:  
Because R2 = R ∘R, we find that R2 = {(1, 1), (2, 1), (3, 1), (4, 2)}. 
Furthermore, because R3 = R2 ∘R, R3 = {(1, 1), (2, 1), (3, 1), (4, 1)}.  
Additional computation shows that R4 is the same as R3, so R4 = {(1, 1), (2, 
1), (3, 1), (4, 1)}.  
It also follows that Rn = R3 for n = 5, 6, 7, . . .
