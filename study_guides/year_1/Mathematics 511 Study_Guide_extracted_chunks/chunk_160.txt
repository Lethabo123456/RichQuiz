The difference of {1, 3, 5} and {1, 2, 3} is the set {5}; that is, {1, 3, 5} − {1, 2,

Definition 21 
Let U be the universal set. The complement of the set A, denoted by 𝐴̅ is the 
complement of A with respect to U. Therefore, the complement of the set A is U − 
A. 
 
An element belongs to 𝐴̅ if and only if x ∉ A. This tells us that: 
𝐴̅ = {x ∈ U | x ∉ A}. 
In Figure 2.10 the shaded area outside the circle representing A is the area 
representing 𝐴̅. We give some examples of the complement of a set. 
Let A = {a, e, i, o, u} (where the universal set is the set of letters of the English 
alphabet). Then 𝐴 ̅= {b, c, d, f, g, h, j, k, l, m, n, p, q, r, s, t, v, w, x, y, z}.
