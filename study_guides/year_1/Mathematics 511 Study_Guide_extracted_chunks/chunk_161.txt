3.2.1 Set Identities 
Table 2 lists the most important set identities. We will prove several of these 
identities here, using different methods. These methods are presented to 
illustrate that there are often many different approaches to the solution of 
a problem. One way to show that two sets are equal is to show that each is 
a subset of the other. We generally use a direct proof to do this. We illustrate 
this type of proof by establishing the first of De Morgan’s laws.
