Cycles 
A cycle Cn, n ≥ 3, consists of n vertices v1, v2, . . . , vn and edges {v1, v2}, {v2, 
v3}, . . . , {vn−1,  
vn}, and {vn, v1}. The cycles C3, C4, C5, and C6 are displayed 
in Figure 4.7. 
 
Figure 4.8: The Cycles C3, C4, C5, and C6. 
 
Wheels 
We obtain a wheel Wn when we add an additional vertex to a cycle Cn,             
for n ≥ 3, and connect this new vertex to each of the n vertices in Cn, by new 
edges. The wheels W3, W4, W5, and W6 are displayed in Figure 4.9.
