Figure 4.15: Multigraph Model of the Town of Königsberg. 

The problem of traveling across every bridge without crossing any bridge 
more than once can be rephrased in terms of this model. The question 
becomes: Is there a simple circuit in this multigraph that contains every 
edge? 
 
Definition 64  
An Euler circuit in a graph G is a simple circuit containing every edge of G. An 
Euler path in G is a simple path containing every edge of G. 
 

Worked Example 
Which of the undirected graphs below has an Euler circuit? Of those that 
do not, which have an Euler path? 
 
 
Solution: 
The graph G1 has an Euler circuit, for example, a, e, c, d, e, b, a. Neither 
of the graphs G2 or G3 has an Euler circuit (the reader should verify this). 
However, G3 has an Euler path, namely, a, c, d, e, b, d, a, b. G2 does not 
have an Euler path (as the reader should verify).
