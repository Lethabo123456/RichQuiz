A Full Adder is a combinational circuit that performs the addition of three 
bits (two significant bits and previous carry). 
 It consists of three inputs and two outputs, two inputs are the bits 
to be added, the third input represents the carry form the previous 
position. 
 The full adder is usually a component in a cascade of adders, which 
add 8, 16, etc, binary numbers. 
 The output 𝑠 is equal to 1 when only one input is equal to 1 or when 
all three inputs are equal to 1. 
 The output 𝑐𝑖+1 has a carry 1 if two or three inputs are equal to 1.
