Worked Example 
Find the Fibonacci numbers f2, f3, f4, f5, and f6. 
Solution: 
The recurrence relation for the Fibonacci sequence tells us that we find 
successive terms by adding the previous two terms. Because the initial 
conditions tell us that f0 = 0 and f1 = 1,using the recurrence relation in the 
definition we find that: 
𝑓2 = 𝑓1 + 𝑓0 = 1 + 0 = 1 
𝑓3 = 𝑓2 + 𝑓1 = 1 + 1 = 2 
𝑓4 = 𝑓3 + 𝑓2 = 2 + 1 = 3 
𝑓5 = 𝑓4 + 𝑓3 = 3 + 2 = 5 
𝑓6 = 𝑓5 + 𝑓4 = 5 + 3 = 8
