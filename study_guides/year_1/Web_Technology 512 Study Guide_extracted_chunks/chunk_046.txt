3.1 JAVASCRIPT VARIABLES 
 
In programming terminology, a variable is a storage area. You store things in variables 
so that you can retrieve them later. Think of variables as small boxes. You can write a 
number on a piece of paper then place that piece of paper in a box. Write a label on 
the box, such as "Mom's phone number", and you'll have a quick way to identify what 
kind of information you have stored in the box. One day, you might even decide to 
phone Mom. If so, you can quickly locate her phone number by looking at the label on 
the box and pulling out the piece of paper with her number on it. 
 
In JavaScript, when you are setting up a variable you start with the letters var (short for 
variable, of course). After a space, you then need to come up with a name for your 
variable (like "Mom's phone number"). The name can be almost anything you want.
