2.4 THE BROWSER OBJECT MODEL  
 
All browsers are split into different parts (objects) 
that can be accessed using JavaScript. Collectively, 
these parts are known as the Browser Object 
Model, or the BOM. At the very top of this 
browser hierarchy is the Window object. This 
represents the entire browser, with its toolbars
