3.3 OPERATOR PRECEDENCE 
 
You can have more than two numbers for your math, of course. You can even mix 
variables and actual numbers. Change the total line of your code to this: 
total = number1 + number2 / 2; 
 
This time, we have a division symbol and a 2 at the end. What do you think will happen 
when you run this code? You may think that JavaScript adds up the two values in our 
variables first and then divides by 2. But it does not. 
 
To clarify what it is you want to do, you can use round brackets. If you want to do the 
addition first then the division, surround the addition with round brackets. Like this: 
total = ( number1 + number2 ) / 2;
