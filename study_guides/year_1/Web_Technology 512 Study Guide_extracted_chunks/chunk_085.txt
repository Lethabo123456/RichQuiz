3.10.1 For Loops 
For loops (lowercase "f") syntax: 
 
 
for (start_value; end_value; increament_value) 
 
{ 
 
//code to be executed over and over again comes here 
 
} 
 
So you start with the word for. After a space you have a pair of round brackets. In 
between the round brackets you need three things, separated by semicolons: a start 
value for your loop, some way for the loop to end, and a way to get from one to the 
other, which is known as the increment value. 
Next is a pair of curly brackets. The code you want 
to execute repeatedly goes between the curly
