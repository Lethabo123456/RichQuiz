2.2.1 Confirm and Prompt 
As well as an alert box, you can have confirm and 
prompt boxes. Using a confirm box will get you 
two buttons, OK and Cancel. They are normally 
used with an IF Statements to detect which of the 
two buttons was clicked. You will learn about IF 
Statements in a later section, but the code for a 
Confirm box is just this: 
confirm("OK or Cancel?")
