You can start grabbing character from the end of string by typing a negative number. 
Hence -4 means grab 4 characters from the end of the string. Here is an example: 
var email = "meme@meme.com"; 
var ending = email.substr( -4 ); 
document.write(ending); 
 
This grabs the last 4 characters from the email address, which are .com.
