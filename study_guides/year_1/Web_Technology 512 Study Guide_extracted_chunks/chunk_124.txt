5.1 JAVASCRIPT STRINGS - CHANGING CASE 
 
A big part of your life as a programmer is 
manipulating strings of text. To help you with this, 
JavaScript comes with lots of inbuilt methods you 
can use. We will take a look these in this and the 
next few sections. Before you go any further, 
though, create a new web page with two SCRIPT 
tags in the HEAD section.

two SCRIPT tags:

var postcode = "sr2 4jh";
