But because the Window object is a Global object you do nothave to use window "dot" 
notation. You can just type the part after window. For clarity's sake, though, it is better 
to keep the window part wherever possible as it is clearer to you what your code 
means. 
 
Other window methods that are commonly used are these: 
 window.clearInterval 
 window.clearTimeout  
 window.close 
 window.open 
 window.print 
 window.setInterval 
 window.setTimeout 
 
The first two and the last two are used to set up and manipulate timers. Timers are 
very useful in animation. The close and open methods do what you would expect them 
to do - close a window and open a new one. The print method brings up an Operating 
System's print dialogue box. Let us have a look at the window.open method more 
closely.
