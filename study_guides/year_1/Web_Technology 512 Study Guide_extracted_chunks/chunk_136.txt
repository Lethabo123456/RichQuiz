5.5.1 The Javascript join Method 
 
You can join strings of text together with the join method. Suppose we wanted our 
email addresses to be separated by hyphens rather than commas. We can do it like 
this: 
var email = 
meme@email_1.com,him@email_2.com,her@email_3.com"; 
var email_array = email.split(','); 
var new_string = email_array.join('-'); 
document.write(new_string); 
 
The first two lines are the same. But look at the third line: 
var new_string = email_array.join('-'); 
 
Again, the join method uses a pair of round brackets with a delimiter inside of them. 
Each position in the email_array will now be joined together with hyphens.
