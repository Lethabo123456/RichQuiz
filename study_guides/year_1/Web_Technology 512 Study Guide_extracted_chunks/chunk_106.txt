Now load your web page into a browser. You should find that nothing happens: the 
alert between the two curly brackets of the function does not display. 
 
The reason the alert does not display is because the function has not been called into 
action. There are lots of ways to call a function. One way is via the onLoad event of 
the browser window. So add this to the BODY section of you HTML: 
<BODY onLoad = "myFunction()">
