Get the day of the month 
To get the day of the month, rather oddly you need getDate. This does not get you 
today's date but a number from 1 to 31, which is the day of the month. 
var day_of_month = the_date.getDate(); 
document.write( day_of_month + "<BR>" ); 
  
Get the weekday 
To get the day of the week you use getDay. A number from 0 to 6 will be returned, 
with 0 being Sunday and 6 being Saturday: 
var weekday = the_date.getDay(); 
document.write( weekday + "<BR>");
