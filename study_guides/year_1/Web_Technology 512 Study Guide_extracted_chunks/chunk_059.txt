Division and multiplication have equal priority. These two in turn have priority over 
addition and subtraction which, again, have equal priority. To clear up what this 
means, take a look at the following: 
var result = 32 - 16 / 8 
 
Because division has a higher priority than subtraction the first thing JavaScript does is 
to divide 16 by 8 to get 2. It then subtracts the 2 from 32 to get an answer of 30. 
 
Now put some round brackets in: 
var result = (32 - 16) / 8
