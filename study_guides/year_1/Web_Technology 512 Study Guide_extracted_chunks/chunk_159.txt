For the month value, January is 0 and December is 11. Hour values are from 0 to 23. 
 
6.2.5.1 Customised Dates 
Once you have a Date object you have quite a lot of inbuilt properties available to you. 
These allow you to get just the parts of the date and time that you require. Let us have 
a look at them. 
 
Get the year 
If you just want the year, add getFullYear to the end of your date object. Like this: 
var the_date = new Date(); 
var year = the_date.getFullYear(); 
document.write(year + "<BR>"); 
 
You can also use getUTCYear. There is a slight difference between the two: FullYear 
gets the locale year, the one we all get globally. But if it is 31st December and heading 
towards Jan 1 of the next year then the locale year could be wrong; UTCFullYear gets 
the universal year - in other words, it corrects for the end of year problem. It's up to 
you which one you want to use.
