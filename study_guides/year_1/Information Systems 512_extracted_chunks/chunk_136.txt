 Microwave 
Microwaves are radio waves that provide a high-speed signal transmission. Microwave 
transmission, often called fixed wireless, involves sending signals from one microwave station 
to another. A microwave station is an earth-based reflective dish that contains the antenna, 
transceivers, and other equipment necessary for microwave communications. Current users 
of microwave transmission include universities, hospitals, city governments, CATV providers, 
and phone companies. Homes and small businesses that do not have other high-speed 
Internet connections available in their area also opt for lower-cost fixed wireless plans.
