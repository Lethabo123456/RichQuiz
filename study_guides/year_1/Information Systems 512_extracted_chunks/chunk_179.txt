 Operational feasibility measures how well the proposed information system will 
work. Will the users like the new system? Will they use it? Will it meet their 
requirements? Will it cause any changes in their work environment? Is it secure? 
 
 Schedule feasibility measures whether the established deadlines for the project are 
reasonable. If a deadline is not reasonable, the project leader might make a new 
schedule. If a deadline cannot be extended, then the scope of the project might be 
reduced to meet a mandatory deadline.
