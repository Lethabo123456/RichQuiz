The following are different types of communication devices: 
 Digital Modems; Cable, DSL and ISDN 
A broadband modem, also called a digital modem, is a communications device that sends 
and receives data and information to and from a digital line.  
Three types of broadband modems are  
 cable modems,  
 DSL modems, and 
  ISDN modems.
