2.5 INFORMATION THEFT 
Information theft occurs when someone steals personal or confidential information. An 
information theft is often linked to other types of cybercrime. For example, a corrupt person 
may steal credit card numbers to make fraudulent purchases.

plaintext into unreadable ciphertext. Two basic types of encryption are private key and
