 Legal Software 
Legal software is an application that assists in the preparation of legal documents and 
provides legal information to individuals, families, and small businesses. Legal software 
provides standard contracts and documents associated with buying, selling, and renting 
property; estate planning; marriage and divorce; and preparing a will or living trust. 
 
 
 Tax Preparation Software

businesses through the process of filing federal and state taxes. These programs forecast tax
