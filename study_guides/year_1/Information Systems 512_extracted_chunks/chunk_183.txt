 Planning Phase 
The planning phase for a project begins when the steering committee receives a project 
request. This committee usually consists of five to nine people and typically includes a mix of 
vice presidents, managers, non-management users, and IT personnel. 
During the planning phase, four major activities are performed:  
(1)  
Review and approve the project requests,  
(2)  
prioritize the project requests,  
(3)  
Allocate resources, such as money, people, and equipment to approved projects, 
and  
(4)  
Form a project development team for each approved project. 
 
 Analysis Phase 
The analysis phase consists of two major activities:  
(1) conduct a preliminary investigation and  
(2) perform detailed analysis.
