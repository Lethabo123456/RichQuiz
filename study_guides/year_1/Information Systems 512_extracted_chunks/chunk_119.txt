 RFID 
RFID (radio frequency identification) is a protocol that defines how a network uses radio 
signals to communicate with a tag placed in or attached to an object, an animal, or a person. 
The tag, called a transponder, consists of an antenna and a memory chip that contains the 
information to be transmitted via radio waves. Through an antenna, an RFID reader, also 
called a transceiver, reads the radio signals and transfers the information to a computer or 
computing device.
