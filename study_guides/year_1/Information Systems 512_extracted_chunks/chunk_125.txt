 Wireless Modems 
Some mobile users have a wireless modem that uses a mobile service provider’s network to 
connect to the Internet wirelessly from a computer or mobile device. Some smartphones also 
can function as a wireless modem, called a mobile hot spot, when tethered to a personal 
computer or mobile device.
