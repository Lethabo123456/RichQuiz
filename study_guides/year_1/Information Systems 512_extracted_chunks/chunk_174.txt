System Development Guidelines 
System development should follow three general guidelines: group activities into 
phases, involve users, and define standards. 
 Group activities into phases.  
Many SDLCs contain the same phases shown in Figure 11-11. Others have more or fewer 
phases. Regardless, all system development cycles have similar activities and tasks. 
 Involve users.  
Users include anyone for whom the system is being built. Customers, employees, 
students, data entry specialists, accountants, sales managers, and owners all are 
examples of users. Users are more apt to accept a new system if they contribute to its 
design.
