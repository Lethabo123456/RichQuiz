WIRELESS TRANSMITTED  
Wireless transmission media send communications signals through the air or space. Many 
users opt for wireless transmission media because it is more convenient than installing 
cables. In addition to convenience, businesses use wireless transmission media in locations 
where it is impossible to install cables. 
Types of wireless transmission media used in communications include infrared, broadcast 
radio, cellular radio, microwaves, and communications satellites. 
 Infrared 
Infrared (IR) is a wireless transmission medium that sends signals using infrared light waves. 
Mobile computers and devices, such as a mouse, printer, and smartphone, may have an 
IrDA port that enables the transfer of data from one device to another using infrared light 
waves.
