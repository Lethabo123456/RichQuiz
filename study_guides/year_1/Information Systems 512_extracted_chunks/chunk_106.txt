WAN 
A wide area network (WAN) is a network that covers a large geographic area (such as a city, 
country, or the world) using a variety of wired and wireless transmission media. A WAN can 
be one large network or can consist of multiple LANs connected together. The Internet is 
the world’s largest WAN.
