The most popular T-carrier line is the T1 line. Businesses often use T1 lines to connect to the 
Internet. Home and small business users purchase fractional T1, in which they share a 
connection to the T1 line with other users. A T3 line is equal in speed to 28 T1 lines. T3 lines 
are quite expensive. Main users of T3 lines include large corporations, phone companies, and 
ISPs connecting to the Internet backbone. The Internet backbone itself also uses T3 lines. 
 ATM 
ATM (Asynchronous Transfer Mode) is a service that carries voice, data, video, and media at 
very high speeds. Phone networks, the Internet, and other networks with large amounts of 
traffic use ATM.
