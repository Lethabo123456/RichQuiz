 Personal Firewall 
A personal firewall is a security tool that detects and protects a personal computer and its 
data from unauthorized intrusions. Personal firewalls constantly monitor all transmissions to 
and from a computer or mobile device and may inform a user of attempted intrusions. When 
connected to the Internet, your computer or mobile device is vulnerable to attacks from 
hackers who try to access a computer or network illegally. These attacks may destroy your 
data, steal information, damage your computer, or carry out some other malicious action.
