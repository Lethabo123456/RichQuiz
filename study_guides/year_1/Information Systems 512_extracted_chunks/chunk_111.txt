NETWORK COMMUNICATION STANDARDS AND PROTOCOLS 
For the different devices on various types of networks to be able to communicate, the 
network must use similar techniques of moving data through the network from one 
application to another.

guidelines that specify the way computers access the medium to which they are connected, 
the type(s) of medium used, the speeds used on different types of networks, and the type(s).
