GPS (global positioning system) is a navigation system that consists of one or more earth-
based receivers that accept and analyze signals sent by satellites in order to determine the 
receiver’s geographic location. 
Many mobile devices, such as smartphones, have GPS capability built into the device or as an 
add-on feature. Some users carry a handheld GPS receiver; others mount a receiver to an 
object such as an automobile, a boat, an airplane, farm and construction equipment, or a 
computer or mobile device. A GPS receiver is a handheld, mountable, or embedded device 
that contains an antenna, a radio receiver, and a processor. Many include a screen display 
that shows an individual’s location on a map.
