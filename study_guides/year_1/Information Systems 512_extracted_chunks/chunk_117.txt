 Advantages of Bluetooth 
 
Advantages of using Bluetooth technology include the following: 
o If a device has Bluetooth capability, using Bluetooth technology is free. 
o Although Bluetooth devices need to be near each other, they do not have to be in the 
same room, within the same line of sight, or facing each other. 
o Bluetooth devices typically require low processing power and use little energy, so 
using Bluetooth technology will not drain a device’s batteries. 
o Establishing a wireless Bluetooth connection is easy. With most Bluetooth-enabled 
devices, you simply tap or click a Bluetooth shortcut or icon to enable Bluetooth. Once 
enabled, the devices usually immediately recognize a connection. (Before initial use,
