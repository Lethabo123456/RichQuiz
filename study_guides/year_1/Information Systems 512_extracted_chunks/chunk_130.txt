 Twisted  
Physical transmission media use wire, cable, and other tangible materials to send 
communications signals. These wires and cables typically are used underground or within or 
between buildings. Ethernet and token ring LANs often use physical transmission media.

 Coxial 
Coaxial cable, often referred to as coax (pronounced KO-ax), consists of a single copper 
wire surrounded by at least three layers:  
(1) An insulating material,  
(2) A woven or braided metal, and  
(3) A plastic outer coating 
CATV network wiring often uses coaxial cable because it can be cabled over longer 
distances than twisted-pair cable.
