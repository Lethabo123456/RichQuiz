steps guide 
you through the process of using a research database: 
a. Locate and then navigate to the research database that contains the information you are seeking. 
Consult a librarian if you need assistance in determining the exact database you should use. 
b. Determine the location from which you can access the research database. For instance, you may 
need to access some research databases from a library computer. Other databases are accessible 
from anywhere if you can verify your identity as a library patron or a student. Some databases are 
available to the public at no charge or with no other restrictions. 
c. Navigate to the research database you plan to use. 
d. If the research database contains an option to perform an advanced search, tap or click the option 
to perform the advanced search. e. Specify the search criteria. Note that not all research databases 
will request the same search criteria. The following list contains some common criteria:  
1. Keywords 2. Author 3
