For most information 
system projects, hardware, software, and people typically are available to support an 
information system. An organization’s choice for using computing resources and 
software services in-house or on the cloud may impact a system’s technical 
feasibility. 
 
 Economic feasibility, also called cost/benefit feasibility, measures whether the 
lifetime benefits of the proposed information system will be greater than its lifetime 
costs. A systems analyst often consults the advice of a business analyst, who uses 
many financial techniques, such as return on investment (ROI) and payback analysis, 
to perform a cost/benefit analysis.
