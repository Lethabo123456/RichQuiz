 Isolated data:  
It often is difficult to access data that is stored in separate files in different departments. 
Assume, for example, that the student email addresses exist in the Student files and class 
room numbers (locations) are in the Schedule of Classes file. To send an email message 
informing students about a room change, data is needed from both the Student file and the 
Schedule of Classes file. Sharing data from multiple, separate files to generate such a list in a 
file processing system often is a complicated procedure and usually requires an experienced 
programmer.
