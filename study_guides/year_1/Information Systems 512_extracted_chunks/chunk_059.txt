2.6 BACKING UP- THE ULTIMATE SAFE GUARD 
To protect against data loss caused by hardware/software/information theft or system 
failure, users should back up computer and mobile device files regularly. A backup is a 
duplicate of a file, program, or media that can be used if the original is lost, damaged, or 
destroyed. To back up a file means to make a copy of it. In case of system failure or the 
discovery of corrupted files, you restore the files by copying the backed up files to their 
original location on the computer or mobile device. Off-site backups are stored in a location 
separate from the computer or mobile device site 
• 
Categories of backups: 
 Full 
 Differential 
 Incremental 
 Selective 
 Continuous data protection 
 Cloud
