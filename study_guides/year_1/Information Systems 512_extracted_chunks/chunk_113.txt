 Token Ring 
The token ring standard specifies that computers and devices on the network share or pass a 
special signal, called a token, in a unidirectional manner and in a preset order. A token is a 
special series of bits that functions like a ticket. The device with the token can transmit data 
over the network. Only one token exists per network. This ensures that only one computer 
transmits data at a time.
