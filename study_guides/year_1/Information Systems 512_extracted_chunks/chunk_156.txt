 Check digit 
 
A check digit is a number(s) or character(s) that is appended to or inserted in a primary key 
value. A check digit often confirms the accuracy of a primary key value. Bank account, credit 
card, and other identification numbers often include one or more check digits. 
 Other Checks  
DBMSs that include the hyperlink and attachment data types can perform validity checks on data 
entered in those fields. Hyperlink entries (web addresses and email addresses) can be tested to ensure 
that the address follows the correct format. Similarly, an attachment entry can be validated by 
confirming that the file exists.
