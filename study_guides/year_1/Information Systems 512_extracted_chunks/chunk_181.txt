 Data and information gathering techniques 
During system development, members of the project team gather data and information. They 
need accurate and timely data and information for many reasons. They must keep a project 
on schedule, evaluate feasibility, and be sure the system meets requirements. Systems 
analysts and other IT professionals use several techniques to gather data and information. 
They review documentation, observe, survey, interview, conduct joint-application design 
sessions, and research. 
 Review documentation: By reviewing documentation such as organization charts, 
memos, and meeting minutes, systems analysts learn about the history of a project.
