Self-Assessment 
1. Find the equation of the line that has a slope of 6 and passes through the 
point (-3, 5).  
2. Find the slope of the line passing through the points (4,3) and (-5,-2).  
3. Find the equation of the line that has a slope of -2 and a y-intercept of -9. 
4. Find the equation of the line that passes through the points (3,4) and         
(-4,6). 
5. Simon runs on a 5 mile path several times a week.  Since he is just 
beginning this exercise program, he runs part of the way and walks the rest 
of the way.  Simon's running speed is 7 mi/hr and his walking speed is 5 
mi/hr.  
Write an equation to represent Simon's exercise program.  The equation 
should be in slope-intercept form. 
6. Write an equation for a line passing through the points (6, a) and (-2, a) 
7. Write an equation for a line passing through the points (c, 2b) and (c, 3b).
