5.3 Indefinite Integrals 
An indefinite integral has the form ∫𝑓(𝑥)𝑑𝑥.When evaluated, an indefinite 
integral results in a function (or family of functions). An indefinite integral of a 
function 𝑓(𝑥) is also known as the antiderivative of 𝑓. A function 𝐹 is an 
antiderivative of 𝑓 on an interval 𝑙, if 𝐹′(𝑥) =  𝑓(𝑥) for all 𝑥 in 𝑙. This is a 
strong indication that that the processes of integration and differentiation are 
interconnected.
