TOPIC TWO | QUADRATIC EQUATIONS 
 
Introduction 
A quadratic equation is a trinomial of the form ax2 + bx + c = 0. There are 
three main ways of solving quadratic equations that are covered below.  

2.1  Factoring 
If a quadratic equation can be factored, then it can be written as a product of 
two binomials. Since the trinomial is equal to 0, one of the two binomial factors 
must also be equal to zero. By factoring the quadratic equation, we can equate 
each binomial expression to zero and solve each for x.
