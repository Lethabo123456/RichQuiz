If 𝑓(𝑥) is continuous on [−𝑎, 𝑎] and 𝑓 is an even function, then 
∫𝑎−𝑎 𝑓(𝑥) 𝑑𝑥 = 2∫𝑎0 𝑓(𝑥)𝑑𝑥 and if  𝑓(𝑥) is continuous on [−𝑎, 𝑎] and 𝑓 is an

These properties of integrals of symmetric functions are very helpful when 
solving integration problems. Some of the more challenging problems can be 
solved quite simply by using this property.

of the Product Rule for differentiation.
