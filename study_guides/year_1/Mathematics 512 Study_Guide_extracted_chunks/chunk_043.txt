Vertical Tangent Line 
A function is not differentiable at 𝑎 if its graph has a vertical tangent line at 𝑎. 
The tangent line to the curve becomes steeper as 𝑥 approaches 𝑎 until it 
becomes a vertical line. Since the slope of a vertical line is undefined, the 
function is not differentiable in this case. 
 
Corner 
A function is not differentiable at a if its graph has a corner or kink at a. As x 
approaches the corner from the left- and right-hand sides, the function 
approaches two distinct tangent lines. Since the function does not approach 
the same tangent line at the corner from the left- and right-hand sides, the 
function is not differentiable at that point. The graph to the right illustrates a 
corner in a graph.
