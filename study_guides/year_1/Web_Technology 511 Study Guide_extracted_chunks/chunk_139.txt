Values of the vertical-align property 
Aligning Text Horizontally and Vertically 
Text can be aligned horizontally or vertically within an element. To align the text horizontally, 
use the following text-align property  
  
text-align: alignment; 
 
where alignment is left, right, center, or justify (align the text with both the left and the right 
margins). 
To vertically align the text within each line, use the vertical-align property vertical-align: 
alignment;
